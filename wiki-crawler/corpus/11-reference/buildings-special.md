Reference data for Special buildings, extracted mechanically from the wiki (no LLM compression --
see HANDOFF.md §5.5). Prose below is reproduced close to verbatim; tables are exact.

Many baronies in the game have a slot for a Special Buildings. This is in addition to any Building or Duchy Building slot that the county may have. Almost all of these buildings are considered a point-of-interest for traveling characters and will grant lifestyle experience the first time they are visited per character. Special buildings are not disabled in baronies that go over the domain limit but having special buildings in 5 baronies above domain limit will disable all income from them. AI rulers will only construct special buildings after all building slots in the barony have been used.

Special buildings are shown on the map with an icon if the Economy and Buildings map mode is selected.

- If the special building icon is bright golden it means the building is active
- If the special building icon is faint golden it means the building is inactive
- If the special building icon is bright blue it means the building can be constructed
- If the special building icon is faint blue it means the building cannot be constructed

## Holy buildings

Holy buildings can only be constructed in a Faith's holy sites by governments that can also construct holdings and have the Crop Rotation innovation and can only be used if the county owner's faith considers the barony a holy site. All holy buildings require 1000 Gold and 6 years to be completed. Holy buildings can be used as destination for University Visit activities but has additional costs if the county faith is considered Hostile. Visiting a barony with a holy building during travel will grant 200 Learning Lifestyle Experience regardless of faith. If the Legends of the Dead DLC is also installed holy buildings increase the Legitimacy and Piety obtained from Funeral activities.

## Universities

Universities do not require any innovation to be constructed, but the county must have a Development of 30 or higher and the county owner must be a Duke or above with the Exalted Among Men level of fame regardless of which ruler in title hierarchy orders the construction. Visiting a barony with a university during travel will grant 200 Learning Lifestyle Experience. When a university is constructed the character who ordered the construction will gain the The Scholar nickname. They also give the following bonuses depending on installed DLCs:

- With the [Royal Court] Royal Court DLC they also increase the quality of sponsored Book inspirations.
- With the Tours and Tournaments DLC they halve the cost of hosting a Board Game or Recital contest for Grand Tournament activities in the barony.
- With the Wards and Wardens DLC they can be used as destination for University Visit activities and also allow guardians to pay 4500 Gold to send a ward to university.

With the Coronations DLC universities can also be constructed in baronies via the Foster Enlightenment oath.

## Mines

Mines are buildings that have 4 levels each. Constructing or upgrading a mine takes 5 years. Mines can only be constructed in Castle, City, Temple and Temple Citadel holdings by rulers who don't have Tribal, Wanua or Nomadic government. All mines increase the effectiveness of stationed Siege Weapons regiments by +10% per level and the quality of sponsored Artisan, Weapon, Armor, and Metalsmith inspirations. Visiting a barony with a mine during travel will grant 200 Stewardship Lifestyle Experience.

- Constructing the Mining Settlement requires 400 Gold and the Crop Rotation Innovation
- Upgrading to Mine requires 500 Gold and the Manorialism Innovation
- Upgrading to Large Mine requires 600 Gold and the Windmills Innovation
- Upgrading to Mining Complex requires 700 Gold and the Cranes Innovation

Most mines are historical buildings. However Inspection activities have a 0.15% chance to create new mine in the activity barony.

## The Forbidden City

The Forbidden City is a special building that can only be constructed by completing a Strengthen Capital great project and paying for the Forbidden City optional contribution. The building provides the following bonuses:

- +10% Monthly Renown
- +10% Monthly Influence
- +5 Courtly Vassal Opinion
- +1 Diplomacy per Level of Influence
- +10 Court Grandeur
- +30% County Holding Taxes
- +20% County Development Growth
- If the barony is the realm capital all governors receive +4% Governor Efficiency
