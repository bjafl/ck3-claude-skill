Reference data for Economic buildings, extracted mechanically from the wiki (no LLM compression --
see HANDOFF.md §5.5). Prose below is reproduced close to verbatim; tables are exact.

Economic buildings can be constructed in all castle, city and temple holdings as long as the barony has the required terrain. For nearly all of them upgrading the building requires the following innovations:

- Crop Rotation for level 2
- Manorialism for levels 3 and 4
- Guilds for levels 5 and 6
- Cranes for levels 7 and 8

## Manor Houses

Manor Houses can be built in the following holdings:

- They can be built in Farmlands terrain and upgraded up to level 8 as long as the required innovations are researched.
- They can be built in Terraced Hills terrain as well as Floodplains terrain in Eastern Asia and upgraded up to level 4 as long as the required innovations are researched. To upgrade them to level 8 the culture must have the Champa Rice innovation in addition to the usual innovation.
- They can be built if the culture has the Pastorialists tradition and the holding has a level 4 Cattle Pastures and upgraded up to level 4 as long as the required innovations are researched.

Manor Houses take 5 years to build or upgrade. Manor Houses gain increased tax income if culture has the Polders tradition and the barony is coastal.
