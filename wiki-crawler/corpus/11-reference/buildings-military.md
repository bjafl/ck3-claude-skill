Reference data for Military buildings, extracted mechanically from the wiki (no LLM compression --
see HANDOFF.md §5.5). Prose below is reproduced close to verbatim; tables are exact.

Economic buildings can be constructed in all castle, city and temple holdings as long as the barony has the required terrain. For nearly all of them upgrading the building requires the following innovations:

- Barracks for level 2
- Burhs for levels 3 and 4
- Castle Baileys for levels 5 and 6
- Royal Armory for levels 7 and 8

If the Tours and Tournaments DLC is installed many of these buildings also reduce the cost of hosting a Grand Tournament in the barony.

## Wind Furnaces

Wind Furnaces can only be built by cultures with the Wootz Steel innovation. They may only be constructed in coastal baronies in India or in any barony within the Duchy of Warangal as long as the top liege's realm includes the County of Vemulavada. Losing the county will not disable the Wind Furnaces in Warangal but will prevent them from being upgraded. They hake 3 years to build or upgrade.
