Castle buildings can only be constructed in Castle holdings.

## Fortification buildings

Fortification buildings increase the defenses of the holding they're built in. Every Castle holding can build one of four fortification buildings depending on the barony's terrain. Like the holding itself, all fortification buildings have their upgrades unlocked through the following innovations:

- Earthworks for level 1 and 2
- Battlements for level 3 and 4
- Hoardings for level 5 and 6
- Machicolations for level 7 and 8
- If the county has a culture with the Stalwart Defenders tradition both the innovation and the holding level requirements are reduced by one level
