Reference data for Duchy buildings, extracted mechanically from the wiki (no LLM compression --
see HANDOFF.md §5.5). Prose below is reproduced close to verbatim; tables are exact.

Duchy buildings can only be constructed by a ruler who holds the associated duchy title and does not have Tribal, Wanua or Nomadic government. Duchy buildings will be disabled if their county holder does not hold the associated duchy buildings but will function regardless of government. Each duchy can only support one such building and if the duchy capital is relocated the building is removed. All duchy buildings take 5 years to construct or upgrade and most have 3 levels.

- Level 1 buildings cost 600 Gold
- Level 2 buildings cost 900 Gold
- Level 3 buildings cost 1200 Gold

## Great Barracks

The Great Barracks duchy building can only be constructed through the Construct Great Barracks great project in the de jure capital of a Military Governor and if the barony already had a duchy building it will be replaced. It cannot be upgraded or destroyed and provides the following bonuses:

- +1 Size of Men-at-Arms Regiments
- +1 Max Title Men-at-Arms Regiments
- +5% Governor Efficiency
- +5% Monthly Influence
- -10% Men-at-Arms Recruitment Cost
- -12% Men-at-Arms Maintenance

The duchy where the building is located receives the following bonuses:

- +6 Fort Level
- +15 Defender Advantage
- +0.2 Monthly Control
- +150% Garrison Size
- +100% Supply Limit
- +50% Hostile Raid Time
- -30 Danger
