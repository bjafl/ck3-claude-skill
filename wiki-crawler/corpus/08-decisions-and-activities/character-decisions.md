Character decisions are available to all characters regardless of status. These range from major life-altering choices to minor lifestyle actions. Most are unavailable if the character has the Incapable trait.

## Major Decisions

Major decisions permanently mark a character's story and notify relevant rulers.

## Petition the Liege

Adult vassals in a Royal Court (or equivalent) unlock this decision every 5 years. Ceremonial monarchs cannot petition. Celestial/Meritocratic/Meritocratic Khanate government characters need Merit Rank 7+.

Cost: 50-250 Prestige or Influence (scales by rank/government). +20 Stress (Arrogant/Shy).

Acceptance depends on liege's Opinion, relations, vassal Influence/Merit, powerful family status, and era. The Hegemon of China is less likely to accept. Liege may demand Gold, Weak Hook, renouncing hooks, or faction departure. Both dynasties gain ±50 Renown based on outcome.

| Petition | Effect | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Resettle Subjects | Liege capital −2 Development, yours +2. Liege capital −10% Growth, −15 Opinion (5 years). | Liege capital < 66% your development. |
| Dismantle Faction | Dismantle/end war with faction. | At war with Claimant/Liberty faction. |
| Realm Peace | Fellow vassal wars end in white peace. | At war with fellow vassal. |
| Sponsor Priests | Different faith counties: +10% Conversion, −15 Opinion (5 years). | Different faith county, same faith as liege. |
| Send Bailiffs | Counties < 66 Control: +10 Control, +0.3 Monthly Control, −5 Danger, −10 Opinion (5 years). | County < 66 Control. |
| Royal Visit | Counties < −25 Opinion: +25 Opinion (5 years). | County < −25 Opinion. |
| Fortify Borders | Border counties: +2 Fort, +20% Garrison, −10 Danger (5 years). | County borders realm. |
| Urbanize County | Liege constructs City holding (random county, favor non-City/high development counties). | City Planning innovation, liege 400+ Gold. |
| Underwrite Temple | Liege constructs Temple holding (random county, favor non-Temple/high development). | City Planning innovation, liege 400+ Gold, same faith. |
| Military Assistance | Liege joins or lends levies (500-all, depends on enemy). Liege boldness/both sides' opinion determine join chance. | External war, −25%+ war score, liege at peace. [No] liege joins wars vs. allies, in debt. |
| Settle Debt | Liege pays all debt. Liege more likely if gold surplus. | 100+ Gold debt, liege can afford. |
| Council Appointment | Appointed best-match position. [No] removal (5 years). Liege more likely if your skill higher than current. | Can join liege council, not already on it. |
| Grant Claim | Gain liege's highest title with your claim (not primary/capital). | Claim on liege title (not primary/capital). |
| Transfer Claim | Gain fellow vassal's title with your claim. | Liege allows revocation, claim on vassal title. |
| Gold | Gain scaling gold. Liege more likely if surplus. | — |
| Family Council Appointment | House member with most skills appointed. [No] removal (5 years). | House head, member eligible for council. |
| Court Position | Grants court position. | [No] hold position. |
| Family Court Position | House member gains court position. | House head, member eligible. |
| Family Governorship Appointment | House member gains Duchy. | House head, adult member unlanded in realm, liege has Duchy with de jure counties. [No] 2+ vassals same house. |
