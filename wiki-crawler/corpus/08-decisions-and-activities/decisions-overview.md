Decisions are character actions taken under specific circumstances. Some can only be used once per character or per game, while others are repeatable. Most decisions become unavailable if the character has the Incapable trait and require specific conditions to be visible.

Decisions are split into three groups based on character status: Character decisions (available to all), Ruler decisions (for landed rulers only), and [Adventurer decisions](./adventurer-decisions.md) (for landless adventurers). Within these categories, decisions are typically marked as either major (permanently affecting the world and notifying other rulers) or minor (with cooldowns, localized effects).

## Classification

Character decisions function independently of a ruler's title or holdings, applying to any adult character meeting the specific requirements. These range from lifestyle choices like meditation or coping mechanisms to significant personal developments like founding a cadet branch or converting to a faith.

Ruler decisions require characters to own landed titles. Major ruler decisions create lasting changes like founding new titles, changing government types, or establishing holy orders. Minor ruler decisions involve realm management, court interactions, and administrative functions with cooldowns preventing spam.

Adventurer decisions are exclusive to [landless adventurers](../02-realm-and-succession/government-overview.md) operating from camps. These include establishing holdings, becoming conquerors, undertaking faith missions, and specialized Hasan Sabbah story events.

## Mechanics and Availability

Most decisions are hidden unless the character meets specific requirements. Decision costs vary by type: prestige, gold, piety, stress reduction, or miscellaneous resources. Many decisions require the character to be at peace, adult, not imprisoned, and not engaged in other activities. Some decisions are tied to specific government types, faiths, cultures, or special modifiers.

Once taken, decisions typically have a cooldown (ranging from 2-50 years, lifetime restrictions, or no reuse) and may trigger events or award traits, modifiers, or titles. The notification system informs relevant rulers when major decisions are taken in their sphere of influence.

## Related Systems

Decisions interact with [Activities](../01-character-mechanics/activities.md) (cannot be taken while engaged), [Schemes](../04-intrigue-and-diplomacy/schemes.md) (some decisions grant scheme advantages), [Lifestyle perks](../01-character-mechanics/lifestyle.md) (unlock special decisions), and various government types that unlock unique decision paths. Many decisions check devotion levels, legitimacy, fame, and other character resources to determine availability.
