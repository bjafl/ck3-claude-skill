Beyond bookmarked scenarios, hundreds of interesting historical characters offer unique playstyles and dynasty-building opportunities across multiple start dates. These figures represent both famous historical names and lesser-known nobles positioned for greatness—or obscurity.

## 867 start date

### Francia

#### East Francia

**Welf Welfen, Count of Ravensburg** — Member of Swabian branch of House Welf, ancestor of Welf IV of Bavaria, founder of Second House Welf. Original House Welf dies with Kunigunde of Altdorf in 1054; Welf IV begins new era. Minor 867 position belies extraordinary destiny—descendants become powerful dukes/kings; one rises Holy Roman Emperor. Can you preserve elder Welf male line and elevate it to greatness?

**Wilhelm II Wilhelmiden, Count of Wien** — Head Wilhelmiden dynasty, rulers March Pannonia—crucial military frontier defending Frankish borders vs Avars/Slavs. Strategic position sparks Aribonen family jealousy. Historical death (Wilhelm and brother Engelshalk) in 871 during Svatopluk I war precedes controversial succession, Wilhelminer War, and eventual House Babenberg rise. By 867, fate unsealed—ally with brother, unite Austria/Steyermark duchies, consolidate power, confront rivals. Seize their lands, restore frontier unity, crown yourself King Bavaria?

**Aribo Aribonen, Count of Passau** — Dawn of rising Aribonen dynasty. Historically granted March Pannonia 871 after Wilhelmiden deaths. Sparked Wilhelminer War as dead margraves' sons reclaimed inheritance. Secured position via Svatopluk I alliance. Retained region until 909 death; lands then fell to Babenberg. Aribonen remained influential Bavarian family. 867: legacy unwritten. Claim Austria, defeat rivals, secure eastern frontier, or fade from history?

**Ernst II Ernste, Duke of Nordgau** — Prominent Bavarian 9th-century noble ruling Nordgau (Bohemian March). Traditions associate him early Luitpolding ancestry (lineage from which Babenberg emerged). In-game: no such ties—forge brand-new legacy. Command critical frontier between East Francia and pagan Bohemia/Sorbia. Assert Bavarian dominance vs Slavic neighbors, establish Bohemian kingdom under German elite rule. Or consolidate East Frankian power, rise as royal house of dukes/kings rivaling Carolingians. Ernste dynasty fate rests shoulders.

**Thankulf II von Thüringen, Duke of Thüringia** — Prominent Frankish 9th-century nobleman, likely old Thüringian kings' descendant. Sorbian March lands form crucial East Frankian military frontier, bordering Slavic tribes. Deep Slavic customs/diplomacy understanding vital managing peaceful/hostile relations. Campaigns suppressed Sorbian uprisings, asserted Frankish authority. Historical 873 death preceded Sorbian rebellion, reflecting stabilizing importance. Successor Radulf II subdued revolt. 867: Thankulf twilight but future uncertain. Strategic crossroads—defend East Francia vs Slavic incursions, serve loyal Carolingian vassal, or pursue bolder vision: unite Sorbian tribes, forge Frankish-Slavic Thüringia kingdom, restore ancient ancestor legacy. Will House von Thüringen shape into mightiest European dynasty?

**Sieghard III Sieghardinger, Count of Ansbach** — Rules under Karling Duke Swabia, Charles the Fat (future Carolingian Emperor). Sieghardinger dynasty minor 867 but rises notable Holy Roman Empire house, particularly Bavaria/Austria. One branch founds Hohenstaufen dynasty—emperors like Frederick Barbarossa. Elevate Sieghardinger name to greater heights, seize Swabian control from powerful liege, consolidate southern Germany power, forge glory legacy. Found Hohenstaufen yourself, lead Germany to golden age?

**Hunfrid Hunfriding, Count of Zürich** — Leads Hunfriding dynasty, Alemannic noble house significant Carolingian/Holy Roman Empire politics. Historically rose become Swabian Dukes, held lands southern France to northern Italy. Extended branches considered Wettin House progenitors (surviving today). 867: navigating Carolingian decline turbulence. Diplomacy, ambition, strategic foresight expand family influence across Christian Europe. Seize Swabian duchy as dynastic stronghold, defy history preserving/empowering Hunfriding name, found own dynastic empire. Secure lasting house legacy or fade into obscurity, overshadowed by cadet branches?

**Udalrich Udalrichinger, Count of Breisgau** — Head Udalrichinger dynasty, influential Swabian noble family descended Agilolfing Swabia (old Alemannia rulers). Once Swabia duchy holders, family power reduced to Breisgau lands. Despite setback, ancient noble blood runs through veins. Ambition, cunning, diplomacy reclaim Swabia for house, restore past glory. Unite remaining Swabian lands one banner, revive Alemannic ancestor legacy. Forge new independent Kingdom Alemannia free Frankish dominance, usher golden age Swabian people. Restore Udalrichinger long-lost glory, rekindle ancient Alemannia spirit, or lost annals history?

### England

**Ælla Oswulfson of Northumbria, Petty King (Duke) of Northumbria** — Invited Great Heathen Army wrath not only self but other English kingdoms too. Snake pit won't save you from blood eagle. Call Vikings' bluff, throw them back across sea, or let ravening pagans have vengeance?

**Alfred Æthelwulfson of Wessex, Earl (Count) of Dorset** — As Vikings push south, Alfred readies troops. Coming years fight numerous battles vs Norsemen, end invasion. Inherit brother's throne, become one of England's greatest kings. Fulfill Alfred's great destiny or be crushed by Great Heathen Army?

**Burghred Beornredson of Mercia, Petty King (Duke) of Mercia** — Rules last truly independent Mercia King, once-mighty Anglo-Saxon kingdom caught between growing Wessex power and looming Viking invasion. Brother-in-law King Alfred Wessex. Once held central England sway, maintained royal alliance influence. Late 9th century: Great Heathen Army landed Northumbria, Viking horde steadily moving south. Historically driven 874 Viking pressure, died Rome exile. 867: Mercia fate undecided. Change realm fate, rally forces, forge powerful House Wessex alliance, stand firm vs Norsemen. Cunning diplomacy negotiate Danelaw boundaries yourself, secure English throne before Wessex. Restore ancient Mercia house power, become first united England king, or legacy lost to Viking warlords/rising Wessex?

**Eadmund Æthelweardson of East Anglia, Petty King (Duke) of East Anglia** — Rules one of last Anglo-Saxon power bastions in eastern England. At war Great Heathen Army. Later remembered as Saint Edmund, martyred 869 refusing renounce Christian faith while Norse hands. Brutal death made him resistance/piety symbol, widespread English veneration. 867: Norse East Anglia invasion ongoing. Fellow Anglo-Saxon ruler support, particularly Wessex/Mercia petty kings, may survive coming storm, preserve rule. Endure: become pious king and powerful warrior saint, drive Norse out England, reclaim York Christendom name. Faith, resilience, martial prowess—East Anglia strong vs Norse onslaught. Be worthy Saint Edmund legacy, bring him glory?

**Dumnarth Cerneu, Petty King (Duke) of Cornwall** — Last recorded Cornwall king, remnant independent Brythonic English rule. Small duchy next Wessex petty kingdom. Outmaneuver Anglo-Saxon neighbors restore ancient Dumnonia kingdom borders under Cornwall banner, or lands become Alfred the Great holdings?

### Croatia

**Braslav Vladinić Senulatović, Ban (Duke) of Slavonia** — Rules crucial Frankish Empire/Slavic world frontier. Historically became key Arnulf Carinthia (future Frank Emperor) ally, fought alongside him vs Great Moravia, tasked Pannonia defense vs rising Magyars. Region fell Hungarian invaders; Braslav's fate unknown, name disappeared records. 867: change history. Swear East Francia loyalty, reclaim Croatia/Balaton lands, turn Slavonia strong Frankish Empire bulwark vs Magyars. Or stay independent, work Franks mighty ally, defend Empire preserving Slavic autonomy. Stop Magyar advance, forge lasting Slavic realm Frankish frontier, or lands fall invading heathens, name fade obscurity?

**Domagoj Dobimirić Domagojević, Ban (Duke) of Croatia** — Rose power 864 seizing throne from Trpimirović dynasty. Reign marked fierce Venice conflict, sparked Croatian piracy Adriatic Sea, growing Venetian Republic independence. Later supported King Louis II Italy vs Arabs Bari. Late years: rising Byzantine influence weakened Frankish control Croatia, sparked rebellion vs Frankish overlordship backed Emperor Basil I. 876 death: Trpimirović dynasty reclaimed throne, ruled 100+ years, brief Domagojević restoration 879-892. 867: reshape dynasty fate, defend throne vs Frankish/Venetian/Arab/Byzantine pressure, outmaneuver Trpimirović rivals, cement family dominance Croatia. Ambition/skill: Domagojević enduring royal house, united powerful Croatian kingdom. Defy history, bring Domagojević glory?

**Zdeslav Trpimirić Tripimirović, Count of Požega** — Exiled Trpimir I son after father's 864 death. Zdeslav and brothers exiled Croatia by usurper Domagoj. Historically returned Byzantine support, overthrew Domagoj heirs, briefly ruled before Branimir (Domagoj relative) killing. Though short rule ended, Domagojević return, Trpimirović line later reclaimed throne under brother Muncimir, ruled Croatia until 11th-century end. 867: Zdeslav remains exile but political tides shifting. Forge strong alliances return Croatia, overthrow Domagoj usurper. Then align Byzantines security/alliances or cast influence off seizing Byzantine Dalmatia, establish independent Croatian kingdom. Restore Trpimirović legacy, ensure survival, or line fade away die like historically?

### Great Moravia

**Rostislav Mojmírid, King of Great Moravia** — Begins reign securing partial Frankish independence. Few years before 867: invited famous Saints Cyril/Methodius Moravia, introduced Slavonic liturgy/Glagolitic script, strengthened cultural identity, loosened Frankish ecclesiastical control. Despite resilience/vision, rule ended betrayal—ambitious nephew/successor Svatopluk I "the Great" captured 870, handed Franks. Svatopluk ruled 20+ years, significantly expanded Great Moravia but never fully escaped Frankish overlordship, remained tributary. Post-death: kingdom quickly succumbed internal divisions, Frank/Hungarian invasions. Rewrite history? Secure Svatopluk's loyalty, fortify Great Moravia, forge powerful enduring kingdom rivaling Frankish kings, resisting fierce eastern pagans—or fade history annals?

**Velemír Divinsky, Count of Orava** — Rules strategic frontier eastern Great Moravia, serves future king Svatopluk I, Duke Nitra/future Great Moravia king. Carpathian highlands Orava borders pagan/Christian realms, countless glory/conquest opportunities. East: White Croats Ungvár struggle survival, ripe subjugation. North: fragmented pagan Poland/Russian tribes vulnerable/disunited, perfect expansion targets under Christ/Moravia banner. Or more ambitious: use Moravian court influence, side Mojmírid rivals like Eastern Franks, claim Great Moravia crown yourself. Ambitious/diligence: reform/revitalize weakening kingdom, establish great power lasting ages. Conqueror pagan or great Moravian king—House Divinsky destiny commanded, bold enough glory?

### Hungary

**Kocel, High Chieftain (Duke) of Balaton** — Second Pribina son. Father attacked/expelled homeland by Mojmir I, Moravia duke, early 800s. Kocel built large fortress seat power Lake Balaton region, surrounded forests/swamps river Zala, ruled Sloviens area. Survive Magyar Carpathian conquest, eventually retake father's Great Moravia lands?

**Gostomil Bijelahrvatskić, High Chieftain (Duke) of Ungvár** — Rules mountainous White Croat homeland, elusive ancient Slavic Carpathian people. Little known but believed common southern Croat ancestry, remained north instead migrating Balkans. Historically vanished 10th century, gradually absorbed rising Slavic/Magyar polities. 867: White Croats small tribal federation, surrounded stronger neighbors: Bulgarians south, Moravians west, pagan Slavs Russia north. Meanwhile, Magyar Pannonia migration underway, threatening region with rising Hungary kingdom. Perilous independence maintenance formidable challenge. Survival options: seek vassalage King Rostislav Great Moravia (protection/realm foothold) or independent work Franks mighty ally. From security position expand influence Nitra, across Pannonia reclaim Transcarpathia. Ambition/cunning: break Moravian control, establish White Croat independent kingdom. Transform precarious frontier thriving White Croat culture bastion? Build kingdom withstand history challenges or people conquered/forgotten, assimilated neighbors? White Croat fate shoulders—preserve ancient legacy?

### Italy

**Louis II 'the Younger' Karling, King of Italy** — Eldest Lothaire (himself eldest Kaiser Louis Pious son). Rightful entire Frankish Empire successor. However, imperial power diminished when crowned Holy Roman Emperor—title offered no real authority. Reclaimed Provence kingdom but uncles/brother unburdened Imperial Crown allegiance continue acting freely. Only Italy King: crowned heads Europe unlikely release titles without fight. Karling dynasty member: start heir at least one kingdom, highly placed succession several others. Few discreet assassinations/well-timed claim wars eventually make Western Europe ruler chunk, from which create either Francia Empire or Holy Roman Empire route. Focus ambitious southward territory outside family control, crown yourself Italia Emperor. Historically saddled quarrelsome neighbors/unstable realm, Louis dies before son birth. Regain all lost empire territories, reestablish Imperial Crown real power?

**Lambert Guideschi, Duke of Spoleto** — Since Roman Empire fall, Italy ruled succession northern barbarians. Your ancestors may among them, but you thoroughly Italian. One of weak king's most powerful vassals; while he calls powerful family you rise against, you fall back fellow vassals equally hostile foreign king, including brother-in-law Tuscany. Or wish avoid bloodshed fair Italy, king has two daughters almost wed-ready. Right factions right time: king topples easily, southern Lombards make easy expansion pickings. Thereafter expand leisure but ever mindful Byzantines. Maybe demonstrate Italian Renaissance needs no schismatic Greek input?

**Boson Bosonid, Count of Provence** — Lesser-known scion powerful Bosonid dynasty—Kingdom Burgundy founders. Relative Boson Bouvinid (Bivinids) became first king; son Louis Burgundy second monarch claimed Charlemagne imperial title himself. Lesser Boso Elder son but nonetheless well positioned. Strong Provence claims/influence. Consolidate power forming Provence duchy, subdue rival vassals, break weakening Karling grip. Crown yourself first free Burgundy monarch, lead Bosonids greatness one of Europe's most prominent dynasties. Ambition/strength: maybe restore Charlemagne Empire—this time Bosonid emperor banner.

**Konrad Welfen, Duke of Upper Burgundy** — Bavarian branch Welf dynasty member. Significant Carolingian politics role, earned many Burgundy titles. Historically descendants rose become Upper Burgundy kings, eventually united realm Lower Burgundy into powerful lasting kingdom. Konrad lineage one nobility, honour, diplomacy. Lead Welf dynasty rule Burgundy, extend France/Germany influence. Burgundy rule alone unsatisfies ambitions, use growing power challenge Karling order itself, maybe crown yourself Holy Roman Emperor. Live up Welf legacy, carve name among Europe's great dynasties?

**Theophylact Tusculani, Count of Orvieto** — Tusculani dynasty founder. Powerful Roman noble family dominating papal/Italian politics 100+ years. Rose prominence especially Tusculan Papacy (1012-1048) producing multiple Popes, holding vast Rome/Latium/Tuscany secular power. Late 9th century: Theophylact rising figure central Italy, vestararius (papal chamberlain)/magister militum (soldiers master), effectively controlling Rome before consolidating Latium/Tuscany power. Though not yet peak family influence, lays dynasty foundations deeply intertwined Church/Italian peninsula nobility. Ideally placed expand Tusculani Papacy influence, position heirs Rome's spiritual/secular circles, shape Italy's future. Pursue Holy See dominance or claim Italian crown, forge powerful kingdom grounded faith/ambition? Central Italy's future may rest hands.

## Historical characters

Various other start dates feature numerous historical characters positioned at interesting junctures—some facing extinction, others on the cusp of greatness. Consult bookmarks and character finders for comprehensive lists tailored to your preferred challenge level and playstyle.

### General advice for interesting character runs

- **Smaller realms** offer room for growth and unique challenges compared to major powers.
- **Positioned characters** often face interesting succession problems or vassalage conflicts that require careful diplomacy.
- **Cultural minorities** in foreign courts provide opportunities for cultural/religious spread or independent realm establishment.
- **Younger characters** allow longer reign spans for legacy building; elderly characters face imminent succession challenges.
- **Low-title characters** with good relationships/claims provide underdog playstyles.
