Hired forces are predefined armies temporarily controlled via Gold payment. These armies cannot raid. Two types exist: Mercenaries and Holy Orders.

## Mercenaries

Mercenaries can be hired for 3-year contracts. Gold cost is paid upon hiring with no additional fees; after 3 years they leave unless the contract is extended (extension costs the same as initial hiring). Rulers may go into debt up to 2 years to hire mercenaries.

Each culture generates 1-3 mercenary companies based on county count of that culture; each additional company is larger and more expensive. Mercenaries pick a county of their culture as headquarters and are available to anyone with capital within diplomatic range. Characters can hire same-culture mercenaries from further away. Mercenary armies contain one or more specific men-at-arms regiments and knights.

Mercenary regiments reinforce 3× faster than regular regiments.

Mercenary composition by company number:

| Company | Levies | Regiments | Knights | Cultural Regiment Chance |
|---------|--------|-----------|---------|-------------------------|
| 1st | 400 | 1 | 2 | 50% |
| 2nd | 800 | 2 | 3 | 66% |
| 3rd | 1200 | 3 | 4 | 80% |

### Hiring Cost Reductions

- -30% Soldiers of Lesser Fortune perk (Overseer tree)
- -15% Communal court type
- -15% "Swords for Hire" tradition (same culture only)
- -30% (estimated) Scoundrel accolade stat
- -25% Condottieri regional innovation (same culture only)
- -50% Malleable subjects tradition (same culture only)
- Many additional modifiers

## Holy Orders

Holy orders are independent military organizations defending and expanding their faith's influence. Each holds a Duke-rank title.

Rulers can create holy orders via the Found Holy Order decision (requires selecting an owned castle/city holding that isn't a county capital as headquarters). The founding ruler becomes Patron; the grandmaster belongs to the order's faith and culture.

Holdings leased to holy orders can be revoked by rulers in the de jure hierarchy containing the holding; revoking the last holding destroys the order. Revocation penalties:

- -40 Opinion with grandmaster
- -20 Opinion with faith's Head of Faith
- Piety penalties/bonuses (varies by faith relationship):
  - -500 Piety (Righteous or same faith)
  - -100 Piety (Astray)
  - +250 Piety (Hostile or Evil; order's faith gains +10 Fervor)
- -15 Popular Opinion and -0.15 Monthly Control for 10 years (if county shares order's faith)

### Hiring

Same-faith rulers can hire holy orders if at war with Hostile or Evil faith, regardless of casus belli. Only one holy order can be hired at a time. Hiring costs Piety (scales on max levies/regiments) unless the ruler is the Patron. Holy orders remain hired as long as employer is at war with another faith.

Holy orders have 500 Levies, 2 Knights, 2 regiments base. Each holding grants +1 regiment and +200 Levies; every 2 holdings grant +1 Knight. Their men-at-arms don't gain building bonuses from their holdings but rather from employer's buildings.

In 867 and 1066, no holy orders exist. In 1178, the Catholic faith has two: Knights Templar and Knights Hospitaller (both in Jerusalem).

### Patronage

A King or above controlling the holy order's barony becomes Patron (even if a subject). If top liege is Duke or below, the order has no patron. Patrons hire holy orders free (same faith required) and receive events allowing holding grants.

If another character hires the holy order, the Patron can repay the hiring Piety cost to hire it themselves (unavailable in multiplayer if hired by human player).

### Members

All holy order members gain the Order Member trait. Same-faith rulers can recruit courtiers (Martial education trait, not Excommunicated, allowed by gender laws, not diarch). With multiple orders, the ruler chooses. Courtiers must accept (Boldness/Zeal affect chances; Ambitious or Pregnant always refuse).

Recruiting costs:
- Courtier: 100 Piety
- Close family member: 200 Piety
- Primary heir: 350 Piety + 50 Legitimacy
