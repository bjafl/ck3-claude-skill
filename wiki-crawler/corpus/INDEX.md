## Not here — check these instead

- **Individual county/duchy/character data** (e.g. "what buildings does county X have") — out of
  scope for this skill. The wiki's own `List of counties`/`List of duchies` pages are far too
  granular (hundreds of KB) to be useful reference material here; point the user to the wiki
  directly for that level of detail.
- **Mod content** (CK3AGOT, RICE, Rajas of Asia, The Fallen Eagle, etc.) — this skill is vanilla
  CK3 only.
- **Full patch-by-patch changelogs** — `00-meta/patch-history.md` is a high-level summary, not a
  changelog; point to the wiki's `Category:Patches` for exact per-patch notes.
