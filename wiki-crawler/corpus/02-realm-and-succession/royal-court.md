A royal court is a ruler's seat of majesty, available to kings and emperors. Courts represent a physical manifestation of authority and provide gameplay mechanics for enhancing prestige, legitimacy, and courtier satisfaction. The visual appearance depends on your realm capital's culture and terrain.

## Availability and Status

Royal courts are available to playable characters of King rank or higher. Some government types require independence: Administrative, Celestial, Meritocratic, Meritocratic Khanate, and Ritsuryō rulers must own no liege. If your highest title is destroyed or taken by another court holder, yours becomes dormant. If taken by a lower-ranked ruler, it transfers to them.

Your court displays your spouse, councilors, and important courtiers. You can also showcase Book, Furniture, Throne, Pedestal Item, and Wall Ornament artifacts. Default appearance is based on your capital's culture but can be set manually in the Court Visuals tab (requires Royal Court DLC).

## Hold Court

Adult rulers not traveling or commanding armies may Hold Court every 5 years. This costs 100 Prestige for Kings or 125 for Emperors, triggers 3 random petition events, and grants 6 Court Grandeur plus 50 Legitimacy.

Random events occur in your court every 24-32 months and are indicated by icons above involved characters.

Each ruler chooses between two court types based on their culture's ethos. Court types provide bonuses at specific grandeur levels and determine which trait courtiers gain after 5 years at grandeur level 5. Changing types costs 500 Prestige and affects petition event likelihood.

### Tribal Court

Tribal and Wanua rulers can only use the Tribal court type:

- Level 1: +5% Knight Effectiveness
- Level 3: +20% Army Loot Capacity, +20% Raid Speed
- Level 5: +25% Dread Gain
- Level 7: +5 Vassal Opinion
- Level 10: −25% Men-at-Arms Maintenance

Courtiers and guests gain +1 Prowess once yearly (max 3 times), and courtiers gain the Warlike court trait.

### Nomadic Court

Nomadic rulers can only use the Nomadic court type:

- Level 1: +15% Herd Capacity
- Level 2: +20% Army Loot Capacity, +20% Raid Speed
- Level 3: +25% Hunter Venator Trait Experience Gain
- Level 4: +5% Herd Gain
- Level 6: +10 Natural Dread
- Level 8: +5 Direct Vassal Opinion
- Level 10: +2% Herd Conversion into Horde Riders

Courtiers and guests gain +1 Prowess and +5% Hunter Venator experience yearly (max 3 times), and gain the Warlike court trait.

## Court Language

Court language determines the language spoken at your court. If you know your court's language, you gain Court Grandeur per county speaking it (+25% bonus if your culture also uses it). Multiple royal courts using the same language grant each other additional bonuses, reduced by −50% for different religions or −25% for different government types.

Change court language every 5 years for 750 Prestige. The new language must be your culture's language or another nearby ruler's court language.

## Court Amenities

Court Amenities are monthly expenses that increase grandeur and provide bonuses. Costs scale with realm size, income, and culture era. Adjust amenities once per year. Tribal and nomadic courts cannot access the last 3 levels.

### Sumptuary Debate

With Fashion amenities at level 1-2 and non-Tribal/Nomadic court, unlock Accuse of Violating Sumptuary Law on adult direct vassals, courtiers, or guests. Cannot target friends or Naked Priests characters. The interaction triggers a combined Diplomacy, Stewardship, and Attraction challenge. Winner gains +350 Prestige and −30 Opinion with loser (90%) or Weak/Strong Hook with imprisonment reason (10%). You gain +2 Intrigue for 2 years but cannot use the interaction again during that time.

## Court Grandeur

Court Grandeur measures your court's fame. Baseline grandeur comes from dynasty legacies, amenities, and artifacts. It shifts toward baseline at 0.2 per month (doubled if your dynasty unlocked the second Glory legacy). Some events and holding court affect it. A small amount is lost on succession. Bonuses persist 6 months if grandeur drops below level requirements.

Newly created courts start with 25 Court Grandeur.
