Feudal is the default European/Indian government. Feudal rulers can create new holdings, form cadet branches, use Crown Authority laws, and set Title succession laws per-title.

## Co-Monarch / Entrenched Regency

Both share the same power-scale mechanics but differ in how they start:

- **Co-Monarchy**: a Feudal, Sōryō, Tribal, or Wanua King/Emperor appoints a family member as co-monarch (diarch); the diarch also becomes designated heir automatically. Ends only by imprisoning and executing the co-monarch.
- **Entrenched Regency**: automatic for an underage or Incapable ruler, or one who takes Enter Seclusion; a level-4 Regency can also become Entrenched. On coming of age, the scale resets to 0 and the ruler may attempt to end it.

| Level | Scale | Diarch powers unlocked | Liege mundane action cost | Can end Entrenched Regency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-19 | Siphon Treasury; Subsidize Liege Authority (both borrowed powers on liege) | 0 | Yes |
| 2 | 20-39 | Free extra Vassal Contract change; Shift Privileges (on liege's vassals) | 0 | Requires diarch acceptance |
| 3 | 40-59 | Legal Meddling; Diarch Revoke Title (on liege's vassals, needs a claim + enough Crown Authority) | 75+ | Requires diarch acceptance |
| 4 | 60-79 | Diarch Imprison; Diarch Retract Vassal (needs enough Crown Authority) | 150+ | Requires diarch acceptance |
| 5 | 80-94 | Elevate Self to Co-Emperor (if Duumvirate conditions met) | 250+ | No |
| 6 | 95-100 | Attempt to Overthrow Liege decision; Invite Conspirators to Coup | 350+ | No |

## Crown Authority

Available to Feudal and Clan governments. Level increases every 20 years after researching the required innovation; base cost 100 Prestige.

| | Autonomous Vassals (0) | Limited (1) | High (2) | Absolute (3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Liege powers | None | Revoke titles; retract vassals; can have tributaries | Vassal titles can't be inherited outside realm; can change gender law; refusing revocation/retraction/imprisonment is a crime; +10% vassal taxes/levy contribution | Can designate heir; can give vassal directives; +25% vassal taxes/levy contribution |
| Succession laws | Confederate Partition, House Succession | Partition, High Partition | Primogeniture, Ultimogeniture, House Seniority | — |
| Vassal wars | Allowed | Allowed | Requires hook on liege (against fellow vassals) | Requires hook on liege |
| Required innovation | None | Plenary Assemblies | Royal Prerogative | Royal Prerogative |

Vassal-opinion swing by personality trait gets progressively worse at higher Crown Authority (e.g. Belligerent Vassal goes from -10 at level 0 to +20 at level 3; Glory Hound/Parochial go from +10 to -40; Minor Landholder from +20 to -40).

## Feudal contracts

Liege–vassal obligations are set in the feudal contract and changed one step at a time. Lowering obligations is free; raising them adds +20 tyranny opinion unless the liege lowers the other obligation, grants a right, or spends a hook. See [Subjects](subjects.md) § Vassal contracts.

## Converting to Feudal

- **Mandala**: converts to Feudal on adopting a Monotheist-doctrine faith, or on being vassalized by a liege with a different government.
- **Tribal**: vassals and independent rulers can adopt Feudal via decision.
- **Missing DLC**: without the listed DLC, these governments fall back to Feudal — Administrative (Roads to Power), Celestial/Mandala/Meritocratic/Meritocratic Khanate/Ritsuryō/Sōryō (All Under Heaven).
