Power sharing delegates significant authority to a secondary ruler called a diarch who aids governance but may pursue personal interests. Diarchies prevent the ruler from changing succession laws. Rulers cannot imprison, revoke titles, retract vassals, or request conversion of their diarch, nor can they request their excommunication. If the diarch is a vassal, higher de jure titles containing their primary title can only go to them.

Regencies cannot occur in realms with existing diarchs, but validity for regency prevents diarchy termination. Three forms of power sharing exist: standard Diarchy, Entrenched Regency, and Duumvirate/Vizierate/Grand Secretariat (varying by government type).

## Mandates

Diarchs pursue assigned mandates while the liege is unavailable. Three options exist:

| Mandate | Benefits | Aptitude |
|---|---|---|
| Fill Coffers | Extra gold Free domain development Feudal contracts adjusted in liege's favor | +1 per Stewardship +0.5 per Intrigue +0.5 per Learning |
| Swell Armies | Positive men-at-arms modifiers Positive levy modifiers Skilled knight courtiers | +1 per Martial +0.5 per Prowess +0.5 per Diplomacy |
| Promote Authority | Characters leave factions Increased crown authority Increased council opinion | +1 per Diplomacy +0.5 per Martial +0.5 per Learning |

Diarchs receive mandate events and may grant benefits to liege, split rewards, pocket them, or ignore duties entirely. Aptitude influences effectiveness.

## Loyalty

Each diarch has loyalty determining power abuse likelihood:

- **Selfless**: Never use borrowed powers; always grant mandate benefits to liege. Agree to power sharing termination
- **Situational**: Sometimes use borrowed powers; split mandate benefits with liege or ignore duties. Termination requires compensation if Scales not at level 1
- **Self-Interested**: Attempt to use borrowed powers; pocket all mandate benefits. Refuse termination if Scales favor them

Loyalty increases with positive Opinion, Dread, Compassion and Honor. Automatically Selfless if liege has Loyalty hook on diarch or diarch has extremely high Honor.

## Scales of Power

Scales of Power (0-100) represent the balance between liege and diarch. Longer sharing increases towards diarch, giving them more realm power. Both may swing Scales every 2 years via:

| Option | Count | Duke | King+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leverage Prestige | 75 | 150 | 350 |
| Leverage Piety | 50 | 100 | 250 |
| Leverage Gold | 100 | 300 | 750 |
| Ask Head of Faith Intercession | 50/100 | 100/300 | 250/750 |
| Use a Hook |  |  |  |
| (Roads to Power) Leverage Influence | 30 | 60 | 100 |
| Leverage Personal Movement Power | 100 | 100 | 100 |

Piety options require same faith. Head of Faith intercession requires 60+ Opinion while opponent lacks 40+ Opinion or Strong Hook.

Scales favoring diarch require Prestige for liege's mundane actions (revoking titles, retracting vassals, imprisoning, executing unless faith has Human Sacrifice or Gruesome Festivals tenet).

## Regency

Regency occurs when a ruler is imprisoned by non-vassals or travels away on external activities. Ends automatically if Scales at level 1 or diarch has Selfless loyalty; otherwise requires Prestige, Piety, Gold, or Weak Hook. During Regency: liege gains +10% Stress Loss, regent gains +15% Stress Gain. Regent gains 150 Prestige when ended.

Regent selection order (can be manually designated every 10 years, reducing opinion with successors and Courtly vassals):
1. Primary heir
2. Spouse or parent
3. Child or sibling
4. Councilor family member

Regency has 4 levels with unique borrowed powers (vetoable by liege at Prestige cost; using them may cause Strife):

| Level | Scales | Power | Effects |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-33 | Siphon Treasury | Stewardship challenge; success grants Gold and Embezzler secret, loses county resources. Failure: 15-50 Strife and 30-75 Opinion loss. Crime on failure. |
| 2 | 34-66 | Regent Imprison | Imprison vassals/courtiers; gain 10 Dread and 15 Strife on success. Failure: Tyranny on courtier or Remove Regent war on vassal. Liege veto costs 150 Prestige. |
| 3 | 67-99 | Legal Meddling | Gain Unpressed Claim on vassal's primary title. Success: 10 Strife and -20 vassal Opinion. Failure: Stress and -10 Opinion. Cost: 150 Prestige guarantee, 80 Prestige for 50% chance, or Learning challenge. |
| 4 | 100 | Modify Vassal Contract/Entrench Regency | Lower obligation or add right to vassal contract (-40 Opinion). Or entrench regency (150 Prestige). |

## Entrenched Regency and Co-Monarchy

Co-Monarchy begins when Feudal/Sōryō/Tribal/Wanua King/Emperor appoints family member as co-monarch (auto-designated heir; ended only by imprisonment and execution). Entrenched Regency is automatic for underage/Incapable rulers or those taking Enter Seclusion decision. Level 4 Regency can become Entrenched. When ruler comes of age, Scales reset to 0 and termination becomes possible.

| Level | Scales | Effects | Liege Cost | Can End |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-19 | Siphon Treasury and Subsidize Liege Authority borrowed powers available | 0 | Yes |
| 2 | 20-39 | Free extra vassal contract change; Shift Privileges power available | 0 | Requires acceptance |
| 3 | 40-59 | Legal Meddling and Diarch Revoke Title powers available | 75+ | Requires acceptance |
| 4 | 60-79 | Diarch Imprison and Diarch Retract Vassal powers available | 150+ | Requires acceptance |
| 5 | 80-94 | Elevate Self to Co-Emperor power available (if conditions met) | 250+ | No |
| 6 | 95-100 | Attempt to Overthrow and Invite Conspirators to Coup powers available | 350+ | No |

## Vizierate

Vizierate (Clan government) costs 350 Prestige to appoint. Diarch gains Siphon Treasury power; liege gains Mulct Vizier interaction (gain gold, swing Scales toward diarch). If diarch is Tax Collector, gains 50% Scales as Tax Collector Aptitude. Cannot start new vizierate for 5 years after ending one. Termination without landed title grant incurs income penalty (0-80% depending on Scales).

Scales go to level 7 under Vizierate:

| 5 | 70-79 | +40% | 56% | 150+ | No |

## Duumvirate

Duumvirate (requires Roman Ceremonies tradition, Emperors only) allows appointment of close family or powerful house heads as diarch. Liege gets percentage Influence bonus; diarch gets flat monthly bonus (both increase every 5 years). Diarch's children may gain Born in the Purple trait.

Diarch automatically becomes Designated Heir; executing or defeating liege in war grants all titles. Previous Designated Heir loses 50 Opinion.

Duumvirate ends only via diarch imprisonment, requiring liege to banish or give trait (Eunuch, Blind, Disfigured, Maimed, One-Legged). If diarch has claims, option to renounce them. Monasticism faith offers Devoted trait option.

| Level | Scales | Effects | Influence Bonus | Liege Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-20 | Siphon Treasury, Co-Emperor Imprison, Shift Privileges powers | +50% | 0 |
| 2 | 21-40 | Co-Emperor Revoke Title, Co-Emperor Retract Vassal, Adjust Bureaucracy powers | +75% | 0 |
| 3 | 41-60 | Demand Private Despotate power; Scapegoat interaction available | +100% | 75+ |
| 4 | 61-80 | Request Imperial Expedition power available | +150% | 150+ |
| 5 | 81-100 | Attempt to Overthrow and Invite Conspirators to Coup powers available | +150% | 250+ |

### Nominal Duumvirate

Children receive Nominal Duumvirate instead. On adulthood, converts to regular Duumvirate; diarch receives skill points based on Scales swing (reduced by half); both get same Influence bonus. Liege can swing Scales both ways; swinging toward diarch is free and grants +20 Opinion.

| Level | Scales | Adulthood Skill Points | Influence Bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-20 |  |  |
| 2 | 21-40 | +2 education, +1 two others | +50% |
| 3 | 41-60 | +3 education, +2 two others | +75% |
| 4 | 61-80 | +4 education, +3 two others | +100% |
| 5 | 81-100 | +5 education, +4 two others | +150% |

Byzantine Empire starts 1066 with Duumvirate, 1178 with Nominal Duumvirate.

## Grand Secretariat

Grand Secretariat (Hegemony of China, cannot be ended) appoints diarch as councilor (decided by Grand Secretariat Control law). Diarch gains +1 Disciple Limit and +100 Movement Power.

Mandates differ:

| Mandate | Benefits | Aptitude |
|---|---|---|
| Placate Movements | Movement Power character gain/faction exit; Slander vs other movements more effective; Increased movement join acceptance | +1 per Diplomacy +0.3 per Stewardship/Intrigue/Learning |
| Cultivate Prowess | Positive men-at-arms modifiers; Positive levy modifiers; Skilled knights | +1 per Martial +0.5 per Prowess +0.5 per Diplomacy |
| Cultivate Merit | Increased examination merit rewards; Mentorship more effective; Merit gain; Confucian Education trait/experience | +2 per Merit Rank +1 per Disciple +0.25 per Confucian Education level |

Scales go to level 6:

| Level | Scales | Power | Effects | Liege Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-19 | Shift Privileges | Diplomacy challenge for county corruption. Success: Gold and 10 Strife. Failure: Stress and -20 Opinion. | 0 |
| 2 | 20-39 | Secretary Director Imprison | Imprison vassals/courtiers; gain 5 Dread and 15 Strife. Veto costs 150 Prestige. | 0 |
| 3 | 40-59 | Modify Circuit Administration | Free vassal contract change liege cannot refuse. | 75+ |
| 4 | 60-79 | Change Mandate | Select mandate, -20 Opinion with liege. | 150+ |
| 5 | 80-89 | Scapegoat Co-Ruler | Requires higher Diplomacy/Intrigue; gain Influence, Strife, and opinion loss. | 250+ |
| 6 | 90+ | Invite Conspirators to Coup | Unlock Overthrow decision; can increase acceptance with Gold or Hook. | 350+ |
