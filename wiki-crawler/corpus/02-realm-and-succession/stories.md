Stories are smaller situations that typically affect a single character. They can begin manually or randomly, progress through events, and shape character development.

## Common Stories

These stories start manually via player decisions.

### Commission Epic

**Decision:** Commission Epic

Chronicle your family's feats with a great epic. Tracks composer and exceptional chronicle chance (increases above 100%). Choose a theme on start:

- Claims: +1-2 Pressed Claims on completion
- Character: +150 Prestige, +50 Renown
- Family: +75 Prestige, +75 Renown
- Truth: +150 Prestige, +250 Piety

Every 40-60 days, events affect chronicle chance and artifact effects. Gain a Book artifact (if Royal Court) or Journal (otherwise) on completion.

### Conqueror

**Trait:** Conqueror

Your ambition is boundless. The world awaits conquest. Every 1-2 years gain: gold knight with high Prowess and Strong Hook, courtier with Military Engineer trait, opinion bonuses with Belligerent/Glory Hound vassals, opinion penalties with negative-opinion vassals, herd if Nomadic.

### Hunting a Mystical Animal

**Trigger:** Mystical Animal sighted

During a hunt, you glimpse a mystical white beast. Capturing it would rival the Holy Grail! Random events occur every 1-2 years.

### A Loyal Hostage

**Trigger:** Child hostage converting to your culture/faith

A young hostage begins warming to you and your ways. Tracks Current Loyalty (−10 to +10). On age of majority, outcomes depend on Loyalty and Opinion:

- High Loyalty: Adopt into house (+150 Renown) or gain Perpetual Hook
- Moderate: Strong Hook
- Low: Rivals
- Very Low: Nothing

Events every 6-12 months affect Loyalty. 75% chance to start if hostage becomes Friends with warden's child/spouse/councilor, or Rivals with parent (if parent not warden's spouse).

### Frankokratia

**Trigger:** Catholic Crusade preparation, Administrative Byzantine Empire, coastal republic in Francia/Iberia/Southern Europe, Byzantine Emperor/capital not Catholic

**Requirements:** May vary per Chance of Frankokratia game rule

Western crusaders lured by a Byzantine claimant turn toward Christendom's heart. Diverted Crusaders tracked by the story; on crusade launch, all start war against Byzantine Empire.

### The Greatest of Khans

**Trigger:** Greatest of Khans trait, Nomadic government (requires Khans of the Steppe DLC)

You are unmatched. Unlocks Greatest of Khans' Onslaught casus belli and Offer Submission or Ruin interaction. End via Reform the Great Khaganate decision. If unended after 75 years: −200 Legitimacy, lose trait; Unrestricted Dominance downgrades to Absolute; Disobedient Kurultai worsens succession.

## Pet Stories

Start when a character gains a pet; name it within days. Every 1-2 years, 30% chance for related event.

### Pet Cat

**Mechanics:** Randomized icon by breed, +1 Intrigue/Learning modifier, pet button (−10 Stress), story ends after 13-19 years with option to pay Gold for Small Wall Ornament. 6% chance to save from Murder scheme (ends story). Bonus events if Pet Dog present. Royal Court DLC adds Rat-hunting Cat option, medium Health boost.

### Pet Dog

**Mechanics:** Randomized icon by breed, one of three modifiers: Pet Dog (+1 Diplomacy/Prowess), Scenthound (+1 Diplomacy/Learning, +0.1 Prestige, hunt bonus, requires Tours & Tournaments), or Beasthound (+1 Martial/Prowess, +0.1 Prestige, hunt bonus, requires Tours & Tournaments). Story ends 13-19 years with Small Wall Ornament option. 5% Murder scheme save. Pet button every 5 years (−10 Stress 98%, random claim 1%, injury 1%). Bonus if Pet Cat present. Affects Hunt activities.

### Hunting Eagle

+1 Martial/Stewardship, +10% Falconry Hunt Success. Story ends 49-68 years with Trinket option. Affects Hunt activities.

### Warhorse

+1 Prowess, +0.25 Monthly Prestige. After 13-19 years, breed or end. 20% Murder scheme save. Train or breed via events:

| Focus | 1st bonus | 2nd bonus |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | +10% Levy Size | +15% Levy Size |
| Agility | +1 Prowess | +2 Prowess |
| Endurance | +25% Stress Loss | +50% Stress Loss |
| Fierceness | +2 Advantage | +6 Advantage |

## Event Stories

Random yearly chance per character, once only.

### Translate a Great Work

**Requirements:** Learning lifestyle or Scholars camp purpose; any courtier/follower

**Bonuses:** More likely if high Learning

+1 Learning. Tracks Translation Quality. Every 1-3 months, event affects Quality. After 5 events, gain 1 Learning lifestyle perk and 10-year modifier: Quality 5+: +10 Clergy Opinion, +1 Monthly Piety, +0.25 Prestige; Quality 0-4: +5 Clergy Opinion, +0.25 Piety.

### Vassal Mediation

**Requirements:** Duty lifestyle focus; two adult non-close-family vassals

**Mechanics:** Tracks two vassals and Fairness value. Events every 10-20 days affect Fairness. After 3 events: Excellent outcome (both +20 Opinion, Friends); Good (Rivals removed, Fairness-dependent); Poor (one +20, other −40 Opinion); Worst (Rivals, fairness tracks outcome).

Ends if either vassal dies/imprisoned.

### Pet Rock

**Requirements:** Age 13+, Eccentric trait

+1 Stewardship, −5% Stress Gain. Events every 2-3 years (25% chance).

### Eminent Eunuch

**Requirements:** Independent, Governor Efficiency; Emperor+ (requires Roads to Power)

**Blockers:** Duumvirate, non-Emperor, no eunuchs

Eunuch of skill or threat. Tracks supported/opposed eunuch every 3-8 months. Modifiers scale by support level (−3 to +6 Stewardship, −50% to −10% Holding Taxes, −35 to −10 Opinion, +15% to −10% Hostile Scheme success, −50% to +25% Stress, −2 to +6 Intrigue).

### The Sycophant

**Requirements:** Adult; any powerful vassal; all councilors appointed

**Blockers:** No Royal Court

Courtier angering vassals. On start: −10 Powerful Vassal Opinion 5y OR fire councilor and +5 Powerful Vassal Opinion/+15 Stress 5y. Events every 2-4 months (50% chance). Ends in 5 years. More likely if low-skill, different-faith, different-culture, or lowborn councilor.

### Mirror in the Mud

**Requirements:** Ruler; more likely if Eccentric

Lowborn doppelgänger joins court, easier to befriend. Doppelgänger has +1 beauty level (−1 if you're Beautiful). Events every 1-2 years (50% chance). Ends if doppelgänger dies.

### Imaginary Friend

**Requirements:** Age 6-12, Ruler

**Blockers:** No blocking traits

**Bonuses:** More likely if Eccentric parent/guardian, Eccentric trait

+5 Learning, −2 Diplomacy, −10 Vassal Opinion. Events every 2-3 months (while child) gain traits, improve/harm friendship. Unique event during Meet Peers. Max friendship: gain Eccentric or Paranoid. Ends on adulthood unless Eccentric (new events based on personality traits).

### Foreign Raised Reformer

**Requirements:** Duke+; 5+ Learning; same culture as capital

**Blockers:** Cannot end while at war/imprisoned

**Bonuses:** More likely if high Learning, Egalitarian culture ethos

**Mechanics:** Raised far from home; embrace or reform culture. Every 3-4 months: reaffirm or reform culture (tracked). Outcomes:

- **Reaffirm twice:** Culture nickname, reduced tradition costs (if Culture Head), choice: Uniter/Conqueror (+150 Prestige, Invade Kingdom once, special scaling levy army) OR People's Great Defender (+500 Renown, +2 Fort Level/+40 Popular Opinion 30y per held county)

- **Reform thrice:** Reformer nickname, 1-2 random innovations, +75 Renown, positive 50-year capital modifier, option: pay 1500 Prestige for double Renown and extend to same-culture counties

### Destined Child

**Requirements:** Child age 10-13

**Blockers:** Dull, Slow, Stupid, Imbecile

**Mechanics:** +10% Monthly Lifestyle Experience. Every 200-250 days, events boost skills. On adulthood: +2-4 all skills, Tool of Fate modifier, story ends. Once per century. Ends if child dies.

## Adventurer Stories

Present while playing historical adventurers in 1066.

### The Song of El Cid

**Adventurer:** Rodrigo Díaz

Wrongfully exiled by liege Sancho II after war with Alfonso VI. Forge your own path or stay loyal. Tracks Loyalty (−5 to +5). Winning battles have 10% Tizona artifact creation chance; choice: keep (−Loyalty, +50 Gold) or give (gain Loyalty, Heroic legend seed if Legends of the Dead). Guaranteed vs. Valencia commander. Barcelona victory creates Colada artifact (same choice). Every 6-12 months, events affect Loyalty; story ends when events exhausted.

**Outcomes** (based on Loyalty vs. Sancho II as ruler):

- **Loyalty +5:** Loyal trait; choice: +350 Prestige/+400 Provisions/Strong Hook OR Veteran Adventurer/Feudal/vassal with best-development county
- **Loyalty −5:** Disloyal/Veteran; Feudal/vassal OR Disloyal/+150 Prestige OR +400 Provisions/−50 Opinion/Weak Hook
- **Between:** Fickle trait

Ends if Sancho II dies.

### The Legend of Hasan Sabbah

**Adventurer:** Hasan Sabbah

More than armies; faith topples empires. Establish Assassins, unburden your people from Seljuks, become Old Man of the Mountain. Choice: convert to Isma'ilism, appoint Amira Zarrab as follower. Tracks Progress (increases via Evangelize Faith decision).

- **Progress 5, Isma'ilism/Nizarism, Head of Faith landed:** Travel Cairo event
- **Abu Mansur al-Nizar alive in Cairo:** Friend, +250 Piety, +1 Progress, convert Nizarism
- **Progress 15:** Unlock Agitate Populace, Begin Revolution, Found Assassins decisions
- **Caliph al-Mustansir dies (Isma'ilism/Nizarism):** Friend Abu Mansur OR friend new Caliph/convert Isma'ilism
- **Ends:** Religion no longer Muslim, became ruler, or converted to Sunni doctrine

### The Violet Poet

**Adventurer:** Wallada bint al-Mustakfi

Caliph's daughter, last Umayyad. Walk unveiled through austere world, sharpen women's minds. Travel to Tulaytulah option on start. Every 5 years, adopt follower/courtier with 21+ Diplomacy or Poet trait as heir; succession transfers story. Every 100-200 days (50% chance), upgrade Double-Moon Tome artifact or gain Herbalist. Travel may trigger upgrades.
