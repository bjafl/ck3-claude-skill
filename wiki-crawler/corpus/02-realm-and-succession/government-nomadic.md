Used by Steppe rulers; without Khans of the Steppe, replaced by Tribal. AI nomads with a very weak realm may switch to Herder.

Landless government giving rulers a nomadic capital domicile. May be affected by Steppe Season situations if The Great Steppe game rule is enabled. Can create cadet branches, raze holdings, swear blood brotherhoods, ask rulers to become willing tributaries, and use the Domination law. Grants the Herd resource, a 150 Dread cap, the Way of the Nomad trait, a Kurultai council, a distinct set of court positions, ignores different-faith marriage penalties with rulers of the same government, and avoids Attrition while in the Steppe region; realms are named after their culture. Cannot construct holdings or use innovations past the Tribal Era. Cannot create titles above their primary title's rank, but can use the Dominate Title decision. Dynasty Heads with Nomadic government unlock the Nomadic legacy track.

Uses Horde Riders instead of Levies. Men-at-arms come from upgrading Horde Riders or recruiting from subjects (free of Horde Riders cost, but Gold cost tripled; can recruit any regiment type available to the subject). Unlike other governments, nomad men-at-arms don't replenish automatically — must be manually refilled, like Adventurer armies. Nomadic rulers can declare war even with armies already raised.

Independent nomads can migrate to any Steppe-region title within migration range (range increases with Domination law level); max migration title rank depends on Dominance law level (none = landless). Migrating swaps the primary title for the target and leaves the vacated domain to a Herder. Peaceful migration needs acceptance from the highest-Herd independent ruler in the target's de jure territory (or their liege, if all are vassals) — refusal starts a war that must be won to migrate; Herders always accept. If the target belonged to a tributary, the character is asked to become a tributary too; refusing gives the previous suzerain a Retaliation War casus belli and −50 opinion. Successful migration pushes other rulers in the target territory to the nearest county outside it, within their own realm.

Nomads can raid; culture heads additionally unlock the Plunder raid intent (see [Tribal](government-tribal.md) § Raiding for the general raid-intent table). Two Nomadic rulers who are rivals or nemeses can challenge each other to a duel.

### County Fertility

Affects Herd. Influenced by steppe season, terrain, holding type, and other factors affecting Fertility/Fertility Growth.

## Obedience

Rulers' and courtiers' Obedience determines whether they're Obedient or Disobedient. Obedient vassals won't join/create factions against their liege or start hostile schemes against them, and will migrate with their liege; Obedient Kurultai members are necessary for a safe succession; Obedient tributaries can have Tributary War Support in their contract. Calculated from relationship/family ties, legitimacy, opinion, traits, dread/prowess difference, and court position. Can be secured for 15 years by paying Herd, Gold, Dread, or spending a hook. The Chief Law Overseer Aptitude reduces the threshold slightly (−1 to −5).

| Always Obedient | Always Disobedient |
|---|---|
| Primary heir, underage, blood sibling, best friend, Herder, recipient of a Paiza artifact | Nemesis, greater military strength, more Herd, Lost Humiliation, war within last 5 years |

Obedience threshold by rank: Count 50, Duke 70, King 100, Emperor 150.

**Obedience modifiers** — variable/dynamic: + Opinion value; +Dread/2 if ruler's Dread >10 and exceeds target's; Legitimacy 3+ gives +25, otherwise −15; −10 for inferior Dread; −15 for lower Prowess.

Traits: Greatest of Khans +50; Conqueror +35; Loyal/Craven +25 (Disloyal/Deceitful/Stubborn negative); Greatest of Khans descendant +20 (Arrogant negative); Content/Humble +10 (Paranoid/Ambitious negative); Gregarious/Diligent +5 (Brave negative).

Relationships: Friend +100 (Rival negative); Spouse/Child/Ward/Guardian +50 (no positive Special Relationship = negative); Concubine/Close Family +25; Extended Family +10.

Realm and court: not preferred heir is negative (magnitude 100); Yeke Jarquchi (Chief Law Overseer) or Truce +50; part of the Kurultai +25; has a court position (courtiers only) +15, lacking one is negative.
