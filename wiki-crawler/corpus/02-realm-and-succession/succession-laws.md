There are three types of law affecting succession to a title: gender, realm and title. Changing gender or succession laws costs 500 Prestige if government is Feudal, Administrative, or Celestial, and 2000 Prestige if government is Clan. It also requires all powerful vassals to either have a positive opinion of the liege or be Terrified or imprisoned.

## Gender law

The gender law determines which gender can inherit titles and claims and who can have more than one partner if its faith doctrines allow. Changing gender law requires High Crown Authority for feudal and clan realms, and only Limited Tribal Authority for tribal realms. There are a few cultural traditions that unlock the Equal law regardless of doctrines.

| Law | Can inherit, be candidates or be given titles | Concubinage and polygamy | Required views on gender doctrine |
|---|---|---|---|
| Male only | Men only | Men only | Male Dominated |
| Male preference | Men take precedence | Men only | Male Dominated Equal |
| Equal | Both genders | Both genders | Equal |
| Female preference | Women take precedence | Women only | Equal Female Dominated |
| Female only | Women only | Women only | Female Dominated |

## Realm succession

The realm succession law determines which and how many heirs will inherit the character's titles upon death. All children will gain claims on titles they did not inherit regardless of the realm succession law. The realm succession law cannot be changed during a power sharing.

### Single heir

Single heir successions are available to most governments at the third authority level but rulers with Administrative, Celestial, Meritocratic, Meritocratic Khanate or Ritsuryō governments must be independent. Clan governments must pay an additional 1500 Piety and their level of devotion must be at least Devoted Servant. Administrative, Celestial, Meritocratic, Meritocratic Khanate and Ritsuryō governments require the Authoritative level of influence to change the realm succession to single heir. Ritsuryō governments also require the ruler to be the Ceremonial Monarch.

| House Seniority | Feudal Clan | Heraldry or Table of Princes | +5 House −10 Glory Hound vassal −15 Eligible child | Upon succession, all of the late ruler's titles will be given to the oldest eligible member of their house. #### Camp succession

Adventurers use the Camp Succession law, which gives everything to the first valid follower among the following:

1. Designated heir
2. Oldest child without any title
3. Closest relative without any title
4. The follower with the highest Prowess

#### Mandala succession

Rulers with Mandala government use the Mandala Succession law, which gives all titles to the oldest child allowed by the Gender Law but they will also have to pass three challenges: one from the predecessor's Mandala Aspect and two from randomly-selected aspects. The difficulty of the challenge depends on the rank of the inherited primary title. The challenge from the predecessor's Mandala Aspect can automatically be passed if the aspect was upgraded to its final level and one challenge can be automatically passed if the dynasty completed the Divine Aspirations legacy. If the heir is imprisoned they automatically lose.

- Aspect of Creation requires a Stewardship challenge
- Aspect of Serenity requires a Diplomacy challenge
- Aspect of Destruction requires a Martial challenge
- Aspect of Trickery requires an Intrigue challenge

If the heir passes the challenge it will gain a point in the skill they used.

- If the heir passes all three challenges they will not suffer any penalty
- If the heir passes two challenges they will lose 1 Level of Devotion and 100 Legitimacy
- If the heir passes one challenge they will lose 2 Levels of Devotion and 200 Legitimacy
- If the heir fails all challenges they will lose 3 Levels of Devotion and 500 Legitimacy and gain 40 Stress, all vassals either become independent or join another liege, and every Kingdom and above title is destroyed

### Candidate succession

Certain governments use a candidate-based succession, where every character eligible has a Candidate Score and when the succession happens the title goes to the character with the highest Candidate Score. All noble characters in the realm can spend Influence to increase or decrease anyone's score and Opinion but there is a limit to how much they can affect the score based on the family rating. They can also ask other rulers to support the same candidate. Upon the death of the governor the family member with the highest succession score will inherit all titles and may reward the character who supported them the most if they're part of another House.

### Kurultai

Rulers with Nomadic government use the Kurultai law, where there are no requirements besides the cost for designating a heir but what happens upon succession depends on the Obedience of the Kurultai. If all Kurultai are Obedient and the region season is not Havsarsan Zud then the realm will have a Stable Succession, where the primary heir inherits everything. The Mongol Empire will always have Stable Succession. If not all Kurultai are Obedient, some Kurultai positions are empty, or the region season is Havsarsan Zud then the realm will have a Chaotic Succession, where every sibling and disobedient Kurultai favored by the gender law will gain Pressed Claims on all of the heir's titles, all obedient Kurultai will gain -25 Opinion, and one of the following events will happen depending on the number of disobedient Kurultai:

|---|---|
|  | 10% of the Herd is given to a Nomadic neighbor A tributary becomes independent and gains -100 Opinion A sibling takes over a tributary's realm, becomes independent and gains -35 Opinion A sibling who is not a Rival becomes one A men-at-arms regiment is given to a neighbor Every domain county loses 5-50 Control Lose a level of fame 3 courtiers join a neighbor's court |
|  | 20% of the Herd is given to a Nomadic neighbor 3 tributaries become independent and gain -100 Opinion 20% of the titles, Gold and Herd will be inherited by a Count or Duke sibling who is not Content or Generous Lose 500 Legitimacy All Gold is divided evenly between up to 5 siblings |

## Title succession

Rulers who have Feudal, Clan, Tribal or Wanua government and are at peace can apply a Title Succession law on Duchy, Kingdom and Empire titles at the cost of 1500 Prestige each. Under Title Succession law all elector characters will vote on who will become the heir and vassals and owe allegiance to the title rather than their liege personally. Kings and emperors can also apply a Title Succession law to all top-rank titles via the Adopt Special Succession Type decision. Removing a Title Succession law costs Prestige scaling with title rank.
