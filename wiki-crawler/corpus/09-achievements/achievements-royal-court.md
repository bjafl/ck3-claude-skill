| [Lingua Franca icon] Lingua FrancaHave all Royal Courts in the world speak your Court Language. May not be available for characters created in the Ruler Designer. | [No] Custom character | Royal Court [Yes] All Royal Courts have the same court language | No custom characters allowed. The most straight forward way to accomplish this is to destroy all royal courts in the game other than your own. Fast kingdom conquest can be done with the By The Sword tradition, which removes the once per lifetime restriction on kingdom level holy wars. AI Adoption of another court's language is weighted down by being a different religious group and their court capital being outside of the same geographic region, and against the number of counties with the AI's current court language. This means that an AI whose religious group is different from yours or whose capital is outside of your capital's region will not adopt your court's language. To keep a vassal king from changing culture to the local culture, or change an existing vassal king's culture, you can convert the culture of their capital county. The AI will switch culture to their liege's if their capital county is the same culture as their liege's. However, the AI is biased towards keeping their current court language. If after sometime the AI doesn't switch their court language to yours, revoke the kingdom title and give it to a character of your culture. Consider starting as Charles the Bald in 867. You start with claims on three additional kingdoms held by your much weaker relatives, and once conquered you will be in a great position to take the "Restore Carolingian Borders" decision giving you a huge de jure empire (and more importantly, the Primogeniture succession law) very early on. From there, just have fun conquering! | VH |
| [Nobody Comes to Fika icon] Nobody Comes to FikaWith the County of Fika as your Primary Title, diverge your Culture and spread it to 30 Counties held by you or your Vassals |  | [Yes] Has created a divergent culture [Yes] At least 30 counties in the realm have the same culture | Note: The County of Fika has to be your primary title at the time you diverge your culture. After that you do not need to have the County of Fika as your primary title anymore to get the achievement. Disable AI culture hybridization and divergence in the game rules to prevent fracture of your culture by your vassals. Pick on your weaker neighbors and diverge your culture as soon as you have the prestige, then form a duchy and just play a regular game, converting enough counties to get the achievement. Consider swearing fealty to the Duke of Kanem right away for safety and ease of expansion, since he'll create a kingdom soon after, allowing you to create a duchy yourself. Converting to the Coptic faith lets you promote your culture 50% faster in converted counties thanks to the Communal Identity tenet. Or simply reform Bori to have that tenet. Alternatively, start as Jarl Haesteinn in 867. Immediately assign your steward to convert the County of Montaigu to Norse culture and your Godi (Realm Priest) to fabricate a claim on the County of Fika. In the mean time, use the "Conquer County" Casus Belli for the County of Melilla to secure a foothold in Africa. Next, use the "Conquer County" Casus Belli to take the County of Zammour, which grants the diplomatic range to declare war on coastal Sub-Saharan holdings. Declare another "Conquer County" war for any Sub-Saharan coastal holding of your choosing, but be careful not to lose Haesteinn's special event troops to attrition. You may now enforce your claim on the County of Fika. After taking the County of Fika and converting the County of Montaigu to Norse culture, select the title "County of Fika," and make it your primary title. Diverge the Norse culture. Once diverged, Varangian Adventure for a title of your choosing or continue as you are and complete the achievement by converting 30 counties to your culture. | VH |
