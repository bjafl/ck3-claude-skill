A dynasty is an unbroken noble line descending from a landed character who founded it. Each dynasty consists of multiple houses competing to bring Renown upon the family name. Dynasty Heads can rename their dynasty at any time.

## Houses

Each dynasty has a Founding House, usually sharing the dynasty's name, as its first house. Ruling members can establish Cadet Branches to create new houses within the dynasty. Each house has a customizable name, motto, and coat of arms featuring two quarters from the dynasty's arms.

If a House Head demands religious conversion from a house member and is refused, a new Cadet Branch is automatically created. Characters born into their father's house under patrilineal marriage or mother's house under matrilineal marriage.

### House head

The House Head leads a house and gains several powers: calling members to war at no cost, demanding religious conversion, obtaining a Weak Hook on newly born members, and receiving monthly Prestige from living and landed members. House Heads with appropriate religious doctrines can legitimize bastards or approve divorces. The Primary Heir becomes House Head upon the current Head's death.

When a character creates a Cadet Branch, they become its House Head and are freed from the old Head's direct influence unless they were the Dynasty Head.

#### Dynasty head

The strongest House Head in a dynasty becomes Dynasty Head. If another House Head becomes 10% stronger, they take the position. Dynasty Head strength is calculated by military power for most governments, but Administrative, Celestial, Meritocratic, Meritocratic Khanate, and Ritsuryō governments use Powerful Family rating. Adventurer government House Heads cannot become Dynasty Heads.

Dynasty Heads gain monthly Prestige from living dynasty members and can disinherit anyone. They have exclusive interactions with same-faith dynasty members:

| Interaction | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Call Dynasty Member To War | 75 Renown | Target may join your current war |
| Claim Title | 150 Renown | Gain Unpressed Claim on target's primary title; target gets -50 Opinion |
| Denounce | 50 Renown (unlanded); 100 Renown + 300 Prestige (heir); 125 Renown + 500 Prestige (landed) | Target gains Denounced trait and -50 Opinion |
| End Dynasty Wars | 75 Renown | All wars between dynasty members end in White Peace |
| Forgive | 25 Renown | Remove Denounced trait and Opinion penalty |

With the first Adventure legacy unlocked, Dynasty Heads unlock Send to the Varangian Guard (350 Prestige, requires unlanded/unmarried member, sends them to Byzantine court with Varangian trait). This is unavailable if the Head's faith has Pacifism, there's active war/truce with Byzantium, or the Byzantine title is destroyed.

Characters with Denounced or Disinherited traits lose those traits if they become Dynasty Head.

### Adoption

Adoption is available to unmarried rulers or those in marriages without children and requires one of: Compassionate trait, childlessness with low Fertility, unmarried with Homosexual/Asexual sexuality, Noble Adoption/Cultivated Sophistication/Dynastic Pragmatism culture tradition, or same-sex spouse with no opposite-gender Consort/Concubine. Children cannot be adopted if imprisoned, landed, have landed close/extended family, or already belong to the same house.

Unless the culture has Noble Adoption or Dynastic Pragmatism tradition (or Adoption is Always Allowed), adoption costs 150 Prestige + 75 Renown, plus Piety based on faith relationship: 0 (Righteous or same), 100 (Astray), 150 (Hostile), 250 (Evil).

Acceptance increases from no parents, friendship, guardianship, shared personality traits, or Ambitious trait in the child. Orphans and those with Devoted trait may bypass age restrictions with Cultivated Sophistication or Dynastic Pragmatism cultures. Adoption decreases Courtly vassal opinion and non-Content children's opinion by -20.

Adventurers always adopt lowborn followers free of Renown costs, creating a new house for the follower. Adopted followers cannot have Devoted or Gallivanter traits.

### House relations

House member interactions improve or worsen relations between houses. With no action for 50 years, relations drift toward Neutral. Positive actions include head gifts (+20%), prisoner release (+20%), friendships (best +20%, regular +10%), completed education (+10%), scheme promotion (+20%), hostage exchanges (+20%), bloc leadership (+20%), and bloc joining (+20%).

Negative actions include abduction (-10%), broken betrothe (-10%), war (-10%), divorce (-10%), rivalries (regular -20%, nemesis -20%), cheating/murder discovery (-20%), execution (-20%), knight death (-20%), imprisonment (-20%), raiding (-20%), refusing ally support (-20%), title revocation (-20%), torture/disfigurement (-20%), slander (-20%), governorship revocation (-20%), estate raid success (-30%), broken grand wedding promises (-20%), and bloc departure (-30%)/removal (-30%).

### Blocs

Blocs are defensive alliances between different house rulers in Japan blocking inter-house wars. Only house heads can leave or request joining blocs. Leaving gives 5-year truces with previous bloc members, -30% relation with leading house, and -15 opinion with leader. Joining costs 100 Prestige; members cannot leave for 10 years.

Bloc leaders choose which houses join, remove houses at will, and are the only ones who can create factions. Only the leader can create factions against top lieges, with automatic member joining. Leaders cannot join other blocs but can pass leadership (+30% relation). Other members can spend 100 Influence (or 200 Prestige without) to request leadership if not liege to the leader, have higher title rank, are two fame levels above, or higher aspiration. Leaders refuse if the asker has different government, but hooks force acceptance.

Cohesion (0-100) grants bonuses based on leader's House Attribute. It's affected by member house relations, reduced by landed rulers (especially different government), increased by +0.25 per house matching leader's attribute, decreased by -0.25 per mismatched house, and -0.1 monthly per member. Reaching 0 disbands the bloc.

Only Ritsuryō or Sōryō government heads create/join blocs or request leadership, though members join regardless. If leadership changes government, previous attribute bonuses continue but cohesion still reduces from attribute mismatch.

There are 5 starting blocs in each date, each starting with 50 Cohesion.

### Noble families

Houses where the head has Administrative, Celestial, Meritocratic, Meritocratic Khanate, Ritsuryō, or Sōryō government become Noble Families with a Family Attribute and titular County title for the head.

## Disinheriting

Disinheriting gives the target the Disinherited trait and permanent -75 Opinion. It's reversible for 75 Renown. Dynasty Heads can disinherit any dynasty member for:

- Free (Disputed Heritage or Bastard trait)
- 75 Renown + 150 Prestige (unlanded)
- 150 Renown + 300 Prestige (heir to title)
- 225 Renown + 900 Prestige (landed or Administrative family member)
- 50 Legitimacy (not Dynasty Head's child or primary heir)
- 100 Legitimacy (child but not primary heir)
- 200 Legitimacy (primary title heir)
- 20 Stress (Compassionate or Forgiving trait)
- -20 General Opinion for 5 years

Dynasty members with the final Coterie legacy can disinherit their own children (no Renown cost, -30 vassal opinion).

## Renown

Renown is accumulated by dynasties and funds powerful Dynasty Head interactions, legacy unlocks, and Splendor increases. Living dynasty members add Renown; Prestige to Dynasty Head and House Head.

Marriage to another dynasty's ruler grants 80% of their primary title's Renown. Non-ruler house members cap at +2 total (100 members); Dynasty Head Prestige at +3; House Head Prestige at +5. Rulers under same-dynasty lieges don't add Renown.

### Dynasty legacies

Dynasty legacies are permanent bonuses unlocked by the Dynasty Head affecting all dynasty members. Each of five tracks contains five legacies. AI characters only unlock legacies from the track with most progress. First legacy costs 250 Renown; each subsequent costs 500 more.

Standard tracks: Warfare, Law, Guile, Blood, Erudition, Glory, Kin, Customs, Activities, Heroic Bloodline, Legitimacy.

Heritage-specific tracks unlock after first legacy is obtained:
- **North Germanic (North Germanic heritage or Norse descent)**: Adventure, Pillage
- **Iberian (Iberian heritage or realm capital in Iberia)**: Metropolitan, Coterie
- **Iranian (Iranian heritage or descendant culture)**: Brilliance
- **Administrative government**: Bureaucracy
- **Nomadic government**: Nomadic
- **Celestial/Meritocratic/Meritocratic Khanate government**: Virtues
- **Mandala/Wanua government**: Divine Aspirations
- **Ritsuryō/Sōryō government**: Elegance

Each track unlocks powerful bonuses to skills, resources, court positions, building capabilities, and unique interactions scaled to dynasty level.
