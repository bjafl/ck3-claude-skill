A hook represents that a character owes you a favor or that you can coerce them. Only one hook can exist per character; new hooks replace weaker ones or those with shorter duration. Holding a hook during non-lethal duels unlocks the Something to Hide special move. Hooks come in three strength levels: weak, strong, and perpetual.

## Weak hooks

Weak hooks can be used once, providing acceptance bonuses (usually not forced) for most interactions. The Obligation hook halves monthly base court position salary (not task expenses).

## Perpetual hooks

Perpetual hooks are permanent and reusable every five years, providing acceptance bonuses (usually not forced). Cannot be fabricated.

- **Loyal Hostage**: Obtained from positive A Loyal Hostage story conclusion
- **Indebted Claimant**: Obtained on each installed claimant if Install Claimants oath is fulfilled
- **Filial Piety**: Obtained at birth if faith has Filial Piety tenet

## Strong hooks

Strong hooks reuse every five years and force acceptance for most interactions. Passively prevent target from joining factions, declaring war, imprisoning, or demanding religious conversion. Having a strong hook on a Realm Priest forces maximum contribution.

### What hooks can't do

Hooks cannot be invoked for: Call to War, Contract War Assistance, Hire Mercenaries, Offer Peace, Purchase Truce, Offer Vassalage, Invite to Activity, Negotiate Settlement, Request as House Head, Influence Ward Personality, or Give Vassal Directive.

Arbitrary rulers may dismiss hooks (costing opinion, prestige, and tyranny).

## Secrets

Characters acquire secrets from criminal or frowned-upon actions. Secrets are discovered through random events or spymaster Find Secrets tasks. Upon discovery, the discoverer can expose the secret (applying negative effects) or blackmail the target (requiring both lack the same secret and know each other's secret). Successful blackmail grants Weak Hook (shunned secret) or Strong Hook (criminal secret); refusal exposes the secret. Secrets can be shared among same-house members if the dynasty has the 4th Coterie legacy.

Exposed vassal/courtier secrets severe enough for imprisonment trigger instant imprisonment if they lack strong hooks (AI choice depends on Compassion trait and relation).

Doctrinal secrets are removed and replaced with matching traits if both the character's and liege's faiths accept the trait. Lover, illegitimacy, and attempted murder secrets auto-remove if no longer valid (marriage/death).

Characters with Witch secret cannot blackmail using it; if both have it, they can reveal it for +20 opinion. Characters asked to convert secretly always agree.
