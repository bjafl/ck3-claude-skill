Activities are special occasions hosted by a character, featuring multiple variants and phased events. Only one activity per character at a time. Incapable characters and those with disease traits cannot host or join. Activities require other characters to join; if none do, the activity cancels and the host gains 20 Stress.

The host chooses a barony for the activity (cost varies by barony); the map highlights the best 5 baronies. Attendees travel to the designated location unless it's their realm capital, bringing relevant entourages. Base cost depends on primary title rank, modified by culture, religion, event, and era modifiers.

| Rank | County | Duchy | Kingdom | Empire | Hegemony |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tribal/Wanua | 15 | 30 | 95 | 110 | 125 |
| Others | 30 | 60 | 140 | 170 | 200 |

Nomadic rulers use Herd instead of Gold for Hunt costs.

## Regular activities

**Hunt**: Prestige, Legitimacy, Stress loss, Glory Hound vassal opinion, Trophy artifacts, Hunter trait, Accolade glory, Provisions. Valid in realm with animal sighting, hunting grounds, royal reserves, high fertility. Variants: Hunt (standard), Legendary (doubled Prestige, artifact quality, legend seed), Falconry (requires Tours & Tournaments, trinket artifacts), Nerge (requires Khans of the Steppe/Nomadic, gives dread/legitimacy/martial). Cooldown 2 years. Requirements: Adult, no Vegetarians tradition, no disease/blind/infirm/maimed/severely injured/brutally mauled.

**Pilgrimage**: Piety, Legitimacy, Stress loss, Pilgrim trait, Gallowsbait trait loss. Valid at Holy Sites. Variants: Pious (standard piety), Worldly (culture modifier), Hajj (requires Hajj doctrine, gives Hajji trait). Cooldown 5 years per Holy Site. Requirements: Adult, Hajji trait if faith requires it.

**Feast**: Prestige, Legitimacy, Opinion, Stress loss, Eager Reveler trait. Valid in domain with food buildings. Variants: Feast (standard), Legendary Feast (requires owning/promoting legend, spreads legend), Murder Feast (requires Forever Infamous perk, tours & tournaments, chance to kill target), Tsagaan Sar (requires nomadic/Mongolic or Turkic, Jan-Mar, gives opinion/stress loss/gold/piety/herd/artifacts). Cooldown 5-15 years. Requirements: Adult, landed.

**Meet Peers**: Opinion. Valid in realm capital. For ages 4-13 only. Cooldown 3 years.

**Grand Rite**: Opinion, Stress loss, Lifestyle experience. Valid in realm capital. Requirements: Adult, Witch trait, Witch Coven house modifier. Cooldown 5 years.

**Festival**: Prestige, Piety, Stress loss, Popular opinion, Control, Development. Valid in domain/estate. Requirements: Ritsuryō or Sōryō government, Adult. Cooldown 5 years.

**Debate**: Influence, Merit, Movement Power, Confucian Education trait experience, become Elder. Valid at own/liege/top liege capital (or Kaifeng with palace building). Variants: Debate Movement (become leader), Imperial Debate (make movement favored). Requirements: Involved in Dynastic Cycle situation, not joined/leading political movement. Cooldown 5 years.

## Wandering activities

Available to adult Dukes+ with Wandering lifestyle perks. Inspection available to all with Administrative, Celestial, Ritsuryō, Meritocratic, Meritocratic Khanate government or Benevolent Governance tenet.

**Inspection**: Taxes, Levies, Control, Development, Pressed Claims, Opinion, Popular opinion, rare new mines. Valid in realm at low-control counties/borders. Cooldown 2 years. Cost: Treasury (if applicable) or Gold.

**Hike**: Fame, Opinion, Stress loss, Wise Man/Woman trait, choose traveling companion. Valid in realm with mountains/desert/hills/forest/taiga/jungle/oasis/no holding. Requires Storyteller perk. Cooldown 1.5 years. Cost: Gold.

**Monument Expedition**: Skill points, skilled courtiers, Prestige. Valid at special buildings/points of interest. Requires Journey perk. Cooldown 3 years. Cost: Gold.

## Examinations

Available to adult rulers with Celestial, Meritocratic, or Meritocratic Khanate government (realm capital only). Guests gain Confucian Education trait or experience; host cannot. Exam Score determines passage chance, based on trait experience, Martial/Stewardship/Learning skills, language knowledge, and prior cheating. Host can increase scores of certain characters per intent.

Cheating has 90% secret chance, 10% caught chance. Caught characters can pay 100 Influence, attempt Intrigue challenge (fail gives +40 Stress), blame a present Friend/Best Friend/Lover (relation becomes Rival), or fail exam (−350 Prestige, −60 Merit, Stress level, permanent reduced Exam Score and restricted choices).

No examination for 6 years pushes Dynastic Cycle toward Tension era each year; 10+ years doubles the effect.

|---|---|---|---|
| Local Examination | Standard | 3 years | Passed Local Examination |
| Capital Examination | Insightful Scholars (Learning boost) | 5 years | Hegemon |
| Capital Examination | Efficient Governance (Stewardship boost) | 5 years | Hegemon in Advancement era |
| Capital Examination | Military Strategists (Martial boost) | 5 years | Hegemon in Expansion era |

## University Visit

Available to adults without tier 5 education trait (20-year cooldown). Valid at universities, holy buildings, China capital, or Constantinople. Requires 200 Piety if barony's faith is Hostile.

Activity Option Books and Notes: Level 2 (150 Gold, 3 Studiousness, +1 random skill), Level 3 (300 Gold, 10 Studiousness, +1 skill, increased Confucian trait experience, Trinket or Book artifact).

Studiousness meter (0-100) determines rewards. Constantinople adds 10, Strengthen Capital adds 10. Rewards scale with Studiousness level: Decent Studies (25: 500 experience), Average (50: 1000), Good (75: 2000), Perfect (100: 3000). Education trait progression improves with higher Studiousness.

## Funeral

Available to rulers with dead relatives (5-year cooldown, any barony). Activity Option Ceremonials affects rewards:

- Level 1: +20 Legitimacy, +200 Piety, −20 Stress
- Level 2: +50 Legitimacy, +250 Piety, −35 Stress
- Level 3: +100 Legitimacy, +350 Piety, −60 Stress

Bonuses from Temple (+10 Legitimacy/Piety), Holy building (+10), Holy site (+10), Great Tower of Silence (+10), Mountains/Desert Mountains + Sky Burials doctrine (+10). Piety adjusted by Mummification (−90%) or Stoic Burial (+25%). Stress adjusted by Stoic Burial (−50%) or Bewailing Burial (+200%).

With Mummification doctrine, choose artifact or 250 Piety. Artifact rarity depends on deceased's traits/title/faith head status; can choose Trinket or Furniture (if Royal Court).

## Camp Revelry

Adventurer activity (3 months, base 15 Gold). Options:

- **Food**: Starting Carousing. Levels 2-3 require Supply Tent, trigger Provisions/Piety/follower events.
- **Drinks**: Guest opinion and Eager Reveler experience. Level 2 requires Supply Tent; Level 3 requires Mobile Brewers upgrade, gives Master of Ceremonies modifier (3 years).

Carousing meter (0-100): Lively (25: −50 Stress), Cheery (50: −75 Stress, +400 Provisions, +50 experience, +1-3 recruit skills), Joyous (75: −100 Stress, +600 Provisions, +100 experience, +1 contract, +1-3 recruit skills), Jubilant (100: −150 Stress, +1200 Provisions, +300 experience, +2 contracts, upgrades camp, +1-3 recruit skills).

Events reward artifacts, friends, Prestige, or Hunter/Hastiluder/Shrewd traits.

## Coronation

One-time, high-interaction activity (Kingdom+ rulers age 12+). In Ceremonial Monarchy realms, only the designated monarch can hold it unless they're the same character. Ritsuryō/Sōryō rulers must be top liege. AI always holds as soon as able. Two variants:

**Coronation**: Realm capital or Holy site (better if both). Crown or Regalia artifact required for some religions (Hinduism/Buddhism/Jainism/Hausa/Finno-Ugric/Slavic). Activity Options at level 2/3: +15/+25 Magnificence. Ceremony (join likelihood, lowest = realm-only), Celebrations (negative event reduction), both level 2+ = +50 Renown; level 3 = +100.

**Anointment**: Head of Faith's location only, requires Faithful devotion, no Excommunication trait. Unlocked by requesting from Head of Faith. Higher rewards. Gives Crowned/Anointed Sovereign law (unless disabled via game rule). Faith gains +4 Fervor.

Magnificence (0-100, five levels):

- Meager (0-24): Stress if Craven/Paranoid/Shy; Crowned in Obscurity modifier (−1 Legitimacy, −2 Court Grandeur); Spurred Nobility if Celebrations lowest (−15% Prestige, −5 opinion, −10 Courtly opinion); can pay/Diplomacy-Learning check for 40 Magnificence
- Modest (25-49): Stress loss if Ambitious/Arrogant/Just; +5 Vassal opinion, +25% Oath Prestige/Legitimacy rewards, +10 Control; Honored by Coronation (Coronation only: +10% Garrison, +10 opinion, −5% Development decline); Crowned in Obscurity if Ceremony lowest; Spurred Nobility if Celebrations lowest
- Respectable (50-74): +10 Vassal opinion, +50% Oath rewards, +50% Prestige/Legitimacy, +20 Control; Impressed by Stately Coronation (Coronation: +1 Fort, +25% Garrison, +20 opinion, −10% declines)
- Grand (75-99): +15 Vassal opinion, +75% rewards, +30 Control; Awed by Wondrous Coronation (Coronation: +2 Forts, +50% Garrison, +40 opinion, −20% declines)
- Resplendent (100): +20 Vassal opinion, +100% Oath rewards, +150% Prestige/Legitimacy, +30 Control; Awed by Wondrous Coronation

Major Supporters (3 max, high-opinion guests) increase Magnificence; Major Detractors (3 max, low-opinion guests) decrease. Factions cannot press during, but decrease Magnificence.

End rewards (second stage): Piety (virtues/sins/devotion/clergy opinion), Prestige (factions/tyranny/traits/legitimacy/guests), Legitimacy (factions/tyranny/Peasant/Populist traits/popular opinion/control/plague/Anointment), Christian/Jewish vassal opinion (Fervor). Crown/Regalia artifact with randomized effects, always +Legitimacy gain. Coronation law level increases (unless disabled).

5 Coronations in one barony grants Traditional Ascension Site (doubled at 10, tripled at 15).

## Blót

Available to faiths with Gruesome Festivals tenet (9-year cooldown, counties with same faith). Duration scales with realm size. Costs 200 Prestige + base cost.

Options:
- **Festival Size**: Increases opinion bonuses. Levels 2-3: +30 Popular opinion (7 years, realm same-faith counties) and opinion choices. Level 3 adds +20 Count+ vassal opinion.
- **Sacrifice Fodder**: Animals (free, 50 Piety, 30 opinion/5 yrs), Majestic animals (scaled Gold, 100 Piety, 30 opinion/5 yrs), Humans (free, 100 Piety, 30 opinion/5 yrs; −50 opinion from non-Gruesome/non-Human-Sacrifice), Majestic humans (scaled Gold, 250 Piety, 30 opinion/5 yrs; −50 from others).

Human sacrifice unlocks Designate Sacrifice on imprisoned characters (kills at activity end). Only one prisoner sacrificed (random if multiple). Head of Faith sacrifice gives +50 Fervor, cheaper holy wars, more likely great holy wars; once per faith.

Ritual Cannibalism tenet gives noble attendees Cannibal trait. Temporal Head of Faith option: gain +50 opinion instead of Festival Size bonuses.

## Chariot Race

High-interaction, 10-year cooldown in realm capital. Emperor+ only, Roman Ceremonies tradition. More successful in Constantinople.

Activity Option Spectacles determines Legitimacy and Popular opinion gains (scales with existing capital opinion). One Honorary Guest for event interaction.

Host gains Influence (if government uses it). Realm capital barony gains +20 Development.

Four teams (Venetoi, Prasinoi, Leukoi, Rhousioi) of 2-3 characters each, sorted by Charioteer trait experience. After guests arrive, betting opens: select team, bet member/whole team, bet placement (first/top 2/top 3), bet amount (50/100/150/200 Gold). Hovering shows team odds and guest bets. Champion Charioteer bet winning gives Influence.

During race, right window shows placements and bets. Champion Charioteer court position (60 Influence) can be appointed.

End: Contestants gain Charioteer trait experience by placement. Winner may receive bow/melee weapon/armor.

## Grand activities

**Grand Tour**: Prestige, Legitimacy, County Control, Popular opinion, Stress loss. Valid vassal realms/realm capital. Requires Adult, 3+ Duke+ vassals, no Administrative government. Cooldown 10 years. Variants: Majesty Tour (Prestige, Renown, vassal opinion), Intimidation Tour (Dread, may force vassals out of factions), Taxation Tour (Gold, no Tribal government).

**Grand Wedding**: Marriage, Legitimacy, Prestige, Opinion, Stress loss, Eager Reveler trait, Accolade glory, Gracious/Magnificent Host modifiers. Valid domain. Requirements: Both adults, Grand Wedding promise. Variants: Grand Wedding (fulfills promise, may give artifacts), Bloody Wedding (requires Accomplished Forger perk, chance to kill spouse's family, 15-year cooldown).

### Grand Tournament

Highly interactive, 10-year cooldown in domain barony. Adult only. Paid with Treasury (if used) or Gold.

Activity Options:
- **Accommodations**: Negative event likelihood. Pay 20 (low), 60, or 180 Gold/Treasury.
- **Prizes**: Prize artifact quality and Gold rewards. Pay 20 (Common), 60 (Common-Masterwork), 180 (Masterwork-Famed + Gold), 360 (Masterwork-Illustrious + Gold), or 720 (Famed-Illustrious + Gold).

Champion appointment (Liege shares rewards); host participating in Melee joins organizer's team.

3 weeks post-tournament: visit areas (Artisan Quarters, Tavern, Temple, Tent Camp, Tourney Grounds, Village) for events.

#### Contests

1-5 contests per tournament. Characters choose participation or observation. Rewards:

- All qualifiers gain Hastiluder trait/experience + positive modifier (5 years)
- 3rd place: small Prestige (larger if Accolade)
- 2nd place: moderate Prestige (larger if Accolade)
- 1st place: large Prestige, artifact, strong modifier (5 years); Accolades give liege moderate Prestige
- 180+ Gold prizes: top 3 get scaled Gold; Accolades give some/all to liege per opinion

Progress to Victory bar shows contest closeness (20+ required to qualify). Host failing but rival succeeding: replace rival (−20 opinion).

## Intents

Activity intents influence events. Most intents have broad availability; special intents require specific conditions (government, trait, trait level, ownership, situation, etc.).

Standard intents: Recreation, Befriend, Murder, Seduce, Worship, Study Hard, Goliardic Lifestyle, Mourn, Altruism, Zealotry, Appease the Populace, Storyteller, Overseer, Survey, Knowledge Hoarding, Curiosity, Justice, Lechery, Recruit, Triumph, Diplomacy, Matchmaking, Mischief, Sow Discord.

Coronation-specific (host-only): Embrace Supporters, Exalt the Crown, Impress Attendees, Weaken Detractors, Advocate Domain, Bear Witness, Disrupt Loyalists, Offer Support, Profess Rights, Seize Advantages.

Hunt variants: Slay Beast (Falconry/Nerge), Succeed (Nerge), Dreadful (Nerge).

Other special: Mystic (Wise Man/Woman trait), Spread Legend (owning/promoting legend), Espouse Legitimacy (third Legitimacy legacy), Increase Influence (Administrative government), Impose Obedience (Nomadic government), Fireside Chat (Roaring Campfire building), Coax to Bloc, Encourage Cohesion, Sideline from Bloc (bloc activities), Assist Examination, Support Entrants, Take Examination, Scout Talent, Favoritism (examination host-only), Increase Movement Power, Gain Disciples, Gain Elder (Mandala/Wanua government).
