Lifestyles represent a character's chosen skill focus and development path. Characters gain lifestyle experience over time and through events, unlocking perks that grant powerful bonuses. Each lifestyle contains 3 focuses and 3 perk trees, each with 9 perks leading to a terminal trait.

## Overview

Characters must have a playable government or be heirs to gain lifestyle experience. Each perk costs 1000 experience to unlock. A character gains approximately one new perk every few years. Perks must be unlocked sequentially (prerequisites enforced, except rare travel events). Characters can reset perks once per lifetime for 100 Stress (affected by Stress Gain modifiers), removing the terminal trait.

**Custom rulers** start with 1 perk point per 3 years of age (first perk at age 18, max 20 points at age 75). Perks match education trait. AI characters always finish current perk tree before starting a new one.

Rulers and adventurers have different perk tree variants. Perks convert when switching between ruler and adventurer governments.

Focus can be changed once every 5 years. Wandering Lifestyle requires Wandering Nobles DLC.

## Focuses

Each focus grants +25 monthly lifestyle experience (improved by education and other factors). Focuses provide bonuses independent of perk trees.

## Perk Trees

### Diplomacy Lifestyle

#### Diplomat
Terminal trait: **Diplomat** (+3 Diplomacy, −20 Days Scheme Phase, enables Call Confederation)

Key perks:
- **Thoughtful:** +100% Gift Opinion Gain
- **Ducal Conquest:** −20% Title Creation Cost, enables Ducal Conquest casus belli, +30 Candidate Score
- **Forced Vassalage:** Enables Forced Vassalization casus belli, +20% Monthly Merit
- **Accomplished Forger:** +75% Fabricate Claim Speed, Buy Artifact Claim interaction
- **Embassies:** −60 Learn Language Phase, +1 Diplomacy per Alliance (max 5)
- **Flexible Truces:** −25% Truce Time, no prestige penalty for breaking

#### August
Terminal trait: **August** (+2 Diplomacy, +1 Martial, +1 Monthly Prestige)

Key perks:
- **Firm Hand:** +0.4% Prestige per Dread, +0.1% Knight Effectiveness per Dread
- **True Ruler:** +0.15 Prestige per Legitimacy Level, +20 Vassalization Acceptance
- **Writing History:** +10% Stress Loss per Fame Level, +25% Legend Spread Chance
- **A Life of Glory:** +100% Fame Impact, +3 Mandala Radiance
- **Dignitas:** +1 Diplomacy per Fame Level, −50% Legitimacy Loss

#### Family Hierarch
Terminal trait: **Patriarch/Matriarch** (+1 Domain Limit, +20% Fertility, +20% Stress Loss)

Key perks:
- **Befriend:** Enables Befriend Scheme, Challenge to Board Game interaction
- **Confidants:** −5% Stress Gain per Friend (max 5)
- **Groomed to Rule:** Children gain +1 to +3 skills, +20 Influence Ward acceptance
- **Friendly Counsel:** Each Friend/Best Friend gives +2 random skills (max 5)
- **Sound Foundations:** Each child gives +1 random skills (max 5)
- **Bound by Blood:** Rescue/Unify House casus belli, +25% Personal Scheme on family
