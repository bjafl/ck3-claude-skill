Modifiers are effects granting bonuses or penalties to dynasties, houses, characters, or counties. Modifiers can be temporary or permanent. Characters can have unlimited modifiers, but only the first 10 display.

## Temporary Modifiers

Temporary modifiers include:
- Event-driven penalties (e.g., "Broken" from torture)
- Status effects (e.g., "Rejected From Marriage Bed" from STDs)
- Activity results (various lifestyle/activity modifiers)
- Scheme outcomes (temporary bonuses/penalties)
- Disease-related modifiers (varies by condition)

Most temporary modifiers last 3-10 years depending on source, with some stacking with permanent effects.
