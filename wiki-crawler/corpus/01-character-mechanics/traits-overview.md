Traits represent personality, abilities, reputation, and physical characteristics that affect attributes, opinions, events, and gameplay mechanics. Most trait effects stack and traits can be inherited, gained through events, decisions, or mental breaks.

## Personality Traits

Personality traits determine how stress is gained from actions conflicting with them and influence AI behavior. Characters rarely have more than 3. Opposite personality traits conflict (cannot coexist) and characters with opposing traits have reduced opinion of each other.

Characters gain personality traits at ages 9, 11, 13 (child player chooses or guardian can pay stress to override). Guardians' traits influence children's results, and cultural traditions affect trait prevalence.

## Education Traits

Acquired at age 16 based on education focus and score (0-100 scale). Levels 1-4 cost 20/40/80, level 5 costs 150 (requires Dynasty "Legitimacy" legacy completion or estate Education Hall).

### Main education traits

**Diplomacy:** Naive Appeaser → Adequate Bargainer → Charismatic Negotiator → Grey Eminence → Virtuoso Arbitrator (+2 to +10 Diplomacy, +10% to +50% Lifestyle Exp)

**Martial:** Misguided Warrior → Tough Soldier → Skilled Tactician → Brilliant Strategist → Exalted Warlord (+2 to +10 Martial, Army bonuses at top level)

**Stewardship:** Indulgent Wastrel → Thrifty Clerk → Fortune Builder → Midas Touched → Golden Sovereign (+2 to +10 Stewardship, +25% Vassal Taxes at level 5)

**Intrigue:** Amateurish Plotter → Flamboyant Trickster → Intricate Webweaver → Elusive Shadow → Conniving Puppetmaster (+2 to +10 Intrigue, +10% Hostile Scheme Potential at level 5)

**Learning:** Conscientious Scribe → Insightful Thinker → Astute Intellectual → Mastermind Philosopher → Erudite Oracle (+2 to +10 Learning, +0.1 Monthly Development at level 5)

**Martial (with Knighthood):** Bumbling Squire → Confident Knight → Formidable Banneret → Famous Champion (+1 to +4 Prowess, separate system unaffected by guardian bonuses)

## Congenital Traits

Appear at birth with very small chance; inherited more often if both parents share the trait or are related.

### Positive congenital

- **Fecund:** +50% Fertility, +5 Years Life Expectancy, +5 Years Fertility
- **Pure-blooded:** +10% Fertility, +0.25 Health, −50% Inbreeding Chance
- **Giant:** +6 Prowess, −0.25 Health (appearance change)
- **Albino:** +15 Natural Dread, −10 Opinion, white hair
- **Strong:** +4 Prowess, +0.5 Health
- **Shrewd:** +2 all skills, +50 Rationality (AI)

### Leveled congenital (−3 to +3)

- **Appearance:** Hideous/Ugly/Homely ↔ Comely/Pretty-Handsome/Beautiful (±1-3 Diplomacy, ±10-30% Fertility)
- **Intelligence:** Imbecile/Stupid/Slow ↔ Quick/Intelligent/Genius (±8 skills, ±30% Lifestyle Exp)
- **Physique:** Feeble/Frail/Delicate ↔ Hale/Robust/Herculean-Amazonian (±6-8 Prowess, ±1 Health)

### Negative congenital

- **Inbred:** −5 all skills, −2 Prowess, −50% Fertility, −30 Opinion
- **Dwarf:** −4 Prowess, −20 Opinion
- **Club-footed:** −2 Prowess, −10 Opinion
- **Wheezing:** −0.15 Health, 4× disease spread chance
- **Scaly:** −20% Fertility, +10 Natural Dread

## Physical & Health Traits

### Permanent physical traits

- **One-Eyed:** +1 Learning, −2 Prowess, +10 Natural Dread
- **One-Legged:** +1 Learning, −4 Prowess, −10 Opinion
- **Eunuch:** −10 Attraction, cannot marry/have children, cannot inherit
- **Blind:** −6 Martial, −10 Martial Prowess

### Health traits (temporary)

**Wounds (recovery 10-40% chance per 3 months):**
- Wounded: −1 Martial/Intrigue, −2 Prowess, −10 Natural Dread
- Severely Injured: −2 Martial/Intrigue, −4 Prowess, −15 Natural Dread, −25% Fertility
- Brutally Mauled: −4 Martial/Intrigue, −8 Prowess, −20 Natural Dread

**Diseases (curable except noted):**
- Bubonic Plague: −7 Health (−2 Elderly), −50% stat, permanent immunity if survive
- Smallpox: −4.5 Health (−1.5 Elderly), −25% stats, permanent immunity
- Pneumonia, Typhus, Measles: −3 to −4.5 Health, −50% stats
- Great Pox: −1 Health, −20% Fertility (STD, congenital if exposed)

**Old age traits (levels 0-4, slow at max level):**
- **Infirm:** −1-2 Diplomacy/Martial/Stewardship, −20% Prowess
- **Clouded Eyes:** −1 Martial, −2 Prowess, gain Blind at level 4
- **Fragile Bones:** −10% Prowess, −3 Advantage, −3 Years Life Expectancy
- **Withering Mind:** −2 Learning, −25% all skills (level 2+), gain Incapable at level 4
- **Faltering Heart:** −1 Prowess, +20% Stress Gain, death at level 5

**Incapable:** Most severe. Cannot have children, declare war, scheme, do activities, be councilor/knight. All skills = 0. Regency forced. Triggers abdication. −25 Natural Dread, +100% Dread Loss, Severe Health Penalty.

## Infamy & Decision Traits

### Infamy (often hidden if forbidden by faith)

- **Excommunicated:** −50% Piety, −50 Zealot Opinion, −50% Piety gain (curable)
- **Murderer:** −15 General Opinion
- **Witch:** −1 Diplomacy, +1 Intrigue, +1 Learning
- **Cannibal:** +2 Prowess, +15% Stress Loss, +20 Natural Dread
- **Kinslayer/Dynastic Kinslayer:** −5-10 Family Opinion, −5 Dynasty Opinion
- **Adulterer/Fornicator:** +10 Same Trait Opinion
- **Incestuous/Sodomite:** Faith-gated, opinion-based

### Major decision traits

- **Conqueror:** −75% Men-at-Arms Cost, −75% Title Creation Cost, free casus belli, +5 Taxes/Legitimacy, regular bonuses (benefits fade if subjugated, at peace 13+ years, or in debt 5+ years)
- **Greatest of Khans:** +2 Diplomacy/Martial, +2 Prowess, +50 Natural Dread, −50% Dread Decay, +100% Cavalry bonuses, +100% Herd Gain (requires war victory in Khans government)
- **Holy Monarch:** +3 Martial, +2 Prowess, +5 Faith Hostility Advantage, −20% Retreat, +35 Popular Opinion
- **Augustus:** +0.5 Prestige, +1.5 Influence, +10 Vassal Opinion (restore Roman Empire)

## Lifestyle Traits

Terminal traits from finishing lifestyle trees. Each grants +2-5 skill, unique effects, and +50 customization cost.

- **Diplomat:** +3 Diplomacy, −20 Days Scheme Phase Length, Call Confederation
- **Strategist:** +3 Martial, +25% Enemy Fatalities, cross rivers/straits without penalty
- **Administrator:** +3 Stewardship, +10 Plague Resistance
- **Scholar:** +3 Learning, +10% Scheme Potential, +15% Development Growth
- **Schemer:** +5 Intrigue, −20 Days Hostile Scheme Phase
- **Gallant:** +2 Martial, +4 Prowess, +20 Attraction Opinion
- **Torturer:** +4 Prowess, +50% Dread Gain, +10 Levy Size
- **Whole of Body:** +20% Fertility, +0.5 Health, −20% Stress Gain

| Inappetetic | −1 Diplomacy, −3 Prowess | Shun Food |

## Dynasty Traits

Hereditary dynasty-wide traits grantable by Dynasty Head.

### Descendant traits (inherited from founders)

- **Sayyid:** +5 Muslim Opinion, +15% Legend Seed yearly (Muhammad descendant)
- **Divine Blood:** +5 Same Faith Opinion, +25% Conversion speed (from Paragon/Savior parent)
- **Consecrated Blood:** +10 Zealot Opinion (from Paragon parent with piety rank 3+)
- **Golden Lineage:** +10% Dread Gain, +200 Vassal Limit, +20% Herd Capacity (Khans exclusive, Greatest of Khans requirement)

### Status dynasty traits

- **Bastard:** −1 Diplomacy, −15 Dynasty Opinion, cannot inherit/claim (Faith-gated "No Bastards")
- **Born in the Purple:** +0.1 Prestige, +0.75 Influence, −25% Short Reign Penalty (born in Constantinople or Byzantine/Roman title parent)
- **Legitimized Bastard:** −1 Diplomacy, −10 Dynasty Opinion, −5% Legitimacy (still −1 Diplomacy)
- **Twin:** +15 Twin Sibling Opinion

| Pensive | Stewardship +1, Learning +1 | Intrigue (−1) |

## Commander Traits

Combat traits for leading armies. Gain experience through battles. 4 levels: +100% bonus (L1), +200% (L2), +300% + 1 Advantage (L3), +400% + 2 Advantage + 1 Martial (L4).

Examples: Aggressive Attacker (+10% enemy fatalities), Defender (−10% friendly casualties), Flexible Leader (−20% enemy defensive advantage), Military Engineer (−10% siege phase time), Logistician (+40% supply duration), Winter Soldier (cold terrain bonuses).

## Leveled Traits

Gain experience through activities/events, progressively improve.

- **Blademaster:** +3-6 Prowess (level 1-2)
- **Hunter:** Path Venator: +1-2 Learning, +1-2 Prowess, +0.1-0.25 Health
- **Traveler:** Path Wanderer or Seasoned: +Diplomacy/Stewardship, +1 Prowess, travel bonuses
- **Governor:** +1-2 multiple skills per level (Celestial/Meritocratic only)

## Commander Traits (Special)

**Commander trait paths** teach wards via guardianship (success based on Opinion/Martial/Prowess). One trait per 5 years.

| Trait | Base effect | Scaling |
|---|---|---|
| Aggressive Attacker | +10% enemy fatal casualties | ×level |
| Defender | −10% friendly fatal casualties | ×level |
| Logistician | +40% supply duration, +4 Travel Safety | ×level |
| Military Engineer | −10% siege phase time | ×level (fort level bonus) |
| Desert Warrior | Negate supply penalty in desert, +2 advantage | +2 advantage per level |
| Jungle Stalker | −20% attrition in jungle | +per level bonuses |

## Faith Traits

Available only to specific faiths.

- **Hajji/Hajjah:** +5 Travel Speed/Safety, +5 Muslim Opinion (completed Hajj or realm capital Mecca)
- **Crusader/Mujahid:** +2 Martial, +3 Prowess, +15 Clergy Opinion (participated in holy war)
- **Holy Monarch:** +3 Martial, +2 Prowess, +5 Faith Hostility Advantage (Great Holy War beneficiary)
- **Berserker:** +2 Martial, +5 Prowess, +15 Natural Dread (Warmonger tenet required)
- **Devoted (Monasticism):** +0.3 Piety, cannot marry/have children/inherit
- **Order Member:** +1 Martial, +4 Prowess, cannot marry/have children/inherit

## Court Type Traits

Require 5+ years courtier service; grandeur level 5 (L1) or 8 (L2). Cannot select at creation.

|---|---|---|---|
| Diplomatic Courtier | +1 Diplomacy | Valued Diplomatic | +2 Diplomacy, +0.3 Prestige |
| Warlike Courtier | +1 Martial, +1 Prowess | Valued Warlike | +2 Martial, +2 Prowess, −5% MAA Maintenance |
| Administrative Courtier | +1 Stewardship | Valued Administrative | +2 Stewardship, +0.2 Monthly Control |
| Intrigue Courtier | +1 Intrigue | Valued Intrigue | +2 Intrigue, +10 Natural Dread |
| Scholarly Courtier | +1 Learning | Valued Scholarly | +2 Learning, +20% Development Growth |

## Other Notable Traits

- **Immortal:** No aging, fertility unaffected, no Incapable/old age traits, immune to random harm. Costs 10000 points (invalidates achievements).
- **Loyal/Disloyal:** ±1 Diplomacy/Intrigue, ±15 Liege Opinion, faction-related (event traits)
- **Veteran Adventurer:** +1 Martial/Prowess per Fame Level, −50% MAA Cost
- **Populist Leader:** −50% Army Gold Maintenance, +15 Popular Opinion, −30 Courtly Opinion
