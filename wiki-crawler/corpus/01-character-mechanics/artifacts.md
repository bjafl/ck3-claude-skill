Artifacts are unique objects in the world providing bonuses to character users and are always inherited by the primary heir. Inventory artifacts are worn (crown, journal, weapon, armor, regalia, 4 trinkets; 9 max). Court artifacts are displayed in Royal Court (3 large/3 small wall ornaments, 2 furniture, 2 books, 4 pedestals, 1 throne; 15 max).

Gifting artifacts grants +20 Opinion if usable by recipient, +5 if not. Unworn inventory artifacts and court artifacts can be damaged or lost during realm capital sieges. Lost artifacts give the previous owner a claim, allowing artifact demand (requires superior military strength) or Artifact Claim War casus belli or duel challenge. Accomplished Forger rulers can buy artifact claims at costs per rarity.

Uncrowned law rulers cannot use Regalia (Hinduism/Buddhism/Jainism/Hausa/Finno-Ugric/Slavic) or Crown (others) artifacts, though owning them is necessary for Coronation activity options.

## Rarity

All artifacts have Rarity determining Durability, repair cost, and destruction gold value.

| Rarity | Durability | Repair Cost (80-100%) | Repair Cost (50-79%) | Repair Cost (20-49%) | Repair Cost (5-19%) | Repair Cost (0-4%) | Gold if Destroyed | Claim Cost | Destroyable |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 60 years | 6 | 13 | 26 | 39 | 65 | 40 | 500 | Yes |
| Masterwork | 75 years | 12 | 25 | 50 | 75 | 125 | 80 | 1000 | Yes |
| Famed | 100 years | 25 | 50 | 100 | 150 | 250 | 150 | 1500 | Requires Aniconism/Extinction of Dharma tenet |
| Illustrious | 150 years | 62 | 125 | 250 | 375 | 625 | 250 | 2000 | Requires Aniconism/Extinction of Dharma tenet |

Durability decreases 1 year per year (except Royal Court displayed items). Non-historical inherited artifacts by non-close family members lose Durability. Employ an Antiquarian to reduce durability loss and enable reforging. Reforging costs Gold, restores Durability, and allows conversion to Pedestal Item (changing effects). Adventurers cannot reforge but can repair. AI rulers don't war over Common artifacts.

## Creating artifacts

**Commission Artifact** (Rulers, 100 Gold): Choose artifact type. Events during creation offer opportunities to increase rarity.

**City holdings** (Adventurers): Purchase artifacts with Gold.

**Knight of the Swan** decision: Obtain Golden Coat of the Swan armor.

**Commission Epic** decision: Create Book (has Royal Court) or Journal (no Royal Court).

**Chess**: Win → 5% chance Common Lucky Chess/Go Board trinket (25% if playing for county). Option to destroy (−10 Stress) or improve (moderate Gold).

**Grand Tournaments** (requires Tours & Tournaments): Contest wins reward artifacts per Prizes option level. Trinket/pedestal items always possible; weapon/armor/bow per contest type.

**All Under Heaven features**:
- Tributaries pay tribute at sufficient Imperial Grace → Fine Seal of Investiture regalia
- Tributaries with Mandala government → Create Bunga Mas decision (pedestal item)

**Royal Court features**:
- Pet death: Pay 10-20 Gold → small wall ornament
- University Visit activity: Book artifact
- Noble opponent battle win: Chance Warbanner large wall ornament (or destroy for 75 Prestige)
- Execute prisoner with Arbitrary/Deceitful/Callous/Sadistic/Vengeful trait: 25% Skull pedestal item (always if Nemesis); −30 opinion without Executioner
- House Banner large wall ornament automatically; last Kingdom+ title taker gets it (−Court Grandeur bonus if different house)
- Independent Celestial/Meritocratic/Meritocratic Khanate government or Chrysanthemum Throne owner: Imperial Seal pedestal item
- Nemesis death with Callous/Sadistic/Vengeful trait (requires Friends & Foes): Skull Goblet pedestal item (not if Sky Burials faith)

### Hunt trophies

Hunt activities grant trinket artifacts (wall ornaments if Royal Court and not Falconry). Give monthly Prestige; rarity-dependent additional effects.

- Foxes, gazelles, hares, roes, non-exotic birds: Common (100%)
- Hyenas, lynxes, wolves, exotic birds: Common (97%), Masterwork (3%)
- Antelopes, aurochs, bisons, reindeers, stags: Common (50%), Masterwork (50%)
- Bears, leopards, lions, tigers: Masterwork (85%), Famed (15%)
- Boars, elks, harts: Masterwork (33%), Famed (67%)
- Legendary variant: Rarity doubled
- Rare events during activity: Rarity increase possible

### Inspirations

Landless characters gain Inspirations allowing artifact creation. Sponsor with Gold (cost scales with inspired character's skills). Once financed, they create artifact with effects per traits. Rarity based on skill averages plus culture/building/event modifiers.

Inspirations appear randomly in royal courts (higher Grandeur = higher chance) or from Adventurer followers with Inspiration is Everywhere perk.

| Inspiration | Artifact Type | Primary Skill | Skill Average |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adventure | Sponsor's choice | Diplomacy, Learning, Prowess | (1.5×Diplomacy + 1.5×Learning + 3×Prowess) / 4 |
| Alchemy | Pedestal Item | Learning | |
| Armor | Armor, Large/Small Wall Ornament | Martial, Prowess | (Martial + 2×Prowess) / 2 |
| Artisan | Furniture, Pedestal, Throne, Trinket | Stewardship | |
| Book | Book, Journal | Learning | |
| Metalsmith | Crown, Regalia, Trinket, Pedestal | Diplomacy, Stewardship, Prowess | (Diplomacy + 2×Stewardship + 2×Prowess) / 4 |
| Weapon | Weapon | Martial, Prowess | (Martial + 2×Prowess) / 2 |
| Weave | Large Wall Ornament | Diplomacy | |

Completing Adventure inspiration grants Veteran Adventurer trait.

## Historical artifacts

Specific pre-existing artifacts tied to historical realms, cultures, and events:

**Byzantine artifacts**: Icons, Swords, Helms, and other items tied to Byzantine history.

**Holy Roman artifacts**: Imperial regalia and religious items.

**Shia artifacts**: Religious reliquaries and items tied to Shia Islam.

**Pagan Kingdom artifacts**: Items from pagan rulers and cultures.

**Hegemony of China artifacts**: Imperial seals, celestial regalia tied to Mandala/Celestial government.

**Swords of El Cid**: Legendary weapons tied to Iberian struggle events.

**Bells of Santiago**: Religious items tied to Santiago de Compostela.

**The Double-Moon Tome**: Unique book artifact.

**Adventure artifacts**: Items obtained during adventurer activities and travels.

**Coronation artifacts**: Crown or Regalia artifacts received upon coronation completion.

**Easter egg artifacts**: Rare secret artifacts hidden in specific scenarios.

## Dynasty banners

All house heads have claims on their dynasty's House Banner (large wall ornament). Banners grant +3 Prestige per house member (living, landed). Bonuses lost if displayed by non-house character; artifact remains but only if destroyed.

## Mummification artifacts

Rulers with Mummification funeral doctrine can choose artifact or 250 Piety upon funeral completion. Artifact rarity depends on deceased's traits (virtues/sins valued), title rank, and faith head status. Can choose Trinket or Furniture (if Royal Court).

## Paizas

Paizas are unique regalia artifacts obtained from Khans or created via special events. Grant bonuses to Diplomacy and Prestige when held by vassals of Khan rulers.

## Talismans

Certain artifacts function as talismans providing spiritual/magical bonuses. _Specific mechanics not covered on the wiki._

## Cranial trophies

Certain artifact types obtained from executing nemeses or through Blót sacrifices grant bonuses to Dread and Prestige when held by cruel rulers.

## Improving artifacts

Artifacts can be improved by spending Gold while an Antiquarian is employed. This restores Durability and allows reforging to change artifact type (e.g., Pedestal Item) and effects. Some artifacts gain additional modifiers from completing special activities or challenges.

### Courtier improves artifact

Court members with high Stewardship or Learning can sometimes spontaneously improve artifacts in your collection, restoring Durability without cost or converting them to higher rarity through their efforts.

## Artifact modifiers

Each artifact type carries inherent modifiers (bonuses/penalties) determined by rarity, creation method, and improvements. Modifiers include:

- Monthly Prestige bonuses
- Skill bonuses (Martial, Diplomacy, Learning, Stewardship, Intrigue)
- Opinion modifiers with specific character types
- Lifestyle experience modifiers
- Combat effectiveness bonuses
- Stress reduction
- Monthly Piety or Gold bonuses
- Legitimacy or Succession-related bonuses
- Culture or Religion-specific modifiers

Complex artifacts (from Coronations, Inspirations, or legendary hunts) gain +3 additional random modifiers per reforging, significantly increasing their power.
