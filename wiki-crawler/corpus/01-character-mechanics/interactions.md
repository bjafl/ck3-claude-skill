Interactions are actions taken with or against other characters, including marriage arrangement, scheme initiation, and war declaration. Most are accessed via right-clicking a character and typically require diplomatic range.

## Diplomacy actions

### War-related actions

**Declare War**: Initiates conflict. Requirements: not in debt, no raised armies (exception: Nomadic rulers may declare while armies raised), not traveling, not in activity, target lacks strong hook, certain governments restrict by title rank.

**Call to War**: War leader requests ally join ongoing war. Scaling prestige cost for offensive wars; cannot call to Great Holy Wars if wrong faith.

**Call House Member To War**: Free call to landed house members (not your liege). House Head only.

**Call Dynasty Member To War**: Costs 75 Renown. Dynasty Head only.

**Offer to Join War**: Propose joining ally/liege/faith defender/peasant-fighting vassal's war. Cannot use if you're at war with their liege. Holy war joins require same faith or non-hostile religion within distance. May progress sender/recipient to friendship; Craven gain 40 Stress, Shy gain 20.

**Purchase Truce**: Requires Defensive Negotiations perk.

### Forming Alliances

**Negotiate Alliance**: Create alliance with rulers having family ties (parent/child, sibling, cousin, spouse, spouse's extended family). Both must be at peace and not imprisoned.

**Propose Alliance**: Requires Diplomatic lifestyle Defensive Negotiations perk. Works with any ruler meeting: at peace, not imprisoned, not vassal/liege, at least Count rank, ineligible for Negotiate Alliance.

### Marriages & Concubines

**Arrange marriage/betrothal**: Propose marriage between characters. Requirements: opposite genders, not recently divorced, consanguinity doctrine permits. Both lieges must approve (self-approval if wandering).

**Break betrothal**: End agreed betrothal.

**Propose Concubinage**: Offer concubine relationship (no inheritance rights for children).

**End Betrothal**: Cancel engagement.

**End Concubinage**: Terminate concubine relationship.

### Other Diplomacy

**Legitimize Bastard**: Ruler with appropriate doctrine can legitimize bastard child. Costs 100 Prestige. Bastard must be in realm. Liege approval required if character is vassal.

**Demand Payment**: Certain perks enable demanding gold from specific character types. Cost varies.

**Buy Claim**: Accomplished Forger perk allows buying artifact claims (cost per rarity).

**Request Claim**: Head of Faith with Spiritual Head doctrine can grant claims on targets. Costs 100 Piety per claim.

## Personal actions

**Send Gift**: Give artifact to character (±20 opinion if usable). Can reduce stress or improve relationships.

**Sway**: Spend Influence (some governments) or Prestige to improve opinion with target. Scales with state religion and other factors.

**Befriend**: Court position holder or perk-enabled character can initiate friendship scheme. Progresses through opinion events.

**Seduce**: Initiate seduction scheme (opposite gender, not close family unless faith permits). Success creates lover relationship.

**Romance**: Develop romantic relationship with lover/soulmate. Progresses through events.

**Elope**: Escape with romantic partner. Gains elopement trait, breaks betrothal.

**Educate Child**: Teach character your ward/child traits matching your personality. Costs Gold per month.

**Offer Ward**: Propose becoming child's guardian. Opinion-modifying.

**Offer Guardianship**: Offer to educate child.

**Influence Ward's Personality**: Spend Prestige/Influence to steer ward's learning choices.

**Ward's Martial Drill**: Military instruction for ward (costs Gold, requires martial focus).

**Host as Honored Guest**: Invite character to court temporarily. Improves opinion.

**Send Poem**: Share poetry (Poet job holder can send). Improves opinion.

**Challenge to Boardgame**: Initiate chess match for opinion/artifact bonuses.

**Make Shieldmaiden**: Grant shieldmaiden status to female character (Norse cultures, +10 Martial).

**Abandon Hook**: Forgiving-trait holders abandon hooks for stress reduction (−20 Weak, −42 Strong). Gives +50 opinion; Strong reusable every 5 years.

## Hostile actions

**Disinherit**: Give Disinherited trait and −75 Opinion. Reversible for 75 Renown. Cannot use on Strong Hook holders or non-vassals.

**Blackmail**: Over shunned secret (Weak Hook) or criminal secret (Strong Hook). Requires target doesn't know you share secret.

**Murder**: Initiate assassination scheme against target.

**Abduct**: Kidnap character. Requires close proximity or involvement in your court/realm.

**Claim Throne**: Press claim on target's primary title via interaction (quick claim).

**Fabricate Hook**: Start scheme to create false hook on target (10-year duration).

**Dismiss Hook**: Arbitrary rulers dismiss held hooks (costs opinion/prestige/tyranny).

**Excommunicate**: If Head of Faith, excommunicate target. High opinion/piety cost.

**Request Excommunication**: Ask Head of Faith to excommunicate target. Costs Piety (scales with hostile faith relations).

**Challenge Rival to Fight**: Duel rival (friend/best friend/lover to start). Weapon of choice, scaling rewards.

**Punish Criminal**: Execute/imprison discovered criminal (costs opinion/prestige/tyranny).

**Release Your Anger**: Humiliate nearby character (costs opinion/prestige/stress, scales with traits).

**Trial-by-Combat**: Invoke duel to resolve disputes (contract/vassal disputes, etc.).

## Appointment Actions

**Influence Candidacy**: Administrative realm governors spend Influence on candidate support (requires approval).

**Ask to Influence Candidacy**: Request character influence candidacy on your behalf.

**Expand Power Base**: Spend Influence/Prestige to gain favor in administrative realm.

**Slander**: Diplomatic action to damage target's reputation (costs Prestige/Influence).

**Promote**: Council member advances through ranks (advisor to chancellor, etc.).

**Retire Governor**: Remove Administrative realm governor from office.

**Request Retirement**: Ask governor to step down.

**Force Retirement**: Force governor out with hooks/schemes.

## Vassal actions

**Grant Landed Title**: Give vassal county/duchy/kingdom title. Creates vassal relations; opinion effects.

**Revoke Title**: Remove vassal's title (tyranny cost unless heir disputes resolved).

**Demand Title**: Forced title demand (Strong Hook only).

**Give Vassal Directive**: Command vassal action (wars, decisions, etc.). Not all directives apply to all vassals.

**Demand Religious Conversion**: Require vassal convert to your faith. Strong Hook forces; weak Hook bonus.

**Imprison**: Detain character. Must be vassal/courtier. Strong Hook forces.

**Demand Hostage**: Request hostage. Strong Hook forces (40 Acceptance weak).

**Recall Hostage**: Bring held hostage home. Strong Hook forces.

**Offer Hostage**: Provide hostage to character. Strong Hook forced.

**Execute Prisoner**: Kill imprisoned character (opinion costs, Prestige gains scale with traits/type).

**Ransom Prisoner**: Release prisoner for Gold.

**Release Prisoner**: Free captured character (opinion/prestige effects).

## Scheme actions

**Invite to Scheme**: Recruit character into active scheme. Strong Hook forces; weak Hook +100 acceptance.

**Recruit to Court**: Bring landless character into court. Weak Hook forced; Strong Hook forced.

## Court position actions

**Demand Council Position**: Force liege grant specific council position (Strong Hook or weak Hook forced). Cannot be removed 25 years.

**Request Court Position**: Ask liege for specific position. Weak Hook +100 acceptance; Strong Hook forced. No removal protection.

**Appoint**: Liege appoints character to court position.

**Dismiss**: Remove from court position (subject to severance/opinion penalties).

## Contract actions (requires Roads to Power)

**Offer Contract**: Propose contract (military, economic, espionage variants).

**Accept Contract**: Adventurer accepts offered contract.

**Complete Contract**: Finish contract objectives for rewards.

## Religious actions

**Request Claim from Head of Faith**: Head with Spiritual Head doctrine grants claims (100 Piety per claim).

**Ask for Gold**: Head of Faith with Spiritual Head doctrine gives Gold (forced).

**Demand Clergy Support**: Administrative realm, Head of Faith, forced cooperation.

**Request Clergy Support**: Ask for religious support (Administrative realm only).

**Share Religious Secrets**: Same-house members (Coterie legacy 4+) share faith-based knowledge.

## Government-specific actions

**Demand Administrative Government**: Force subject accept Administrative government (Strong Hook only).

**Modify Theme Administration**: Change Administrative realm theme (Strong Hook on top liege, forced).

**Modify Vassal Contract**: Change contract terms (Weak/Strong Hook force; no tyranny). Cannot re-modify in vassal's lifetime.

**Swing Scales of Power**: In Diarchy, swing 10 points toward your preference (Weak/Strong Hook).

**Ask to Promote Legend**: Request legend promotion from characters with legend knowledge.

**Declare Me Regent**: Force character declare you as future regent (both Weak/Strong Hook forced).

**Declare Me Co-Emperor**: Propose co-emperor status (Roman Ceremonies tradition; Weak +50 acceptance, Strong forced).

## Relations actions

**Befriend**: Start friendship scheme/progression.

**Make Best Friends**: Elevate friendship to best friend (requires base friendship).

**Make Lovers**: Transition friendship to romance (opposite gender, faith-permitted).

**Make Soulmates**: Upgrade lovers to soulmate commitment.

**Become Rivals**: Initiate rivalry (opinion/trait effects).

**Become Nemeses**: Escalate to nemesis (severe opinion penalty, challenge rights).

**Acknowledge Bastard**: Publicly acknowledge bastard child (prestige gain, legitimacy increase).
