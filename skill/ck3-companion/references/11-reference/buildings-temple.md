Reference data for Temple buildings, extracted mechanically from the wiki (no LLM compression --
see HANDOFF.md §5.5). Prose below is reproduced close to verbatim; tables are exact.

Temple buildings can only be constructed in Temple and Temple Citadel holdings. All of them take 3 years to build or upgrade.

## Breweries

Breweries buildings can only be constructed if the country's faith is Catholicism or has the Ritual Celebrations tenet. Having any Breweries building will unlock the Court Brewmaster court position.

Brewery buildings can also be constructed in City holdings if their culture has the Brewing Culture tradition added to it.

| Building | Monthly Tax | County effects | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brewing House | +0.25 | +2% Development Growth +0.05 Monthly Control +5 Popular Opinion | The heart of any brewery, where the brewing process begins. Here, the ingredients are mixed, boiled, and prepared for fermentation. |
| Storage Cellar | +0.4 | +4% Development Growth +0.1 Monthly Control +10 Popular Opinion | A cool, dark place where brewed beverages are stored to age, ensuring they develop the desired flavors and qualities. |
| Brewing Vats | +0.55 | +6% Development Growth +0.15 Monthly Control +15 Popular Opinion | Large containers where the wort is boiled and herbs or hops are added, a critical step in the brewing process to impart bitterness and aroma. |
| Grain Barn | +0.7 | +8% Development Growth +0.2 Monthly Control +20 Popular Opinion | A barn for keeping large quantities of grain dry and safe from pests, ensuring a steady supply of ingredients for brewing. |
| Well House | +0.85 | +10% Development Growth +0.25 Monthly Control +25 Popular Opinion | A well made to ensure a clean and reliable source of water, which is essential for high-quality brews. |
| Barrel Room | +1 | +12% Development Growth +0.3 Monthly Control +30 Popular Opinion | An area where the fermented brew is carefully stored in barrels, properly sealed and ready for distribution. |
| Kegging Room | +1.15 | +14% Development Growth +0.35 Monthly Control +35 Popular Opinion | A dedicated space for filling kegs. These are often used for larger quantities and are favored by inns and taverns. |
| Taster's Hall | +1.3 | +16% Development Growth +0.4 Monthly Control +40 Popular Opinion | A hall where brewers and guests can sample the different brews, providing an opportunity to enjoy the latest brews. |
