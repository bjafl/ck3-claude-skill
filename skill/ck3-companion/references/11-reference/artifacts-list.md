Catalog of CK3's named/historical artifacts and other rarity-templated artifact categories, with their exact effects and requirements. See [Artifacts](../01-character-mechanics/artifacts.md) for how artifacts are created, worn/displayed, gifted, lost, and for the rarity/durability/repair system. Unless noted, "Court Grandeur" bonuses only apply while the artifact is displayed in a Royal Court (requires Royal Court DLC).

## Commissioned icons

The Commission Icon decision lets any character create a Trinket icon: landed rulers get Masterwork rarity (+0.3 or +0.4 Monthly Piety); landless rulers have a small chance at Common rarity (+0.1 or +0.2 Monthly Piety). Rulers with a Royal Court can instead create a Large or Small Wall Ornament icon with two of: Monthly Piety, Temple holding/building construction cost reduction, Zealot vassal tax contribution, Zealot vassal opinion, Clergy opinion.

## Historical artifacts — general notes

Historical artifacts mostly have fixed effects. If lost, or if the artifact's required title doesn't exist at the chosen start date, they can be found randomly through Adventure inspirations instead. A character who obtains a historical artifact but lacks the requirements to use it only gets its Court Grandeur bonus. Artifacts requiring a specific religion can also be used by a faith with the matching syncretism tenet.

## Story and struggle artifacts

### Swords of El Cid

Two artifacts, only obtainable after 1066 and only one of each can ever exist: created via Adventure inspiration as a Pedestal Item (Tizona, if made this way, can then only be used by Iberian-heritage cultures), or created by Rodrigo Díaz winning battles during the active "The Song of El Cid" story (requires Roads to Power), where he can choose to keep the sword or send it to Sancho II.

| Artifact | Effects | Adventure region | Rodrigo Díaz requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tizona | +5% Monthly Renown; +0.5 Monthly Prestige; -5% Men-at-Arms Maintenance; +10% Light Cavalry Toughness; +3 Court Grandeur | Asia Minor, Middle East | 10% chance on winning a battle (guaranteed if enemy commander is King of Valencia) |
| Colada | +5% Monthly Renown; +0.5 Monthly Prestige; +10% Light Cavalry Pursuit; +10% Light Cavalry Screen; +3 Court Grandeur | Southern Europe, Western Europe | Won a battle where the enemy commander owned Barcelona |

### Bells of Santiago

Trinket artifact usable only by characters Involved or Interloper in the Iberian Struggle; a holy relic, so the opposite religion gets a casus belli to recover it. If a character whose faith can't use the version they hold (but is Christian/Muslim, or has the matching syncretism tenet) obtains it, it is melted into their own religion's version. After 4+ meltings, its description tracks the melt count.

|---|---|---|---|
| Bells of Santiago | +0.1 Monthly Piety per Powerful Vassal of Council; +0.5 Monthly Renown; +10 Same Faith Opinion; +10 Christian Opinion; -10 Muslim Opinion; +3 Court Grandeur | Christianity | Count Hermenexildo Gutierrez of Santiago |
| Former Bells of Santiago | +0.1 Monthly Piety per Powerful Vassal of Council; Medium Disease Resistance Health boost; +10 Muslim Opinion; -10 Christian Opinion; +3 Court Grandeur | Islam | Emir Yahya ibn-al-Zafir of Tulaytulah (1066 start) / Prince Ali ibn al-Mu'min of Qurtubah (1178 start) |

### The Double-Moon Tome

Journal historical artifact starting with Wallada bint al-Mustakfi in the 1066 start. Base effects: +5% Monthly Diplomacy Lifestyle Experience, +5% Monthly Learning Lifestyle Experience, -5 Days Romance Phase Length, -5 Days Seduce Phase Length, +2 Attraction Opinion.

It can be upgraded via the Write Muwashshah Poem decision (choosing Keep Poem) or through The Violet Poet story events. Each upgrade adds one of the following modifiers (description updates each time):

+1 Diplomacy per Level of Fame; +1 Diplomacy; +1 Stewardship; +2 Prowess; +0.03 Monthly Prestige; +5% Knight Effectiveness; -2% Mercenary Hire Cost; -8 Days Learn Language Phase Length; +4 General Opinion; +3 Clergy Opinion; +2 Dynasty Opinion; +2 Independent Ruler Opinion; Small Health Boost.

## Coronation artifacts

Every independent starting King-tier-or-above ruler, except the Byzantine, Holy Roman, and Japanese emperors, begins with a coronation artifact: Famed rarity if Emperor-tier or realm size ≥60, otherwise Masterwork. Regardless of rarity it always grants +8% Legitimacy Gain, +0.02 Monthly Prestige, and one random effect. It is a Regalia if the character's religion is Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, Hausa, Finno-Ugric, or Slavic; otherwise a Crown. With Royal Court installed, the first time a ruler raises their title rank to King or above, a courtier (the Antiquarian if employed) gains an inspiration to create one.

### Paizas

Trinket artifacts unlocked by the Establish Paiza System decision. The Gurkhan receives one automatically and can grant more to Nomadic-government subjects for 25 Gold + 350 Prestige; Nomadic rulers without one can also request one. Receiving a Paiza grants Legitimacy: 20 (Count), 100 (Duke), 200 (King), 300 (Emperor). Quality depends on the issuing Gurkhan's title rank and Dominance law. While the issuer remains Gurkhan, the Paiza gives +5 Advantage and -50 Danger in Steppe counties, plus access to the Abuse Gurkhan's Authority decision and the Leverage Khan Authority interaction (usable against Nomadic-government characters; only the holder having Nomadic government can use these). If the issuer stops being Gurkhan, all their Paizas lose these effects and give only +0.05 Monthly Prestige until the new Gurkhan re-establishes the system.
