Temple Citadel buildings can only be constructed in Temple Citadel holdings. All of them take 3 years to build or upgrade.

## Capital Temple Complex

The Capital Temple Complex is a building chain available to rulers with Mandala government. The buildings can only be constructed or upgraded via great projects and will be disabled if the owner does not have Mandala government or the county is not the realm capital. These buildings have their own dedicated building slot separate from regular, duchy and special buildings.
