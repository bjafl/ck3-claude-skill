Reference data for County buildings, extracted mechanically from the wiki (no LLM compression --
see HANDOFF.md §5.5). Prose below is reproduced close to verbatim; tables are exact.

County buildings are unlocked by specific innovations or traditions and can only be built once per county. They provide a sizable increase to holding taxes, but are limited by terrain. like other buildings, county buildings require the county culture to meet the innovation requirements.

## Caravanserai

Caravanserai can only be built in holdings with Desert, Desert Mountains, Drylands, Floodplains, Oasis or Steppe terrain. They take 5 years to construct or upgrade. They can also be constructed on any terrain in Iberia if the Iberian Struggle has ended in Conciliation. The Caravaneers tradition allow the construction of a second Caravanserai in the same county.

If county culture has the Forest Folk or Forest Wardens tradition and barony has a Forestries building at level 4 or higher the terrain requirements are ignored but the building can only be upgraded up to level 4 if the terrain doesn't match. Watermills take 5 years to construct or upgrade.

If county culture has the Hill Dwellers tradition and barony has a Hill Farms building at level 4 or higher the terrain requirements are ignored but the building can only be upgraded up to level 4 if the terrain doesn't match.
