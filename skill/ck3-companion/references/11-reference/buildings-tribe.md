Reference data for Tribe buildings, extracted mechanically from the wiki (no LLM compression --
see HANDOFF.md §5.5). Prose below is reproduced close to verbatim; tables are exact.

Tribe buildings can only be constructed in Tribe holdings. Adopting a different government grants the same amount of random buildings as previously built in the barony.

## Tribal buildings

Tribal buildings can only be constructed or upgraded by rulers with Tribal government. They come in 2 levels and upgrading them requires a certain innovation.

- Level 1 buildings cost 75 Gold and 200 Prestige
- Level 2 buildings cost 100 Gold and 350 Prestige

### Gathering Halls

Constructing or upgrading a Gathering Halls building takes 5 years and upgrading the building requires the Barracks innovation.

| Level 1 effects | Level 2 effects |
|---|---|
| +100 Levies +0.25 Monthly Prestige +0.2 Monthly Control -30% Feast Cost in Holding | +175 Levies +0.5 Monthly Prestige +0.4 Monthly Control -30% Feast Cost in Holding |

## Wanua buildings

Wanua buildings can only be constructed or upgraded by rulers with Wanua government. They come in 4 levels and upgrading them does not require any innovation.

- Level 1 buildings cost 75 Gold
- Level 2 buildings cost 100 Gold
- Level 3 buildings cost 100 Gold
- Level 4 buildings cost 150 Gold
