Innovations are technological, legal, and ideological advances that cultures unlock and share within their realms. Once unlocked, any county or character of that culture can use the innovation. They are divided into four categories: Military and Civic, Cultural, and Regional, plus a fifth category—Silk Road—added by the All Under Heaven DLC.

## Advancement Mechanics

**Military and Civic innovations** progress monthly based on average county development and cultural exposure (neighbors who already possess the innovation). Base advancement is 5%, plus Fascination bonus plus an additional 40% if the innovation has current Exposure status. The Culture Head sets one innovation as Fascination, providing a 20% base bonus plus 1% per Culture Head skill point matching the innovation (or 27% base and 1.35% with Scientific perk). Exposure automatically targets one adjacent/co-religion innovation and cannot be controlled.

**Regional innovations** unlock when a culture has sufficient presence in their region and remain unlocked even if territory is lost.

**Cultural innovations** are always active but require specific cultures or traditions.

**Silk Road innovations** (All Under Heaven DLC) start available only to Han culture. After Han research them, cultures with Chinese heritage, the Sinophilic tradition, or a ruler controlling a Silk Road hub in the region with the innovation can research them. Other rulers unlock them via the Visit a Silk Road Market decision and skill challenge.

Advancement formula: 0.3 + (average cultural province Development × 0.02) + 0.2 if ahead in era. Random innovations from decisions like Unite the Slavs are determined by in-game date.

## Eras

Cultures progress through four Eras which determine available innovations: Tribal (start), Early Medieval (year 900), High Medieval (year 1050), and Late Medieval (year 1200). Once 8 innovations from an era are unlocked and the minimum year is reached, cultures slowly progress to the next era based on average development. Even without reaching an era, Culture Heads can select next-era innovations as Fascination at much slower progress once 8 current-era innovations are unlocked.

Tribal, Nomadic, and Wanua governments cannot use innovations beyond Tribal Era. A Culture Head with such government prevents era progress and slows Fascination progress dramatically.
