Great Holy Wars prioritize target kingdoms based on multipliers calculated from distance to the Head of Faith and regional classification. These weights determine which kingdoms the AI Heads of Faith are most likely to declare Great Holy Wars against.

## Targeting Multipliers

The base multiplier for each region type is:

- 100 for a key religious holding (Jerusalem for Catholics and Jews, Mecca and Medina for Muslims)
- 75 for the kingdom where someone who made a Human Sacrifice of the Head of Faith came from
- 50 for kingdoms with a holy site
- 40 for kingdoms in heartland regions
- 30 for frontier regions
- 20 for fringe regions
- 10 for stretch goal regions
- 1 for all other kingdoms

When a Head of Faith is landed, these multipliers are divided by the distance from their capital. If unlanded, they're divided by distance from the faith's primary religious site (Rome for unspecified Abrahamic faiths, Mecca for unspecified Muslim faiths). Additionally, there is a 20x multiplier if a landed ruler of the same faith holds the kingdom title but lacks sufficient territory within it.

## Abrahamic

### Christian

**Unlanded Head of Faith location:** Rome

#### Catholic Regions

- Key Religious Holding: Holy Land (Duchies of Oultrejourdain, Palestine, Urdunn, Lebanon, and Damascus)
- Heartland: Northern Italy, France, Germany, England
- Frontier: Near East, Mediterranean, Balkans, Iberia
- Fringe: Celtic, Germanic, Central European
- Stretch Goal: Baltic

#### Orthodox/Iconoclast Regions

- Heartland: Thessalonika, Greece
- Frontier: Anatolia, Near East, Mediterranean, Balkans, Holy Land
- Fringe: Central European

#### Coptic Regions

- Heartland: Ethiopia

#### Apostolic Regions

- Heartland: Caucasus
- Frontier: Anatolia, Near East, Holy Land

#### Other Christian Regions

- Key Religious Holding: Holy Land
- Heartland: Northern Italy, France, Germany, Italy, England, Thessalonika, Greece, Ethiopia, Caucasus
- Frontier: Anatolia, Near East, Mediterranean, Balkans, Holy Land, Iberia
- Fringe: Celtic, Germanic, Central European
- Stretch Goal: Baltic

### Muslim Regions

- Key Religious Holdings: Mecca and Medina (whichever kingdom holds them de jure, typically Arabia) Note: This does not apply to Qarmatians.
- Heartland: Holy Land, Arabia, Near East, Egypt, Persia, North Africa
- Frontier: Sicily, Anatolia, Iberia, Afghanistan
- Fringe: The Sahel, Mediterranean, Western India, Southern France

**Unlanded Head of Faith location:** Mecca

### Yazidi Regions

- Heartland: Kurdistan
- Frontier: Southern Middle East, Eastern Middle East, Caucasus
- Fringe: Caspian Steppe, Arabia, Holy Land

### Jewish Regions

- Heartland: Holy Land
- Frontier: Arabia, Near East, Egypt
- Fringe: Anatolia, Persia, Ethiopia

**Unlanded Head of Faith location:** Jerusalem

### Dualism/Zoroastrian

- Heartland: Persia
- Frontier: Afghanistan, Near East
- Fringe: Arabia, Holy Land, Anatolia, Mongolia, Western India

**Unlanded Head of Faith location:** Baduspan
