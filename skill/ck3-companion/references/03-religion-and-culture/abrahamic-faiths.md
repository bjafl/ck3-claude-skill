## Islam

Muslim faiths have the Teachings of the Prophet special doctrine and the Muhammad's Succession main doctrine, which divides them into four sub-groups. Most of them also have the Jizya special doctrine.

If a new Muslim faith is created and both the old faith and the new one have a head of faith and the same Muhammad's Succession doctrine the new faith will keep its previous head of faith but unlock a decision to change it. The founder of the new faith will receive a choice to either gain a Weak Hook on their head of faith or lessen the requirements of the decision.

## Judaism

Jewish faiths have the Teachings of Moses special doctrine and the Halakha Authority main doctrine, which divides them into four sub-groups.

In year 1000 all historical and new faiths with the Rabbinic and Polygamous doctrines will change from Polygamous to Monogamous. Player culture heads will have the option to pay 250 Piety to give their culture the Polygamous tradition. Players who vassalized their head of faith can pay a Level of Devotion to prevent the doctrine change.
