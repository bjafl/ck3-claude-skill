Struggles are situations progressing through multiple phases, with transitions occurring 3 months after sufficient Catalysts accumulate. Characters are classified as Involved, Interloper, or Uninvolved, determining their influence and effects. Visibility is automatic but can be overridden. Struggles can be disabled in game rules. Ending a struggle grants a Heroic legend seed.

## Involvement Levels

- **Uninvolved:** Capital outside struggle region; unaffected; no Catalysts generated
- **Interloper:** Capital in region but faith/culture not involved; partially affected; generates Catalysts
- **Involved:** Capital in region with involved faith/culture; fully affected; generates Catalysts

Once a faith or culture becomes involved, it remains even if requirements no longer apply.

## Iberian Struggle

Present in all start dates. A faith or culture becomes involved if 80% of its counties are in Iberia. These are always involved:

**Faiths:** Mozarabism, Catholicism, Muwalladism, Ash'arism, Rabbinism, Karaism

**Cultures:** Andalusian, Aragonese, Asturleonese, Basque, Castilian, Catalan, Galician, Portuguese, Bedouin, Maghrebi, Mashriqi, Baranis, Sephardi

| Involvement | Holy Wars | Struggle Clash | Inter-faith Marriage | Can End |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Involved | No | Yes | Yes | No |
| Interloper | No | No | No | Yes |
| Uninvolved | Yes | No | No | Yes |

### Phases

Four cyclic phases; always starts in Opportunity.

#### Opportunity

**Involved effects:** −30% Mercenary Hire, −50% Casus Belli Prestige Cost (Iberia targets), +150 Prestige (new language/title grant to Involved), −25% Culture Conversion, −50% Faith Conversion, +25% Convert Faith Task Speed, +1 Cultural Acceptance (Dev tasks), Fabricate Claims costs Prestige, Border Raid casus belli, Commission Epic/Sell Minor Titles decisions, Bargain Fealty/Contract Assistance/Buy Claim/Demand Payment/Foment Revolt/Purchase Truce interactions, Befriend/Abduct/Claim Throne/Fabricate Hook schemes.

**Interloper effects:** −50% Casus Belli Cost, −25% Culture/−50% Faith Conversion, +25% Convert Faith Speed, −10% Holding Taxes, −5 Popular Opinion, Iberian Foothold decision.

**Catalysts to Hostility:** +25 Break Truce, +10 Expose Secret/Rival-Nemesis/Kill/Convert/Use Hook, +5 Same-faith building, +3 Gain claim/enforce demands, +1 yearly. Towards Conciliation: +25 Release without request, +10 independence/county grant/culture convert/best friends, +5 feast invite/ransom/rights grant/city building, +3 gift/language learn/truce purchase/befriend/ally, +1 yearly.

### Endings

Three ways by independent Involved or Interloper (each grants dynasty 10,000 Renown). Interlopers can also take Iberian Foothold decision.

#### Dominance

**Requirements:** Every county de jure your kingdom capital is same culture/faith, <20% Iberia by other Involved, Hostility phase + controls/kingdom+ de jure Hispania 5y/two de jure Hispania kingdoms or Unite Spanish Thrones decision or two of Balansiyyah/Cordoba/Galicia/Gothia/Tulaytulah.

**Effects:** Conquistador nickname, create Empire of Hispania, Struggle Clash casus belli persists. Choose permanent: Faith Hostility (House gains modifier, +1000 Piety, −50% Holy War cost, +50% Convert Faith speed, +40 Stress if Cynical, Holy Wars −1 Devotion), Cultural Hostility (+1000 Renown, −50% Conquer Duchy cost, +50% Promote Culture, −50% Cultural Acceptance all Involved, +40 Stress if Humble), or Domination (+3000 Prestige, −50% both costs, +50% convert/promote, −75% acceptance, +40 stress if Cynical/Humble, Holy Wars −1 Devotion).

#### Status Quo

**Requirements:** Compromise phase, peace all Involved, <50% Iberia by Involved, Fame Exalted+ or kingdom de jure Hispania, −60/Strong Hook all Involved OR <King/Emperor only OR >25% Iberia by Interloper/Uninvolved, one of Balansiyyah/Cordoba/Galicia/Gothia/Tulaytulah.

**Effects:** Pragmatic nickname, destroy Empire of Hispania, create empire per kingdom (5+ counties); small kingdoms become neighbor empire de jure; controlled duchies become primary kingdom de jure; +50% Holy War/Conquer Duchy costs, independent Involved houses gain Compromise modifier, capitals gain Regional Stronghold modifier.

#### Détente

**Requirements:** Conciliation phase, Fame Exalted+, kingdom de jure Hispania, alliance all Involved, ≤50% Iberia control.

**Effects:** Equitable nickname, create Empire of Hispania, enable inter-faith Involved marriages, allow same culture/faith to join defensive wars (different culture/faith targets), disable Holy War between Involved, independent Involved houses gain Conciliation modifier, +50% Cultural Acceptance.

## Iranian Intermezzo

Present only in 867. While active, Dissolution factions cannot form for Abbasid Empire.

Faith/culture becomes involved if 50% of counties in Persia. Always involved:

**Faiths:** Ash'arism, Maturidism, Mu'tazilism, Isma'ilism, Zayidism, Imamism, Azraqism, Alevism, Mazdayasna, Khurmazdism, Khurramism, Afridunism, Mahayana, Nestorianism

**Cultures:** Afghan, Baloch, Daylamite, Khwarezmian, Kurdish, Persian, Sogdian, Tajik, Bedouin, Mashriqi, Yemeni, Brahui

| Involvement | Fewer Dissolution | More Claimant |
|---|---|---|
| Involved/Interloper | Yes | Yes |
| Uninvolved | No | No |

Involved/Interloper rulers gain Detractor of the Caliphate or Supporter of Caliphal Authority trait (determines stance). Switch via decision every 20 years (unless Sunni Caliph dies). New Involved/Interloper rulers unlock side-choice decision. Detractor killing Sunni Caliph (different dynasty) forces switch to Supporter, cannot revert. Supporter inheriting Sunni Caliphate forces switch to Detractor. Sunni Caliphate cannot be destroyed during this situation.

### Concession

If reached, the situation ends (auto-trigger):

- All independent Involved wars end in White Peace
- 50-year truce all independent Involved
- All independent Involved lose all claims
- Supporter House Heads gain Rebuilding Efforts 100y
- Detractor House Heads gain Frontier Ambitions 100y
- If Caliph al-Mu'tass alive, loses Extremely Suspicious modifier

### Endings

If not reaching Concession, can end via:

#### Abbasid Humiliation: Found a New Caliphate

**Requirements:** Islam, Unrest phase, Detractor, King+, realm > Caliph, allied 2+ kings or 50+ counties, control 15 Involved counties same faith, Head of Faith (or no Head/different head), two of: Sayyid/Wise/Brilliance legacy/4 Devotion.

**Effects:** Righteous nickname, Persian Empire title, Caliphate title, +50 Fervor, +5000 Renown/Piety, 75% county/ruler conversion, House Light of the Walayah 100y, Convert Faith task enhanced.

#### Abbasid Humiliation: Dominate the Caliph

**Requirements:** Islam, Unrest phase, Detractor, King+, realm > Caliph, allied 2+ kings or 50+ counties, Sunni Caliph holds Arabian Empire, Fame Illustrious+, control Samarra duchy, held Samarra county 5y, hold Baghdad OR imprisoned Caliph OR Strong Hook.

**Effects:** Protector of the Caliphate nickname, Arabian Empire + vassals, +5000 Renown/Prestige, Sunni Caliph becomes vassal, House unlocks Request Honorary Title/House Unity Interference interactions.

#### Renewed Caliphate

**Requirements:** Stabilization phase, 4 Devotion, Supporter, Head of Faith is Sunni Caliph, alliance Head, Caliph holds Arabian Empire, 50% Involved independent Supporters, no Detractor duke+, no non-Sunni faith duke+.

**Effects:** +50 Fervor, +10000 Renown (Caliph +10000 Renown/+5000 Prestige), 50% conversion, Persian Empire kingdoms become Arabian Empire de jure, Convert Faith task enhanced, House Head unlocks Subjugation casus belli, Sunni Caliph House Head unlocks Subjugation, every independent Supporter becomes vassal, no Muslim dissolution/independence (Caliph), Detractor becomes crime, supporters less likely to join factions. (Roads to Power) Caliph can change to Administrative government.

#### Iranian Resurgence

**Requirements:** Fame Exalted+, King+, Iranian heritage, 60% Involved/Interloper Iranian heritage, 30% struggle region control, 2+ allied kings Iranian heritage or 40+ Iranian heritage counties.

**Effects:** Flame of Persia nickname, Persian Empire title, House Flame of Persia modifier, Involved Iranian rulers gain Rekindled Persian Spirit modifier. Independent non-Iranian liege Involved become independent (+20 Vassalage Acceptance Iranian). +5000 Prestige/+10000 Renown, 50% conversion, +100 Cultural Acceptance Iranian, Iranian cultures more effective/resistant. Inheritable army: 500 Asawira/2000 Ayyar. Involved Iranian get: 100 Asawira/400 Ayyar/400 Zupin Spearmen/200 Tawashi/100 Tarkhans. Secret faith option to reveal and convert family/vassals.
