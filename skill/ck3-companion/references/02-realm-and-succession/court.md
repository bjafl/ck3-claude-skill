A court consists of unlanded characters living under a ruler: courtiers if the liege is a ruler, adventurers if the liege is an adventurer. Courts contain family members, knights, lovers, and courtiers. Rulers may also hold hostages and receive guests. Prisoners are managed through the Court Positions tab.

## Courtiers and Followers

Unlanded characters living at court can be appointed as councilors, commanders, knights, or vassals. Without a court position or minor title, courtiers have a 2% monthly chance to leave. This chance adjusts by -0.1% per 10 opinion points above 0 and +0.1% per 10 points below 0. Additional modifiers apply:

| Condition | Chance to leave |
|---|---|
| Child | −3% |
| Any claim | −1% |
| Pregnant | −1% |
| Married to the liege | −0.5% |
| Low health | −0.3% |
| Culture has the Cultural Primacy tradition | −0.3% |
| Culture has the Isolationist tradition | −0.3% |
| Liege or another courtier is a friend | −0.2% |
| Liege or another courtier is a lover | −0.2% |
| Liege or another courtier is a close family member | −0.2% |
| Bastard | +0.3% |
| Rival | +0.5% |
| Culture has the Audacious Cadets tradition | +0.5% |
| Culture has the Caravaneers tradition | +0.5% |
| Culture has the Swords for Hire tradition | +0.5% |

Adult courtiers can be recruited from other courts. Acceptance depends on opinion difference, Lodgings court amenities, relations with both rulers, and whether the courtier has a position or is a knight. Recruiters can spend 25 Gold to increase acceptance or use a Hook to force recruitment. Dismissing non-family courtiers costs 75 Prestige and -20 Opinion for 20 years.

### Hostages

Hostages are landless children from another ruler's close family, sent as assurance of peace or submission. Both parties can offer, demand, or exchange hostages before or during wars. Wardens (those holding hostages) gain:

- +0.1 to +1 Monthly Prestige (varies by title rank difference)
- +0.1 to +1 Monthly Piety if hostage's faith is Hostile or Evil (varies by title rank)
- +0.25 to +0.5 Monthly Renown if hostage is from different dynasty (varies by Dynasty Splendor level)
- Chance to gain Perpetual Hook when hostage reaches age 16
- Option to kill (gain +10 Dread) or spare (gain +10 Opinion) if hostage's liege declares war

Declaring war on a Warden holding a family member causes -1 Fame level, -250 Prestige, -25 Opinion with all family, and 5 years of penalties: -25 Opinion, -50% Siege Phase Time, -15% Army Damage, -15% Army Toughness.

Celestial, Meritocratic, and Meritocratic Khanate government vassals cannot demand or offer hostages to each other or their liege.

### Guests

Guests are unlanded characters who stay at court for three years, then have a 5% monthly chance to leave. They interact with court members and participate in random events. Guests can be convinced to stay by appointment to council, court position, landed or minor title, or through seduction scheme. Direct recruitment costs variable Gold (base 15 Gold, increasing with claims, useful traits, high skills, or dynasty splendor). Courts with fewer courtiers receive more guests. If a guest with claims departs, the liege can convince them to stay one more year.

## Court Positions

Court positions grant specialized roles to courtiers and direct vassals, providing increasing benefits but costing monthly salary to rulers. Imprisoned characters cannot have court positions except Executioner. Removing a court position costs 200 Prestige for rulers, 25 Prestige for adventurers, and grants -30 decaying Opinion (removed if given another position). Positions can be automated to suggest the best candidate or assign automatically.

Court positions cost salary in Gold or Prestige (affected by government type, culture traditions, realm size, era, and Emperor status). Positions with assigned tasks grant additional bonuses but increase salary by +0.06 per primary title rank.

Salary modifiers:
- −75% if Tribal or Wanua government
- −20% if culture has Esteemed Hospitality tradition
- +10% to +60% based on realm size (30-120+)
- +25% to +75% based on era (Early Medieval to Late Medieval)
- +25% if Emperor
- Treasury resource replaces Gold for governments that use it

Owner of the Chrysanthemum Throne title cannot have court positions unless landed.

### Aptitude

Court position bonuses depend on aptitude, which progresses through 5 levels (20 points per level):

| Aptitude level | Minimum points |
|---|---|
| Terrible | 0 |
| Poor | 20 |
| Average | 40 |
| Good | 60 |
| Excellent | 80 |

Modifiers: +20 for family members of same culture (with Family Business tradition), -20 for Vassals, -75 for Infirm characters (-50 for Caravan Master, 0 for Court Gardener).

### Recruit for a Court Position

Rulers can recruit courtiers via Court tab button or decision. Choose between above-average or below-average Aptitude character. Cost scales with aptitude and monthly income. Recruitables: Personal Physician, Caravan Master, Court Gardener, Chief Eunuch, Wet Nurse, Bookmaker, Champion Charioteer, Foreign Emissary, Siege Engineer, Stargazer, Khlon Glan, and Court Brahmin. Characters with 15+ Learning or Scholar/Theologian/Whole of Body trait always have option to recruit Excellent Personal Physician.
