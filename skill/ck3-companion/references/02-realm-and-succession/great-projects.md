Great projects are massive realm-wide undertakings that require coordinated contributions from your realm. They begin with a starting cost, which is refunded if canceled before any mandatory contributions are paid. Once the first contribution is made, cancellation is permanent. All mandatory contributions must be paid before the project progresses, and optional contributions can be made until the project reaches 50% completion. Contributors receive rewards, and the total mandatory cost is displayed before starting.

## Contribution System

Any vassal in the de jure hierarchy holding territory where a project occurs can contribute, gaining +35 Prestige and +30 Influence. Governors gain experience; merit-based governments gain Merit. Your top liege can request contributions; refusing incurs penalties unless the vassal benefits. Acceptance improves via Influence, Hooks, or mutual benefit. A Weak Hook increases acceptance; a Strong Hook forces it. Projects cancel if the location is conquered by another ruler.

## Develop Communities

Available to Administrative, Celestial, Ritsuryō, Meritocratic, and Meritocratic Khanate rulers (Hegemon of China also requires Minister of Works). This 10-year project targets a duchy, granting Development to all counties and upgrading random buildings in each holding. Costs scale with Treasury income.

**Mandatory contributions:**
- Send Construction Crew: 100 Treasury, rewards 60 Merit or 350 Prestige
- Provide Engineers: 100 Treasury, rewards 60 Influence

**Optional contribution:**
- Governor's Capital Development: 400-800 Treasury (varies by culture era), only payable by the duchy governor; grants +1 Development to capital and Governor trait experience

## Capital Temple Complex

Available exclusively to independent Mandala government rulers. On completion, you gain 350 Prestige, 100 Legitimacy, and 150 Renown; your capital holding becomes a Temple Citadel or upgrades its Capital Temple Complex building. Starting costs 250 Piety per level. Projects can repeat per realm capital (max 2 buildings per de jure kingdom).

Subjects with upgraded Temple Complexes refuse to contribute and must be forced. Resource rewards go to contributors; modifiers go to the top liege.

The project has five levels, each requiring increasing legitimacy and devotion levels, with mandatory and optional contributions. Completion times range from 5 to 15 years. Contributions vary by level and include resources, prestige, piety, influence, and merit, with optional contributions granting powerful dynasty and realm modifiers. Canceling costs 75 Prestige.

## Hegemony of China Great Projects

These projects are exclusive to the Hegemon of China, its diarch, and certain council positions (halved costs if started by diarch/councilor; diarch/councilor gain 1000 Influence on completion). Costs scale with Treasury income.

### Dynasty's Grand Campaign
**Councilor:** Grand Marshal | **Time:** Instant

Declares war on the target duchy owner (auto-completes if owner becomes subject). Contributions pledge to conquer the selected duchy, with optional pledges for adjacent areas. Cannot target tributary-held duchies.

### Establish Control
**Councilor:** Minister of Justice | **Time:** 5 years

Grants +10-20 Control. Contributions include Administrators, Court Magistrates (with Confucian Education), and City Guards. Rewards Merit, Stewardship/Learning experience, Prestige, and Martial experience.

### Conduct Census
**Councilor:** Minister of Revenue | **Time:** 35 years

Grants +10 Control, +10% duchy holding taxes, +10% duchy development growth, +10 duchy popular opinion (all 20 years). Contributions include Census, Fertility Effort, Natural Disaster Insurance, Purge Treasury Debt, and Verify Exams. Can reveal cheating during examinations.

### Compel Religious Uniformity
**Councilor:** Minister of Rites | **Time:** 35 years

Counties with different faiths convert (+5 Control, different-faith counties +10 popular opinion 10 years). Contributions include Provide Religious Liaison, Construct New Shrines, Investigate Improper Rites, and Promote Faithful Relocation.

### Sinicize Area
**Councilor:** Minister of Rites | **Time:** 35 years

Cultures with different heritage convert; same-heritage gain acceptance; tribal holdings convert to castle; tribal vassals change to Celestial government. Contributions include Provide Cultural Liaison and Send Inhabitants (three options).

### Military Operation Exercise
**Councilor:** Minister of War | **Time:** 5 years

Grants +10 Control (with stationed men-at-arms), +20% garrison size, +10% stationed men-at-arms damage, +5% toughness (20 years). Contributions include Troop Exercises, Bootcamp for Levies, Equipment Checkup, Military Academy Courses, Natural Disaster Test, and Siege Exercise. Restores artifact durability and grants bonuses to levies and siege weapons.

### Construct Great Barracks
**Councilor:** Minister of Works | **Time:** 5 years

Duchy capital gains Great Barracks duchy building (removes existing if present). Only for de jure duchy capitals with military governors. Contributions include Construction Materials, Fortification Materials, Workforce, and Soldiers (+20 Legitimacy each).

### Improve the Great Wall
**Councilor:** Minister of Works | **Time:** 5 years

Upgrades Great Wall buildings. Contributions include Hire a Master Builder and Begin Construction, and Improve the Walls (adds baronies to upgrade list).

### Strengthen Capital
**Councilor:** Minister of Works | **Time:** 5 years

Grants +100 Legitimacy, +4 Monthly Prestige (20 years), stable dynastic cycle (20 years), improved university activities (20 years). Can only repeat every 20 years. Contributions include Construction Materials and Workforce; optional: Forbidden City building, Skilled Architects, Talented Engineers, and Marvelous Sculptors (+10 Court Grandeur 20 years).

### The Grand Canals
**Councilor:** Minister of Works | **Time:** 5 years

Counties gain Grand Canal modifier. Contributions include Construction Crew, Dig Crew, Dredging Boats, Engineers, Logistics, and Materials. Rewards merit, various lifestyle experience, prestige, and influence.

### Extend the Great Wall to Shanhai Pass
**Time:** 10 years

Extends Great Wall to Shanhai Pass region (requires control). Eight mandatory Construction Material contributions.

### Extend the Great Wall to Liaodong
**Time:** 10 years

Extends Great Wall to Liaodong region (requires control). Contributions include Construction Materials and Logistics.
