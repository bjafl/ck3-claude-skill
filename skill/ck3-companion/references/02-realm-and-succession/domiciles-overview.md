Domiciles are the seats of authority for characters whose government types do not require owning landed titles. The visual appearance of a domicile's buildings depends on your culture, while terrain matches that of the barony where it is located.

## Types of Domiciles

Each government type has a corresponding domicile:

- **Camp**: Adventurer governments
- **Administrative estate**: Administrative governments
- **Celestial estate**: Celestial, Meritocratic, and Meritocratic Khanate governments
- **Manor**: Ritsuryō and Sōryō governments
- **Nomadic capital**: Nomadic governments

These structures serve as the visual representation of your realm's power while you maintain a non-feudal government structure.
