Tribal is common in Northern/Eastern Europe, Central Asia, and most of Africa (more common at earlier start dates). Tribal realms have stunted county development (it affects only Supply Limit, doesn't passively grow), can't advance past Tribal-Era innovations, can't construct new holdings, and usually can't change Succession Law (only Confederate Partition, plus the Challenge the Ruler decision). Tribal baronies give only half supply limit to non-Tribal armies; non-Tribal armies suffer a Supply Limit penalty in hostile Tribal territory. Tribal rulers have an inferior Royal Court.

Compared to Feudal: uses Prestige instead of Gold for realm-building and men-at-arms, has access to the Tribal Authority law, unlocks Subjugate/Conquest casus belli, can start a Co-Monarchy, can always raid (unless faith forbids it) and gains +100 Prestige per battle won.

Unique effects: men-at-arms cost/maintain via Prestige (maintenance −30%); can't imprison vassals/courtiers without Tribal Authority level 2; vassals give taxes/levies scaled to the liege's Level of Fame; −50% title creation cost; +0.2 monthly Prestige; −20 opinion from non-Tribal/Wanua/Nomadic vassals.

## Raiding

Attacking another character's holding without being at war, for Loot. Requires an army raised as raiders, which needs at least one of: Unreformed faith, Tribal government, Practiced Pirates tradition, Ghazi Status tax decree, Legacy of Piracy dynasty modifier, Frontier administration (Roads to Power), Nomadic government (Khans of the Steppe), or Wanua/Meritocratic Khanate government (All Under Heaven).

Embarking as raiders requires the Longships or West African Canoes innovation, or the Practiced Pirates tradition. Raiding armies can't move; maintenance is −50%. Loot converts per the army's Raid Intent (halved by a Marches duchy building, zero if upgraded):

| Intent | Loot conversion | Raid speed | Raiders speed | Effects | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pillage | 100% Gold (or 100% Gold+Prestige, or 50% Gold+100% Herd) | 100% | 100% | −1.5 Prestige/Loot if not Tribal/Wanua/Nomadic | — |
| Adventure | 100% Gold+Prestige (variants as above) | 80% | 50% | No hostile county attrition; +300% Army Loot Capacity | Longships innovation |
| Capture | up to 50% Gold / 25% Gold+50% Herd | 70% | 75% | +15% chance to imprison characters | — |
| Terrorize | up to 100% Prestige+10% Dread | 40% | 100% | +30% chance to be offered a sack; 25% chance each of: −5 fort level & −75% garrison for 5y / −1 development / lose 1 building | Scorched Earth or No Quarter legacy |
| Plunder | up to 100% Gold+150% Herd | 40% | 50% | 2% chance to gain an innovation | Must be culture head |

A raided holding is immune to raiding by the same enemy for 5 years, and gets −50% Taxes/+50% Building Construction Time/−10% Development Growth for that period (shown by a torch icon over raider-selected armies). Realms at truce cannot be raided. A commander with 20+ raids has an escalating chance per raid to gain the Raider trait.

If the commander is the ruler, a raided holding has a 30% chance (50% with Raider trait) to offer sacking its county: extra treasure (+Gold/+Prestige, county −40 development progress), or — if raiding a feudal holding and the raider's capital is under 15 development — capture slaves (capital +40 development progress, sacked county −1 development / −80 development progress). Either choice gives the county a 20-year Recently Sacked modifier (immune to re-sack, +20 popular opinion) and +10 Stress to Compassionate/Forgiving raiders. Raided rulers get a decaying −15 opinion (+1.47/year) of the raider.

With Northern Lords, qualifying rulers have a 20% chance (40% for North Germanic culture group or 2nd Adventure legacy) to be offered trade instead of raiding: requires being at peace, commanding the raiding army, raiding a castle, and either Longships or the 2nd Adventure legacy. Accepting gives the army 10 Loot and +20 opinion but blocks raiding that target for 5 years, and gives the county +20 popular opinion for 5 years.

Raiding armies can fight barterer armies to seize their Barter Goods as loot.

## Tribal vassals

Provide levies/gold scaled to the liege's Level of Fame: 0%/0% at Disgraced, +8% gold / +15% levies per tier above that. See [Resources](../01-character-mechanics/resources.md) § Level of fame.

## Converting to Tribal

- **Clan/Feudal**: can embrace Tribalism by decision if all their holdings are already Tribal.
- **Missing DLC**: without Khans of the Steppe, Herder/Nomadic fall back to Tribal; without All Under Heaven, Wanua falls back to Tribal.
