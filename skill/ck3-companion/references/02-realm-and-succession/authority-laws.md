Each realm has an authority law, which determines how much control its ruler has over the realm. Each level grants increasing powers to the ruler but also affects the Opinion of vassals. Increasing the authority level costs 10 Prestige per Realm Size, capped at 90 Realm Size and 900 Prestige, and is reduced by 11 for every point of Learning. The cooldown for increasing the level of an authority law can be ignored if the culture has the All-Things innovation.

## Crown authority

The Crown Authority law is available to Feudal and Clan governments. The law level can be increased every 20 years after researching the required innovation and has a starting cost of 100 Prestige.

## Decree

The Decree law is available to Mandala governments and can be changed at the cost of 500 Prestige if the ruler's Mandala Aspect allows it. Every decree allows titles to be revoked and vassals to be retracted and refusing these is considered a crime. Every decree also allows the heir to be designated.

## Dominance

The Dominance law is available to Nomadic governments and can only be increased after gaining sufficient Herd. The amount required scales with the culture era and the primary title rank. It has a starting cost of 300 Prestige and can be increased every 5 years. At the start of the game nomads will have the law set to equal their title rank, capped at High.

The Unrestricted law level is only available to a Gurkhan unless The Great Steppe has been disabled in game rules.
