Travel is used by characters leaving their home for activities, vassal homage, or adventurer contracts. Characters travel with Entourages containing relevant attendees. Personal Physician and Bodyguard court holders always join; other characters can be added via travel options.

Scheme progress pauses if only one party travels (unless both are in same entourage). Rulers traveling outside their realm appoint a regent. Random events occur every 20 baronies traveled.

## Mechanics

Two key factors affect travel:

- **Speed**: Movement rate vs. armies (100% = army speed)
- **Safety**: Danger event likelihood

Travel route takes shortest path through baronies; detours manually add to avoid danger or visit points of interest. Danger events depend on terrain, holdings, county control, and opinion. Plague-infected baronies have plague danger. Cultural traditions reduce certain terrain dangers.

## Points of interest

Map displays baronies with notable features providing bonuses (stackable per criteria):

- **Kingdom capitals**: Experience for matching court lifestyle (Diplomacy if no Royal Court)
- **Empire capitals**: Triple experience for matching court lifestyle
- **Legendary/Mythical legend capitals**: Diplomacy experience
- **Holy sites**: 100 Piety; Wise Man/Woman gain trait experience
- **Baghdad/Constantinople/Rome (outside realm)**: Traveler trait experience; option to delay 14 days for bonuses
- **Alexandria/Halicarnassos**: 150 Prestige
- **Special buildings**: Experience for matching lifestyle (mines, natural features, etc.)
- **Merit government capitals**: 60 Merit (if visiting ruler's government uses it too)

## Travel events

While traveling, characters encounter events based on:

- Entourage composition (courtiers, family, knights)
- Current terrain and baronies
- Character traits and relationships
- Wealth and court holdings
- Safety level

Events can grant experience, relationships, artifacts, stress changes, and skill gains. Danger events risk injury, capture, disease, or death depending on safety level and entourage quality.

### Traveling with family

Traveling with spouses, children, or concubines creates special family events. Children gain education experience; spouses/concubines improve relationships or trigger romantic events.

### Traveling with knights

Knights participate in travel events, gaining experience or triggering challenges. Knights may gain new traits from battle sites or conduct heroic deeds.

### Court position holders in travel

Court positions (Steward, Spymaster, Clerk, etc.) continue their duties while traveling or participate in entourage events. Some positions unlock special travel interactions.

## Adventurer travel

Adventurers travel between contracts, moving camps, or exploring. They gain the Traveler trait experience while wandering and can visit points of interest for bonuses. Adventurer camps cost 10-20 Gold to establish and provide travel base amenities.

## Vassal homage

Vassals can petition or pay homage to lieges by traveling to them, improving opinions or resolving disputes. Travel time and cost scale with distance.

## Religious pilgrimage

Characters on religious pilgrimages gain Piety and Pilgrim trait experience while traveling to holy sites. Pilgrimage activities trigger special events with monks, hermits, and religious figures.

## Forced travel

Characters imprisoned and transferred between courts must travel to their new location. Characters banished travel to random locations. Fleeing characters move quickly to safe areas.

## Ending travel

Travel concludes when the destination is reached, traveling character dies, realm changes, or travel is cancelled manually. Upon completion, characters resume normal realm activities and regime changes end.
