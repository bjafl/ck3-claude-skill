Eight bookmarks recommend prominent starting characters across two dates (867 and 1066 start). Many are suited for specific achievements. For additional options, see interesting characters and historical characters listings.

## 867 start date

### The Iranian Intermezzo

Persia stands at a crossroads as the Abbasid Caliphate weakens. Ambitious warlords, mountain chieftains, Zoroastrians, Buddhists, and Turkic mercenaries contest control of the vast plateau.

**Ya'qub ibn al-Layth**
- King of Makran, age 27. Medium difficulty.
- Clan government. Ash'ari faith, Persian culture.
- A self-made copper-smith's son, disfigured but capable. Already at war with the Tahirids, his supporters whisper of changing loyalties.
- Skills: 8, 12, 9, 8, 4, 13. Traits: Ambitious, Brave, Temperate. Education: Skilled Tactician. Additional: Disfigured, Organizer, Holy Warrior, Blademaster, Peasant Leader.
- Gold: 1200, Prestige: 200. Unpressed claims: Kabul, Bhakkar, Sauvira, Ghur. Special soldiers: 300 Ayyars.

**Ismail Samani**
- Count of Bukhara/Firabr, age 17. Easy difficulty.
- Clan government. Maturidi faith, Tajik culture.
- Recently welcomed as ruler of Bukhara after his family dominance. Expects complete submission from family.
- Skills: 10, 10, 4, 5, 3, 9. Traits: Compassionate, Humble, Just. Education: Charismatic Negotiator. Additional: Quick, speaks Arabic.

**Muhammad ibn Abdallah**
- Duke of Fars, age 46. Medium difficulty.
- Clan government. Ash'ari faith, Tajik culture.
- Consummate administrator faithful to the Abbasids. Recently narrowly escaped death, health waning.
- Skills: 3, 10, 9, 5, 8. Traits: Diligent, Stubborn, Humble. Education: Astute Intellectual. Additional: Flexible Leader, Pilgrim, Poet, speaks Arabic.
- Modifier: Captain of Baghdad (+1 maximum hostile schemes). Unpressed claim: Zabulistan.

**Rostam I Bavandid**
- Count of Mazandaran/Baduspan, age 21. Advanced difficulty.
- Clan government. Ash'ari faith (secretly Mazdayasna), Daylamite culture.
- Old Sassanid and Arsacid bloodline. Grapples with Islam's dominance over ancestral traditions.
- Skills: 5, 8, 4, 5, 3. Traits: Diligent, Just, Cynical. Education: Skilled Tactician.

**Sūrī of Mandesh**
- Duke of Ghur, age 24. Advanced difficulty.
- Clan government. Mahayana faith, Tajik culture.
- Last Buddhist lord west of the Indus. Rich in silver/iron but targeted by warlords north and south. Legacy weight heavy.
- Skills: 9, 5, 10, 7, 3. Traits: Craven, Patient, Diligent. Education: Thrifty Clerk. Additional: speaks Vrachada.

### The Struggle for Iberia

Old powers breaking apart. Asturias rife with ambitious vassals. Al-Andalus strong but discontent brewing. Military border districts murmur independence.

**Guifré 'the Hairy'**
- Count of Urgell/Pallas/Cerdaña, age 27. Advanced difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Catalan culture.
- Reappointed to father's lands after father and uncle murdered by Duke of Toulouse. Cousin has also retaken birthright under new liege (cousin of father's killer). Seeks Barcelona.
- Skills: 10, 10, 0, 10, 5, 10. Traits: Vengeful, Ambitious, Just, Patient. Education: Skilled Tactician. Additional: Quick, Shrewd, Rough Terrain Expert.
- Gold: 500, Prestige: 500.

**Lubb Muzez**
- Count of Tudela/Nájera, age 36. Medium difficulty.
- Clan government. Muwalladism faith, Basque culture.
- Banu Qasi dynasty once overlooked Ebro Valley. Now as Banu Musa, plots recovery from ancient Tudela seat.
- Skills: 5, 7, 3, 7. Traits: Ambitious, Brave, Vengeful. Education: Flamboyant Trickster.
- Pressed claim: Duchy of Toledo.

**Hermenexildo Gutierrez Menéndez**
- Count of Santiago/Tui, age 20. Medium difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Galician culture.
- Powerful vassal to king Adelfonso III. Controls southern Galicia with unsullied administration. Loyalty expanded ancestral domain.
- Skills: 5, 6, 7, 4. Traits: Ambitious, Brave, Trusting. Education: Fortune Builder. Additional: Administrator.
- Pressed claim: County of Coimbra.

**Abd al-Rahman ibn Marwan**
- Duke of Badajoz, age 48. Medium difficulty.
- Clan government. Muwalladism faith (secretly Mozarabism), Andalusian culture.
- Tumultuous relationship with Umayyad sultan. Strong Mozarab support. Stronghold in Badajoz threatened by zealous Banu Danis.
- Skills: 4, 4, 7, 7. Traits: Ambitious, Cynical, Fickle. Education: Brilliant Strategist. Additional: Quick, Blademaster, Aggressive Attacker.
- Pressed claims: Coimbra, Beja.

**Emir Adanis (Adanis ibn Awsaga)**
- Duke of Beja, age 44. Easy difficulty.
- Clan government. Ashari faith, Baranis culture.
- Tasked by Umayyad sultan protecting coast from Vikings. Berber Banu Danis dynasty risen in influence. Concerned about Mozarab subjects preferring nemesis Ibn Marwan.
- Skills: 7, 10, 3, 5, 4, 11. Traits: Diligent, Stubborn, Zealous. Education: Fortune Builder. Additional: Scarred, Unyielding Defender, Architect, speaks Arabic.

### Wrath of The Northmen

King Ælla threw legendary Viking Ragnarr Loðbrók into a pit of snakes. His sons invaded England seeking vengeance. Anglo-Saxon kings face their darkest hours.

**Halfdan "Whiteshirt" Ragnarsson**
- Duke of Jorvik, age 39. Easy difficulty.
- Feudal government. Asatru faith, Norse culture.
- First invasion wave successful. Wrestled York from Ælla's hands with brother Ivar. Will take so much more.
- Skills: 4, 7, 6, 5, 3. Traits: Ambitious, Wrathful, Callous. Education: Skilled Tactician. Additional: Viking, Hunter.
- Gold: 500. Special soldiers: 4x 1250 Levies.

**Ivar "the Boneless" Ragnarsson**
- Duke of the Isles, age 22. Easy difficulty.
- Tribal government. Asatru faith, Norse culture.
- Rumored weak in body but unmatched in mind. United Norse warbands, won great victories. In 870 will fade from history—can you keep him alive?
- Skills: 6, 6, 8, 12, 3, 6. Traits: Ambitious, Callous, Vengeful. Education: Brilliant Strategist. Additional: Viking, Blademaster, One-legged.
- Gold: 500. Special soldiers: 4x 1250 Levies.

**Sigurdr "Snake-in-the-eye" Ragnarsson**
- Duke of Sjaelland, age 27. Medium difficulty.
- Tribal government. Asatru faith, Norse culture.
- Stayed behind to rule late father's lands. His descendants will be Kings of Denmark, Norway, England 100+ years. Can you fulfill destiny?
- Skills: 8, 6, 8, 5, 3. Traits: Patient, Gregarious, Zealous. Education: Fortune Builder. Additional: Viking, Hunter.

**Björn Ironside**
- Duke of Uppland, age 41. Easy difficulty.
- Tribal government. Asatru faith, Norse culture.
- Fearless raider pillaging France, Iberia, Italy with friend Hæsteinn. Now Scandinavian forces assault Britain. Promised vengeance for father; descendants rule Sweden 200 years.
- Skills: 8, 7, 5, 3, 3. Traits: Brave, Impatient, Honest. Education: Charismatic Negotiator. Additional: Viking, Hunter.
- Gold: 400.

**Alfred (Earl Alfred)**
- Count of Dorset, age 18. Advanced difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Anglo-Saxon culture.
- Vikings push further south; Alfred readies troops. Will fight numerous battles, end invasion, inherit throne to become one of England's greatest kings.
- Skills: 13, 11, 13, 8, 13, 11. Traits: Honest, Just, Gregarious, Diligent. Education: Brilliant Strategist. Additional: Intelligent.
- Gold: 400.

### Humble Beginnings

Every dynasty starts humbly. Generations climb slowly via careful marriages and wars. But these times herald great change—heroes with courage and luck can raise families from obscurity to legend.

**Jarl Hæsteinn**
- Count of Montaigu, age 52. Easy difficulty.
- Feudal government. Asatru faith, Norse culture.
- Rich through cunning and courage. Led lucrative Mediterranean raids with Björn. Settled in Brittany. Franks offer rich pickings; Viking warrior aids political turmoil. But fortune brings enemies.
- Skills: 9, 9, 5, 7, 4, 6, 6.5. Traits: Lustful, Ambitious, Greedy. Education: Brilliant Strategist. Additional: Quick, Viking, Adventurer, Whole of Body.
- Gold: 500, Prestige: 2000, Gold: 5000.
- Special soldiers (with Northern Lords): 2x (1200 Levies, 200 Huscarls, 100 Varangian Veterans). (without Northern Lords): 2x (1200 Levies, 300 Huscarls).

**Rurik "the Troublemaker" Rurikid**
- Duke of Novgorod, age 44. Easy difficulty.
- Tribal government. Asatru faith, Norse culture.
- Rose from nothingness with brothers bringing order to eastern wilderness. Sole ruler of great chiefdom. Only beginning—descendants found Tsardom of Russia.
- Skills: 12, 8, 8, 6, 3. Traits: Gregarious, Ambitious, Just, Brave. Education: Brilliant Strategist. Additional: Quick.
- Gold: 500.
- Special soldiers (with Northern Lords): 600 Levies, 200 Huscarls, 200 Varangian Veterans. (without Northern Lords): 600 Levies, 400 Huscarls.

**Daurama Daura**
- Countess of Daura, age 25. Medium difficulty.
- Tribal government. Bori faith, Hausa culture.
- Last Magajiya of Daura, symbol of old Hausa ways. New husband Bayajiddah from Iraq represents change—outsiders bringing strange faiths/cultures. History says she's the last—must she be?
- Skills: 10, 10, 10, 10, 10, 8. Traits: Just, Honest, Trusting. Education: Charismatic Negotiator. Additional: Mystic, Diplomat.
- Gold: 500.

**Eudes Robertine**
- Count of Anjou, age 10. Advanced difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, French culture.
- Father Robert the Strong slain by Viking Hæsteinn (July 866). Mother died months later birthing brother Robert. Orphan boys destined for French throne; descendants (Capets) rule France centuries.
- Traits: Calm.

**Álmos Árpád**
- King of Mogyer Confederation, age 47. Medium difficulty.
- Nomadic government. Taltosism faith, Mogyer culture.
- Seven Hungarian tribes elected supreme leader. Son Árpád will inherit—if Hungarians keep independence. Eastern dangers loom.
- Skills: 7, 8. Gold: 1000. Traits: Ambitious, Gregarious, Just, Calm. Education: Brilliant Strategist.

### The Carolingians

Louis the Pious's death divided the great Carolingian Empire into East, West, and Middle Francia. Realms splintered further via wars, rebellions, inheritance chaos. Can any Karling reunite the empire?

**Charles "the Bald"**
- King of West Francia/Aquitaine, age 44. Medium difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, French culture.
- Half-brothers ruled their own kingdoms at his birth. Known as "the Bald," eventually inherited Aquitaine and West Francia, even Italy and Holy Roman Empire. Line didn't persevere—last grandson died 7 years after his death.
- Skills: 3, 6, 8, 7, 8. Traits: Deceitful, Impatient, Stubborn. Education: Astute Intellectual.

**King Lothaire II**
- King of Lotharingia, age 31. Medium difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, French culture.
- Forced marriage to Teutberga despite love for mistress Waldrada. No children produced. Continuous struggle to divorce wife and legitimize bastards. Never succeeded; died without legal heir.
- Skills: 5, 6, 5, 6, 7. Traits: Cynical, Temperate, Calm. Education: Intricate Webweaver.

**Ludwig "the German"**
- King of East Francia, age 61. Medium difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Franconian culture.
- Ambitious man seeking to reconquer empire pieces he considers birthright. Setbacks plagued him—military campaigns failed, rebellious sons pried away lands.
- Skills: 5, 6, 5, 7. Traits: Ambitious, Stubborn, Trusting. Education: Misguided Warrior.

**Louis II "the Pious"**
- King of Italy, age 32. Medium difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, French culture.
- Crowned Holy Roman Emperor when imperial power diminished—title offered no real authority. Managed to reclaim Provence but uncles/brother act as they please.
- Skills: 2, 7, 5, 4. Traits: Arrogant, Greedy, Fickle. Education: Fortune Builder.
- Gold: 750.

**Karlmann**
- Duke of Bavaria, age 37. Medium difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Franconian culture.
- Eldest son of Ludwig, rebelled forcing father to grant Bavaria. Expanded domain fighting Slavs east, elected King of Italy, later stroke forced abdication of Bavaria and Italy to younger brothers.
- Skills: 5, 8, 5, 3, 8. Traits: Greedy, Zealous, Humble. Education: Brilliant Strategist.

### Living Gods on Earth

Regal and spiritual power intertwine in Southeast Asia. Dharmic faiths strengthen; Kings once called "Devaraja" (God Kings) assume divine authority. Which builds lasting devotion?

**Pyinbya**
- Raja of Pagan, age 50. Medium difficulty.
- Mandala government. Ari faith, Burmese culture.
- Competing Irrawaddy valley city, now regional power with vast tributaries. Long royal line; Pyinbya turns city into aspiring realm.
- Skills: 6, 4, 3, 2, 2. Traits: Patient, Calm, Ambitious. Education: Charismatic Negotiator.

**Padumaraja**
- Sdach of Haripuñjaya, age 37. Medium difficulty.
- Mandala government. Mahayana faith, Mon culture.
- Not yet promising as Pagan/Angkor metropolises. Small Mon city-state often subservient despite privilege position. Padumaraja last of Haripunjaya dynasty with grand ambitions—will he finally elevate?
- Skills: 2, 4, 3, 5, 3. Traits: Patient, Deceitful, Zealous. Education: Flamboyant Trickster.

**Lokapala**
- Raja of Mataram, age 40. Medium difficulty.
- Mandala government. Vaishnavite faith, Javanese culture.
- Controls entire eastern Java with great direct control. Western island still tributes rival Srivijayan emperors. Destiny extend Sanjaya rule over other islands or become Srivijayan tributary?
- Skills: 3, 4, 5, 3, 2. Traits: Brave, Ambitious, Generous. Education: Skilled Tactician.

**Jayavarman III**
- Sdach of Angkor, age 47. Easy difficulty.
- Mandala government. Vaishnavite faith, Khmer culture.
- New rising power since Jayavarman II first assumed Devaraja title. After his death, realm barely recognizable as great empire—reduced to city-state. Trouble surpassing predecessor; will Varmans live up to divine legacy?
- Skills: 1, 3, 4, 2, 1. Traits: Arbitrary, Impatient, Zealous. Education: Thrifty Clerk.

**Indra Warmandewa**
- Raja of Kutai, age 41. Advanced difficulty.
- Wanua government. Shaivite faith, Dayek culture.
- Small realm first inviting Hindu priests. Segara dynasty haven't leveraged alliance for Mandala authority—rule built on tribal loyalties and religious ceremony. Continue Mandala path or embrace tribal ways?
- Skills: 4, 2, 5, 3, 4. Traits: Zealous, Impatient, Generous. Education: Fortune Builder.

## 1066 start date

### The Fate of England

King Edward the Confessor's childless death triggers succession crisis. Harold Godwinson, William the Bastard, and Harald Hardrade compete. Svend and Malcolm wait for weakness.

**Harold Godwinson**
- King of England, age 44. Advanced difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Anglo-Saxon culture.
- Powerful house leader, king's brother-in-law. Elected new king by peers. Faces Duke of Normandy and King Harald of Norway (aided by exiled brother Tostig) seeking throne.
- Skills: 6, 4, 6, 5. Traits: Arbitrary, Arrogant, Ambitious. Education: Skilled Tactician. Additional: Organizer, Robust.

**William "the Bastard"**
- Duke of Normandy, age 39. Medium difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Norman culture.
- House Normandy traces Norse viking lineage; established 200 years mainland. William dreams ruling England, believes succession promised by Edward and recognized by Harold. Now Harold broke oath.
- Skills: 7, 7, 6, 10, 10. Traits: Ambitious, Diligent, Brave. Education: Brilliant Strategist. Additional: Legitimized Bastard.
- Pressed claim: Kingdom of England.
- Special soldiers: 1000 Levies, (800 Levies, 400 Armored Footmen, 20 Onagers), (800 Levies, 400 Bowmen, 20 Onagers), (800 Levies, 400 Armored Footmen, 20 Onagers), (800 Levies, 400 Light Footmen, 400 Pikemen).

**Harald Hardrada**
- King of Norway, age 51. Medium difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Norwegian culture.
- Formidable military talent from youth. Failed Denmark conquest. Now eyes England—ancient contract claims English crown should pass him. Harold Godwinson wears it instead!
- Skills: 7, 7, 5, 7. Traits: Zealous, Greedy, Brave. Education: Skilled Tactician. Additional: Varangian, Berserker, Traveler.
- Gold: 250. Pressed claim: Kingdom of England.
- Special soldiers: (600 Levies, 200 Armored Footmen, 10 Onagers), (600 Levies, 400 Light Footmen), (600 Levies, 10 Onagers).

**Svend Estridsson**
- King of Denmark, age 47. Easy difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Danish culture.
- Blood ties to England/Norway yield strong claims both thrones. Small undisciplined armies make conquest unfeasible. Leverage marriages—children wed powerful families grant alliances toward empire rivaling Rome.
- Skills: 6, 2, 7, 6. Traits: Lustful, Wrathful, Zealous. Education: Midas Touched.
- Pressed claim: Kingdom of England.

**Malcolm "the Big Headed"**
- King of Scotland, age 27. Easy difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Scots culture.
- Known to Scotland as "Great Chief"; situation tenuous but opportune. South: rich Northumbria vulnerable as England tears. Home: Norse warlords and Gaelic highlanders contest rule.
- Skills: 10, 8, 8, 7. Traits: Wrathful, Cynical, Arrogant. Education: Charismatic Negotiator. Additional: Quick.

### Rags to Riches

Throughout Europe, families fight and scheme. Disputes, battles, dynastic turnover constant. Kings replaced; newcomers reach for stars. Which will you be?

**Murchad (Petty King Murchad)**
- Duke of Munster, age 39. Easy difficulty.
- Feudal government. Insular faith, Irish culture.
- High Kingship fallen in/out of house Briain grasp amid Irish noble house wars over territory vs each other and Vikings. Murchad hails destined-for-kingship line; little known about brief Munster reign. Restore house Briain to Irish throne?
- Skills: 10, 12, 6, 3, 3, 12. Traits: Temperate, Gregarious, Impatient. Education: Charismatic Negotiator. Additional: Rough Terrain Expert.
- Recommended for new players; used in in-game tutorial. Strongest Ireland ruler in 1066; easily form Kingdom of Ireland for Emerald Isle achievement.

**Matilda di Canossa**
- Duchess of Tuscany, age 20. Medium difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Italian culture.
- Destined inherit substantial Northern Italy territory. Become powerful well-connected ruler, eventually rise to unique rank Imperial Vicar/Vice-Queen Italy. Long after death, legendary for piety and suspected witchcraft.
- Skills: 9, 6, 10, 9, 12, 6. Traits: Diligent, Calm, Zealous. Education: Astute Intellectual.
- Imperial Vicar modifier: +25 head of faith opinion, -10% temple holding/construction cost/time.

**Yahya Dhunnunid**
- Duke of Toledo, age 43. Medium difficulty.
- Clan government. Muwalladi faith, Baranis culture.
- Kingdom Toledo established late 11th century when León King takes city—eradicates Emir Yahya. Dhunnunids lose regional rule they've held generations. Manipulate rivals, find allies; challenge Iberian kings?
- Skills: 8, 10, 7, 12. Traits: Just, Diligent, Vengeful. Education: Intricate Webweaver.

**Vratislav**
- Duke of Bohemia, age 34. Easy difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Czech culture.
- Přemyslid family ruled Bohemia 100+ years. Relied heavily seniority succession; Vratislav case leads strife with brothers. Tactical resource management—material and not—needed become King Bohemia?
- Skills: 5, 12, 5, 4, 5, 12. Traits: Just, Diligent, Wrathful. Education: Golden Sovereign.

**Robert "the Fox"**
- Duke of Apulia, age 51. Medium difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Norman culture.
- Started thief, highwayman band leader. Among many siblings, adventurous son never predicted brilliant strategist/statesman ruling vast lands. Powerful Duke southern Italy. Consolidate realm power—Kingdom Sicily awaits!
- Skills: 4, 9, 6, 6. Traits: Arbitrary, Greedy, Ambitious. Education: Exalted Warlord. Additional: Robust.

### Iberia in Pieces

King Ferdinand I divided kingdom among three sons. Greed, ambition, holy wars push peninsula to boil. Family bonds fray. Will any Fernandez sibling emerge victorious?

- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Castilian culture.
- Most troubled kingdom. Tried extreme remedies stabilize rule. Fragile land too tempting—brother Alfonso invaded 1071. Kicked off many wars. García spent most time imprisoned. Take arms against brothers—lead kingdom to glory?

**Alfonso VI**
- King of León, age 26. Easy difficulty.
- Feudal government. Catholic faith, Castilian culture.
- Middle son, father's favorite. Inherited rich middle kingdom. Outsmart and subjugate both brothers, rule reunited kingdom. Via political/family diplomacy and aggression, unite three kingdoms under one banner?

**Sancho II "the Strong"**

**Urraca (Infanta Urraca)**

**Antso IV**
