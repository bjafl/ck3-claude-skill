Knights are characters assigned to fight in armies. Rulers can only have limited numbers of knights (appointment defaults to Prowess skill but can be manually forced or forbidden). Diarchs cannot be appointed knights. If armies are defeated in battle, each knight has 10% capture chance and 5% death chance (55.3% capture and 27.6% death if defeated during Early Battle Phase).

## Cultural Alternatives

Knight is the default name for most governments; Champion for tribal. Culture-specific alternatives:

- Bushi (Japonic)
- Hetaireiarches (Greek)
- Baghatur (Mongolic, Turkic)
- Shoorveer (Sauraseni, Marathi, Magadhan, Pahari, Vrachada, Rajasthani, Sinhalese, Kashmiri, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada)
- Aswārān (Iranian)
- Fāris (Arabic)

## Appointment

Knights must be:
- Adult
- Not imprisoned
- Not diarch
- Not Blind or Incapable
- Clergy/Devoted characters only if faith has Recruitment Clerical Function, Warmonger, or Unrelenting Faith tenet
- If Royal Court installed: ruler's culture's Martial Custom must allow
- If Royal Court not installed: faith's View on Gender must allow

## Combat Performance

Knights provide significant realm fighting force. Strength: extremely high damage per combat width (can out-damage/stackwipe larger armies). Weakness: low total toughness (need men-at-arms/levies to absorb damage), potential wounds/maiming/death/capture.

Knights can be wounded, maimed (one-eyed, one-legged, disfigured, incapable), killed, or captured. Chance decreases with higher Prowess, if army outnumbers enemy, or if difficulty lowered. Berserker knights may instantly kill other knights if lower than 80% of Berserker knight's Prowess.

Knight combat stats scale on Prowess: each Prowess point grants 50 Damage and 10 Toughness, modified by realm Knight Effectiveness.

Example: One levy = 10 Damage, 10 Toughness. One knight with 10 Prowess (base effectiveness) = 50 levies' damage. Early tribal realm with 7 knights (15 avg Prowess, 120% effectiveness) = ~630 levies' damage.

## Accolades

Accolades are special named honors granted to unlanded knights (8+ Prowess, 15+ liege opinion) at 100-200 Prestige cost (by title rank). Less likely to die/be captured than regular knights. When accolades die, replaced by courtier meeting requirements or newly-created character.

Each accolade has Squire Aptitude (determines successor's Martial, Prowess, traits), increased by:
- Accolade's gained Glory
- Liege's Martial
- Accolade's Prowess
- Accolade's Martial
- Aspiring Blademaster trait
- Hastiluder trait
- Accolade has child

Rulers can have up to 5 accolades (depending on King/Emperor rank, Bannus/Knighthood innovations, Glory legacy 2, Feudal/Clan/Tribal/Nomadic/Mandala/Ritsuryō/Sōryō/Wanua government).

### Rank

Accolades have rank 1-6; each rank increase grants attribute point for new attribute or leveling existing (max 3 attributes). Ranks increase via sufficient Glory:

- Army victorious in battle
- Liege wins war vs. higher-title ruler
- Win combat vs. other knights
- Attend activities (especially Grand tournaments)

| Rank | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|------|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Glory | 100 | 300 | 600 | 1000 | 1500 | 2100 |

Accolades lose 200 Glory on succession if previous didn't die in battle/duel. Existing attributes don't downgrade/remove even if rank reduced. No Glory maximum.

### Accolade Attributes

Available attributes depend on knight's traits (rare event unlocks). Only one of each per accolade. _Not covered on the wiki_ (detailed attribute tables too extensive for full transcription; see in-game for full stats and requirements). 

Key attributes include: Besieger, Blademaster, Charmer, Contender, Disciplinarian, Fanatic, Huntsmaster, Idealist, Manipulator, Marauder, Master of Revels, Mentor, Politicker, Reeve, Scoundrel, Stalwart, Tactician, Thug, Valiant, House Paragon.

#### Retinue Attributes

Retinue attributes unlock unique men-at-arms at rank 2; accolades can have only one. Examples: Archer, Outrider, Pike Captain, Skirmisher, Vanguard, Lancer, Camelier, Crossbow Captain, Elephant Rider, Horse Archer, Gunner. _Not covered on the wiki_ (detailed stats game-dependent; see in-game for specifics).
