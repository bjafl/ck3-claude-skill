Adventurer decisions are exclusive to landless adventurers operating from camps. These decisions shape an adventurer's story, from establishing territorial holdings to legendary achievements and faith-based missions. Most adventurers cannot use them if they have a disease trait.

## Major Decisions

Major decisions permanently affect the world and notify all rulers who may be impacted.

| Decision | Effect | Cost | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| Found a Holding | Start constructing a Castle in the camp's barony. Every 4-6 months gain a construction choice. With 18+ Stewardship, choose Watermills level 1 or 3. With Fame or Devotion level 3, upgrade Castle to level 2-3. With 40+ Opinion with county holder, choose Manor Houses level 1 or 3. Gain 2000 Prestige or start a war to claim the county upon completion. | 400 | Barony lacks holding, City Planning innovation, 10+ liege opinion, 3+ of: 1000 Devoted Servant, Illustrious, 18 Stewardship. [No] Currently building. |
| Become a Great Conqueror | Triggers Conqueror trait event when landed. Gain non-inheritable army at camp (200 Vanguard, 100 Lancers, 400 Archers). Pressed Claim on top-tier domain title. Start Landless Adventurer Invasion or Claim CB war. (requires Legends of the Dead: Gain The Greatest Conqueror legend seed) | 5000 | Conqueror frequency not None. 8+: 22 Martial, Living Legend fame, 20+ wars, 5+ wars vs. Kings/Emperors, 4000 Men-at-Arms, 12+ killed, 20+ Stand With Me/Raid contracts, Strategist+Gallant, or 28 Martial/6 Knights with 18 Prowess/12 Knights with 12 Prowess. [No] Taken before. |
| Champion of Culture | Pressed Claim on camp's duchy. War vs. duchy holder grants +10% Men-at-Arms Damage, -50% Siege Time (15 years), 4000 levies, 600 Handpicked Faithful. Instantly claim duchy with 3+: Noble spouse, Same faith, Friend/lover with duke, Higher fame, Hook on duke. | 500, 2000 | Same culture as camp county, different from duchy holder, holder is vassal, Illustrious fame. [No] Populist Leader, taken before. |
| The Champion of the Lion | Gain Champion-Errant trait, Golden Coat of the Lion armor, "Knight of the Lion" legend seed, Swansong house modifier, Courteous Knight or Valiant Knight choice. Remove Bandits' Benefactor if present. | 2000 | Camp in Africa, Exalted Among Men fame, Swords-for-Hire purpose, 10 Martial/Prowess contracts (non-criminal). [No] Gallowsbait, taken before. |
| The Champion of the Tiger | Gain Bushi-Errant trait, Golden Coat of the Tiger armor, "Knight of the Tiger" legend seed, Swansong house modifier, Courteous Knight or Valiant Knight choice. Remove Bandits' Benefactor if present. | 2000 | Camp in Asia, Exalted Among Men fame, Swords-for-Hire purpose, 10 Martial/Prowess contracts (non-criminal). [No] Gallowsbait, taken before. |
| The Knight of the Swan | Gain Knight-Errant trait, Golden Coat of the Swan armor, "Knight of the Swan" legend seed, Swansong house modifier, Courteous Knight or Valiant Knight choice. Remove Bandits' Benefactor if present. | 2000 | Camp in Europe, Exalted Among Men fame, Swords-for-Hire purpose, 10 Martial/Prowess contracts (non-criminal). [No] Gallowsbait, taken before. |
| The Travels of <Player> | Choose lifestyle perks (1 per 8 visited POI) or travels journal artifact. | 500, 1500 | 26+ visited POI, 2+: 42+ POI/Exalted fame/60 Wanderer trait experience. [No] Taken before. |
| The Faith Path - Ascension | Gain Zealous trait (+40 Stress if not zealous already). Unlock The Faith Path - Prelude decision. | 2500 | 2+: Pilgrimages to 2 holy sites, Theologian, Paragon of Virtue devotion. [Yes] Camp county same faith. [No] Cynical, taken before. |
| The Faith Path - Prelude | Gain Missionary nickname. Chance to convert camp county's faith. Failure gives 5-30 Stress, -75 Opinion with realm ruler. Unlocks The Faith Path - Ascension decision. | None | Unlocked via The Faith Path - Ascension. [No] Camp county same faith/holy site/ruler is faith head. |
| Levy the Outcasts | Gain Bandits' Benefactor modifier, 100 Looters, 50% chance 100 Bandits, 50% chance 100 Poachers (doubled/tripled with Gallowsbait tracks at 60%). -3 Fame. Remove Knight-Errant trait if present. | 500, 2000 | 3+: Ambitious, Schemer, 40%+ Gallowsbait tracks in Bandit/Marauder/Poacher/Thief/Trickster. [No] Taken before. |
| Legacy of House | House gains Adventurer Bloodline modifier. Cannot accept criminal contracts. | 1000 | Exalted Among Men fame, fully upgraded Pavilion, 15+ followers, 5+ Roll of Patrons rulers, 50+ non-criminal contracts. [No] Gallowsbait. |
| Designated Terrain | Choose a designated terrain type (one-time). | None | Finding Your Way perk required. |
| Master Terrain | Choose a master terrain type (one-time). | None | King of the Wilds perk required. |
| Desperate Measures - Butcher Animals | Consume pets/livestock for provisions: Cat (50), Dog (75), Warhorse (150), Kennels (750), Ample Steeds (1250), Elephantry Reserve (2500). Gain Stress unless Callous. | 150 | 0-500 Provisions, at peace. Cooldown: 25 years. |
| Adopt the Nomadic Ways | Government becomes Nomadic. Gain camp county. 25% Provisions convert to Herd. | 750 | (requires Khans of the Steppe) Camp's barony owner has Herder government. |

## Minor Decisions

Minor decisions have cooldowns and localized effects without world-shaking impact.

| Decision | Effect | Cost | Requirements | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Visit Holding | Visits the holding. | None | — | — |
| Gather Provisions | Attempts to gather provisions. | None | [No] Disease trait | 3 years |
| Adopt a Kennel Dog | Gain a pet dog. | None | Kennels upgrade | 5 years |
| Humiliate the Stooge | Gain Humiliated the Stooge modifier (2 years): +25% Stress Loss, -25% Stress Gain. 1st use: +10 Dread, -65 Stress, -25 Stooge Opinion. 2nd: +20 Dread, -30 Stress, -25 Opinion. 3rd: +30 Dread, -15 Stress, -25 Opinion. 4th: Stooge leaves, Drove Away modifier (2 years): +25% Stress Loss, -5 General Opinion. | 20 | Stooge officer | 2 years |
| Invite Poets | 3 characters with 14+ Diplomacy arrive in a few months. | 15, 75 | Laureate officer | 10 years |
| Go Fishing | Lose Stress. Chance to gain provisions, friend, or Calm trait. Can pay gold to improve rewards. | None | Coastal or river barony | 2 years |

## Hasan Story Decisions

These decisions are exclusive to Hasan Sabbah and relate to the Ismaili Assassin Brotherhood. They cannot be used with disease traits and are removed if Hasan converts faiths.

| Decision | Effect | Cost | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evangelize the Faith | Chance camp county converts to character's faith. Chance gain Strong Religious Community modifier (10 years): -25% Faith Conversion Speed. Cannot use Evangelize or Agitate in same country. After 5 successes, gain event to travel to Cairo. | 250 | County 0-25 Development, negative opinion, [No] holy site/same faith/holder is faith head. Cooldown: 1 month. |
| Agitate the Local Populace | Chance camp county gains Agitated Populace modifier (15 years): -25 Popular Opinion. Chance gain Strong Religious Community modifier (10 years): -25% Faith Conversion Speed. | 250 | [Yes] 15+ Evangelize uses or Caliph al-Mustansir dead. [Yes] Camp county different faith. Cooldown: 1 month. |
| Begin the Revolution | Gain 900 Asawira, 1500 Ayyar. Start war vs. realm ruler. | 250 | [Yes] 15+ Evangelize uses or Caliph dead. [Yes] 5 counties same faith. [Yes] At peace. Cooldown: 1 month. |
| Found the Assassins | Create Hashshashin holy order in camp barony. +2 Intrigue, +10 Faith Fervor, Founded Holy Order modifier (5 years): +0.5 Monthly Piety, +10 Same Faith Opinion, Old Man of the Mountain nickname. If no holding, create one. If no special building, add Hashshashin Fortress. If Alamut Castle exists, upgrade to Alamut Fortress. | 1500 | [Yes] 15+ Evangelize uses or Caliph dead. [Yes] Barony same faith, Mountains/Desert terrain. [No] Country capital, leased. |
| Expand the Assassins | Barony leases to Hashshashin holy order. | 250 | Founded the Assassins decision taken. [Yes] Barony same faith, Mountains/Desert terrain. [No] Country capital, leased. Cooldown: 1 month. |
