Ruler decisions are exclusive to landed rulers controlling titles. These decisions shape realm governance, declare major doctrinal stances, establish holy orders, and manage state affairs. Most require the character to be adult and at peace unless specified.

## Found a New Title

Only independent adult rulers at peace can create new titles. New titles initially named after primary title.

| Decision | De Jure Integration | Legend Seed | Cost | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Found a New Duchy | Only primary county | [No] | 150, 250, 200 | Illustrious OR custom duchies enabled. |
| Found a New Kingdom | All titles | [No] | 300, 500, 200 | 30 realm size OR 3 duchy titles. Illustrious OR custom kingdoms enabled. |
| Found a New Empire | All titles | [Yes] | 1200, 2000, 600 | 120 realm size OR (3 kingdoms + 80 realm size). Exalted Among Men. [No] Iberian Struggle involved/interloper. Custom kingdoms enabled. |

## Escape from Prison

If imprisoned, all decisions except Escape from Prison disable. Success depends on Prowess vs. jailor Intrigue. Penalty: −30 jailor opinion (10 years). Failure: moderate health (5 years) or Wound. Friend/Best Friend/close family 10+ in same jailor's prison may escape regardless of character's outcome.

Success chance decreases with: Craven, Weak, Delicate, Frail, Feeble, or dungeon imprisonment.
